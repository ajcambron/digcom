# Join a GMetrix Classroom (Student)
{: .no_toc }

Students create a GMetrix account once, then redeem your classroom code to unlock the practice content. Plan 15 minutes of class time the first day; most of it goes to the three students who forgot which email they used.

## Before class

- Have the classroom code from [Guide 1]({% link teacher-resources/cert-prep/01-gmetrix-create-classroom.md %}) on the board.
- Decide on **one** sign-in method for the whole class and say it out loud. A GMetrix account stays tied to the method that created it, and GMetrix support tells students who started with SSO to keep using the same provider. A student who switches methods later can land in a second, empty account and assume their work vanished.
- Launch one practice exam yourself from a student account on a lab machine. Some Adobe practice content runs in the browser; some needs the GMetrix desktop app and the Adobe application installed. Find out which before 28 students do.

## Step 1: Create an account or sign in

1. Go to **gmetrix.net** in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
2. Choose one:
   - **Single sign-on (recommended):** select the **Google**, **Microsoft**, or **Clever** icon at the bottom of the login window and follow the prompts. Use the one that matches your school login.
   - **Email:** select **Sign up** below the **Sign In** button, complete the **User Registration** form, accept the terms, and select **Confirm**.

## Step 3: Launch a practice exam

{: start="6"}
6. Go to **Home** and open the program that just unlocked.
7. Select a practice exam.
8. Pick a mode:

| Mode | What it does | When to assign it |
|:-----|:-------------|:------------------|
| **Training Mode** | No time limit. Feedback and resources after each question. | Every attempt until a student can explain *why* each answer is right. |
| **Testing Mode** | Timed like the real exam. No feedback until the end. | Final two attempts before exam day. Target 80% or higher twice in a row. |

## Hand this to students

A short version to paste into Schoology or print:

> **Join our GMetrix class**
> 1. Go to **gmetrix.net**.
> 2. Click the **Google** button (always the same button, every time).
> 3. Click **Redeem Code** on the left.
> 4. Type our class code: `__________`
> 5. Click **Redeem Code**.
> 6. Click **Home**, open the practice test, and choose **Training Mode**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|:--------|:-------------|:----|
| "My practice test is gone" | Signed in with a different method than before | Sign out, sign back in with the original method. Check **Profile** for the email. |
| "Invalid code" | Typo, or the classroom hit its Available Uses cap | Re-type; if the cap was hit, raise it on the classroom's Details tab. |
| Exam will not launch | Content needs the GMetrix desktop app or the Adobe app | Install GMetrix SMS from support.gmetrix.net/download and confirm the Adobe app is installed on the machine. |
| Forgot password | Email sign-up users only | **Forgot your Password?** on the login window. SSO users reset through the school account. |
