## This Week's Reading

- [StudioBinder: Film Lookbook Examples & How to Make a Lookbook](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/film-lookbook-examples/)
- [StudioBinder: Film Lighting, The Ultimate Guide](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/film-lighting/)

**Look for:** How a lookbook shows mood, color and composition, and the basic lighting setups (three-point, high-key, low-key).

## Producing Task 20: Build a lookbook / mood board, and annotate your shot list with lighting and camera notes

1. Collect reference images for mood, color, lighting and composition, with a short caption on each.
2. Arrange them into a lookbook, organized by scene or by category (lighting, color, framing).
3. Add a **lighting and camera note** to each shot on your shot list.

## Pre-Production Logistics

Draft a **shoot-day calendar** for the start of DFM 300: which scenes, where, and who's needed each day.
