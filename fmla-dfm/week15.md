## This Week's Reading

- [StudioBinder: 11 Essential Location Scouting Tips for Any Budget](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/essential-film-location-scouting-tips/)

**Look for:** Scouting with people you already know, using one location for several scenes, and getting permission in writing.

## Producing Task 15: Scout and lock your primary location and a backup

1. Visit your top location(s). Take photos from where the camera would go.
2. Check sound (fridges, HVAC, traffic), power outlets, light at your shoot time, and where the crew can wait.
3. Get **written permission** from the owner or manager, and lock a **backup** location.

## Pre-Production Logistics

**Milestone: location locked.** Add the primary and backup to your location list with contact info and scouting photos.
