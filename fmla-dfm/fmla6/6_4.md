## Reading

- [StudioBinder: Film Lookbook Examples & How to Make a Lookbook](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/film-lookbook-examples/)
- [StudioBinder: Film Lighting, The Ultimate Guide](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/film-lighting/)

**Look for:** How a lookbook shows mood, color and composition, and the basic lighting setups (three-point, high-key, low-key).

## Steps

1. Collect reference images for mood, color, lighting and composition, each with a short caption.
2. Arrange them into a lookbook, organized by scene or by category.
3. Add a **lighting and camera note** to each shot on your shot list.
