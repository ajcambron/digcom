## Reading

**Look for:** Your category's rules and runtime cap for the 2026–27 cycle.

## Steps

1. Draft a **shoot-day calendar** for the start of DFM 300: which scenes, where, and who's needed each day.
2. Put everything in one folder, in this order: **script, shot list, blocking, storyboard, lookbook, cast list, location list, costume/prop list, shoot calendar**.
3. Check every piece is the latest version and matches the others (same scene numbers, same cast).
4. List anything unresolved for Day 1 of DFM 300.
