## Reading

No new reading: use the camera-shots article from 6.1.

## Steps

1. Pick your most important scene.
2. Storyboard every shot in it, matching your shot list. Stick figures are fine; show framing and movement.
3. Fix anything in the shot list the storyboard showed you was missing.

## Before You Submit

- [ ] Storyboard for one key scene
- [ ] Updated shot list
- [ ] Everything is saved in your team's DFM folder
- [ ] Submitted
