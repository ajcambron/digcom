## Reading

- Judith Weston, *Directing Actors*: the opening chapters (acting as doing; playing an intention) (in the course materials)

**Look for:** Why directors give actors intentions and actions to play instead of line readings.

## Steps

1. Make final fixes to your revised draft. Check it fits your length target, then label it **LOCKED** with the date.
2. Pick **3 key lines**. For each, write the character's **intention**: what they're trying to do to the other person (to warn, to win over, to shut down…).
3. Write the direction you'd give the actor for each line: intentions and actions, never a line reading.
4. Write and post your **casting call**: the project, each role, the shoot dates and how to audition.
