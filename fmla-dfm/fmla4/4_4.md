## Reading

- [StudioBinder: What is a Table Read and Why Are They Important?](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/table-read-through/)

**Look for:** Hearing the script out loud to catch weak dialogue, pacing problems and confusing moments before you shoot.

## Steps

1. Assign every role, plus one reader for action lines. Read the whole script out loud without stopping.
2. One teammate takes notes: lines that stumbled, scenes that dragged, anything confusing.
3. Revise the script from those notes and cut until it fits your length target.
4. Draft a **costume and prop list, scene by scene**.
