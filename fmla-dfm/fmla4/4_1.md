{% include lesson-parts/stinger.md %}

## Reading

- [AAHSFF Rules & Categories](https://www.hsfilmfest.com/rulesandcategories)

**Look for:** Your category's runtime cap, the content rules (every song, image and clip must be licensed) and what past winners in your category have in common.

## Steps

1. As a team, pick one AAHSFF category from the table on the [FMLA DFM overview](../index.md#aahsff-categories). Drama is the default if you can't agree.
2. Write a **logline**: one sentence that names the main character, what they want, and what stands in the way.
3. Find **3–5 past nominees or winners** in your category on the festival site. For each, note the title, runtime, and one thing it does well.
4. Write your **length target**: about one script page per minute of screen time, under your category's cap.
5. Draft a **location and talent wishlist**: every place your story might happen and every role you'll need.

## Before You Submit

- [ ] Category + length target
- [ ] Logline
- [ ] 3–5 comparable past winners with notes
- [ ] Location/talent wishlist
