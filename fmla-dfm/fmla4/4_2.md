## Reading

- [StudioBinder: Save the Cat Beat Sheet Explained](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/save-the-cat-beat-sheet/)

**Look for:** How the beats map onto a short film. A 10-minute film still needs a catalyst, a midpoint and a finale, just compressed.

## Steps

1. Use the Save the Cat beats as a checklist. Give every beat 1–3 sentences.
2. Next to each beat, write the **page it lands on**, scaled to your length target.
3. Cut or merge beats until the total fits under your category's cap.
4. Narrow your location wishlist to a **shortlist of 2–3** real places.

## Before You Submit

- [ ] Beat sheet with page targets
- [ ] Location shortlist (2–3)
- [ ] Everything is saved in your team's DFM folder
- [ ] Submitted
