## Reading

- [StudioBinder: Indie Film Casting Explained](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/indie-short-film-casting-pointers/)

**Look for:** Casting breakdowns, writing a clear casting call, and running auditions.

## Steps

1. Write a **casting breakdown** for each role: age range, personality, and anything the actor must be able to do.
2. Pick a short audition scene (1–2 pages) from your locked script.
3. List candidate actors for each role, and reach out to at least two per lead role.
