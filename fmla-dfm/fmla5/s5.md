## Steps

1. **Cast list:** every role with the actor's name and contact, plus a backup for each lead, all confirmed in writing.
2. **Location list:** primary and backup locations with contact info, scouting photos and written permission.
3. **Costume/prop list:** final, with who is bringing each item.
4. Check all three lists match your locked script: same scenes, same characters.
