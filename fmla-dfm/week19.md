## This Week's Reading

- Walter Murch, *In the Blink of an Eye*, Ch. 1 ("The Rule of Six") (in the course materials)

**Look for:** Murch's six criteria for a cut, and why emotion comes first.

## Producing Task 19: Plan 3 alternate ways to cut one key moment, and justify your choice

1. Pick one key moment from your script.
2. Describe **3 different ways** it could be cut: which shots, in what order, and where each cut lands.
3. Choose one. Justify it with Murch's Rule of Six in 3–5 sentences.
