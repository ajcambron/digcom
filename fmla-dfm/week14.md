## This Week's Reading

**Look for:** Casting breakdowns, writing a clear casting call, and running auditions.

## Producing Task 14: Hold auditions; lock your lead role(s) and backups

1. Write a short **casting breakdown** for each role: age range, personality, anything the actor must be able to do.
2. Hold auditions. Have each actor read a short scene from your script, and record it if they agree.
3. Choose your lead(s) and a **backup** for each. Confirm with them in writing.

## Pre-Production Logistics

**Milestone: leads cast.** Record each lead's and backup's name and contact in your cast list.
