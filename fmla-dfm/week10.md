## This Week's Reading

- [StudioBinder: How to Format a Screenplay](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/brilliant-script-screenplay-format/)

**Look for:** Scene headings (INT./EXT., location, time of day), action lines in present tense, character cues and dialogue.

## Producing Task 10: Write a complete first draft in proper screenplay format

1. Turn your beat sheet into scenes, in order.
2. Use screenplay format: Courier 12 pt, scene headings, action, character cues, dialogue.
3. Check the page count against your length target. Being a little long is fine; you'll cut in Week 11.

## Pre-Production Logistics

**Reach out to your top-choice location(s)** to ask about access and permission. Keep a record of who you contacted and what they said.
