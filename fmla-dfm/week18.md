## This Week's Reading

- [StudioBinder: 50+ Types of Camera Shots, Angles, and Techniques](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-camera-shots/)
- [StudioBinder: Film Coverage and Efficient Shot Listing](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/film-coverage/)

**Look for:** Shot sizes and angles, what each one says about a character, and how coverage gives your editor choices.

## Producing Task 18: Storyboard and shot-list your locked script at your locked location

1. For every scene, write the **blocking**: where the actors stand and move.
2. Build a **shot list**: scene number, shot number, shot size, angle, movement and a one-line description.
3. Storyboard the most important scene. Stick figures are fine.

## Pre-Production Logistics

**Finalize costume and prop sourcing**: who brings what, for which scene.
