## This Week's Reading

- [StudioBinder: Save the Cat Beat Sheet Explained](https://www.studiobinder.com/blog/save-the-cat-beat-sheet/)

**Look for:** How the beats map onto a short film. A 10-minute film still needs a catalyst, a midpoint and a finale, just compressed.

## Producing Task 9: Write a full outline / beat sheet sized to your category's runtime cap

1. Use the Save the Cat beats as a checklist. Every beat gets 1–3 sentences.
2. Next to each beat, write the **page it lands on** in your script, scaled to your length target.
3. Cut or merge beats until the total fits under your category's cap.

## Pre-Production Logistics

Narrow your **location shortlist to 2–3** real places, and **start the talent wishlist** with names.
