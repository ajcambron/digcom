## This Week's Reading

- [AAHSFF Rules & Categories](https://www.hsfilmfest.com/rulesandcategories)
- [AAHSFF past nominees and winners (on the festival site)](https://www.hsfilmfest.com/)

**Look for:** Find your category's runtime cap, the content rules (every song, image and clip must be licensed) and what past winners in your category have in common.

## Producing Task 8: Lock your target category and logline; research 3–5 comparable past winners

1. As a team, pick one AAHSFF category from the table on the [FMLA DFM overview](index.md#aahsff-categories). Drama is the default if you can't agree.
2. Write a **logline**: one sentence that names the main character, what they want, and what stands in the way.
3. Find **3–5 past nominees or winners** in your category. For each, note the title, runtime, and one thing it does well that your film could learn from.
4. Write your **length target**: about one script page per minute of screen time, under your category's cap.

## Pre-Production Logistics

Draft a **location and talent wishlist**: every place your story might happen and every role you'll need to cast.
