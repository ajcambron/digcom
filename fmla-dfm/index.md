# FMLA DFM: Introduction to Digital Filmmaking During Leave

DFM 200's college session ends **Oct 23, 2026** with the Final Presentation. While your teacher is on leave, Units 4–6 keep your team working toward one goal: **writing and prepping a script for the All American High School Film Festival (AAHSFF)**, which you'll shoot in DFM 300 and submit to the festival.

| Unit | Dates | Focus | Summative |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Unit 4](fmla4/index.md) | Nov 4 – Dec 1, 2026 | Writing the Script | Locked Script & Actor Notes |
| [Unit 5](fmla5/index.md) | Dec 2, 2026 – Jan 4, 2027 | Casting & Locations | Cast & Location Lock |
| [Unit 6](fmla6/index.md) | Jan 5 – 22, 2027 | Visual Plan & Director's Prep Packet | Director's Prep Packet |

## Every Class

DFM meets on **B-days**. Every class follows the same routine:

1. **Stinger (10 min).** Answer the day's stinger on your Stinger Response Sheet.
2. **Team check-in (5 min).** Open the day's page and split the steps.
3. **Reading and team work time.** Do the reading, then the day's steps together.
4. **Save and wrap up** before you leave.

## How You're Graded

Each unit has four formatives (one per class, due by the start of the next class) and one summative that pulls them together, submitted on the last S day.

| Unit | .1 | .2 | .3 | .4 | S (summative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | AAHSFF Target & Logline | Outline & Beat Sheet | Screenplay Format & First Draft | Table Read & Revision | Locked Script & Actor Notes |
| 5 | Casting Breakdown | Auditions | Location Scouting | Permissions & Sourcing | Cast & Location Lock |
| 6 | Blocking & Shot List | Storyboard | Director–Editor: Alternate Cuts | Lookbook & Lighting | Director's Prep Packet |

## Before Unit 4 (Oct 27 and 29)

The S | 3 days after the Final Presentation are a head start, with nothing graded: form your team and watch past AAHSFF nominees and winners on the [festival site](https://www.hsfilmfest.com/) so you're ready to lock a category on 4.1.

## AAHSFF Categories

Lock one on 4.1. It sets your script's length from then on: about **one script page per minute** of screen time.

| Category | Max length |
|---|---|
| Drama (recommended default) | 20 min |
| Comedy | 15 min |
| Documentary | 15 min |
| Horror / Sci-Fi / Fantasy | 10 min |
| Animation | 10 min |
| Experimental | 10 min |
| Video Podcast | 10 min |
| Storyblocks: Representation Through Storytelling | 15 min |
| Broadcast Journalism: Feature Story | 5 min |
| Sports Feature | 5 min |
| Music Video | 5 min |
| Micro Movie | 1 min |

Check the [current rules and categories](https://www.hsfilmfest.com/rulesandcategories) once the 2026–27 cycle opens (expected around November 2026): the categories and dates can change.

## For the Substitute

- Start class by projecting today's stinger from the [DFM stinger deck](bellringers.md) and taking attendance. Then project the day's page (find it on the unit page).
- Teams work on their own. Circulate and check each team has the page open and someone working on each deliverable.
- Each day's teacher page has the full plan and grading notes. Add `-teacher` to the end of the page's address, for example `4_1-teacher.html`.
