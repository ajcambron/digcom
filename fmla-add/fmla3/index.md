# Unit 3 | Premiere Pro Module 2: Fire Academy Recruiting Video (Oct 13 – Oct 30, 2026)

## Introduction

Module 2: Miami Dade College hires you to cut a 60-second recruiting video for its Fire Science Technology Academy. You build a story spine from interviews with text-based editing, add B-roll, music and sound effects, trim with slip, slide and roll edits, and deliver two captioned versions.

This unit replaces the regular Applications of Digital Design Unit 3 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Premiere Pro.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BrainBuffet videos** | 2.01–2.17 (17 videos, 126:39 of video) |
| **Class periods** | 13 school days on the A/B calendar: 4 formative days plus 2–3 S days per section |
| **Summative** | the 60-second Fire Academy recruiting video, exported twice through Media Encoder: one version with burned-in captions and one with a sidecar caption file |
