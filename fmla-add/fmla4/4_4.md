# 4.4 | Horror Chase: No Way Out, Part 4: Stinger Sheet Check

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Stinger Sheet Check.** Your Stinger Response Sheet for this unit gets checked at the end of class.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 55 min | BrainBuffet Module Work |
| 8 min | Stinger Sheet Check |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Horror films are often graded green or blue. How does color change the way an audience feels?

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 3.13: Splitting Edits**.

Today's videos (26:33 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 3.11 | 11:54 | Sound Design |
| 3.12 | 4:02 | Scene Edit Detection |
| 3.13 | 10:37 | Splitting Edits |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Cut a 30-second festival trailer for No Way Out from your finished film and title cards.

## Stinger Sheet Check (last ~8 min)

Before the check, make sure every stinger from this unit is on your sheet, each with:

- [ ] the question rewritten in your own words
- [ ] your answer, at least 3 sentences
- [ ] your neighbor's answer, copied
- [ ] 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Premiere Pro** course and go to **Module 3**. Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Premiere Pro**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Premiere Pro on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. The module's footage lives in **`/Users/Shared/GMetrix`** on every lab Mac (Finder › **Go › Go to Folder…**). It's read-only: keep your own `.prproj` in your project folder and back it up to Drive at the end of class. Lost? See [ADD 2.1, Finding the GMetrix Folder]({{% link applications/add2/2_1.md %}}#finding-the-gmetrix-folder).
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
