# Unit 4 | Premiere Pro Module 3: Horror Chase Spec Film: No Way Out (Nov 4 – Dec 1, 2026)

## Introduction

Module 3: you edit a horror spec film headed to festivals. You work with proxies and mixed formats, plan in Freeform view, trim with ripple, extract and lift, design sound, crop to 2.40:1, color-grade for horror, and build rolling credits.

This unit replaces the regular Applications of Digital Design Unit 4 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Premiere Pro.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BrainBuffet videos** | 3.01–3.19 (19 videos, 138:26 of video) |
| **Class periods** | 14 school days on the A/B calendar: 4 formative days plus 3 S days per section |
| **Summative** | the No Way Out horror chase scene with a 2.40:1 crop, a horror color grade and rolling credits, exported to the festival specs (2048 × 1080, 24 fps, ProRes 422 HQ), with the project archived |
