# 5.3 | Athlete Showreel, Part 3: Module Worksheet

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Module 4 Worksheet.** Complete and submit it in the last 25 minutes of class.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 33 min | BrainBuffet Module Work |
| 25 min | Module Worksheet |
| 5 min | Submit & Wrap-Up |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Speed ramp or hard cut between slow motion and normal speed: which feels more exciting, and why?

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 4.07: Effect Presets**.

Today's videos (8:18 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 4.07 | 8:18 | Effect Presets |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Make 15–30 second teaser versions of the showreel for TikTok, Instagram Stories or X, keeping the story clear in less time.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Premiere Pro** course (Module 4). Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Premiere Pro**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Premiere Pro on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. The module's footage lives in **`/Users/Shared/GMetrix`** on every lab Mac (Finder › **Go › Go to Folder…**). It's read-only: keep your own `.prproj` in your project folder and back it up to Drive at the end of class. Lost? See [ADD 2.1, Finding the GMetrix Folder]({% link applications/add2/2_1.md %}#finding-the-gmetrix-folder).
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Module 4 Worksheet (last ~25 min)

Open your **Module 4 Handbook** (in the module's resources in BrainBuffet) and answer its three **Critical Thinking** questions in Schoology. Write 3–5 sentences for each, and use at least one vocabulary term from this module in every answer.

*Stuck on how to start? Try: "In this module I learned that ___. This matters because ___. For example, ___."*

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
