# Unit 6 | Premiere Pro Module 5: Exam Prep & Practice (Jan 5 – Jan 22, 2027)

## Introduction

Module 5 is short, fast concept videos aimed at ACP exam objectives the projects didn't cover deeply: client questions, shot types, Ultra Key, copyright and releases, PSD import, typography, audio fades, white balance, syncing, blend modes and Warp Stabilizer. It ends with a Grand Finale challenge.

This unit replaces the regular Applications of Digital Design Unit 6 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Premiere Pro.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BrainBuffet videos** | 5.01–5.14 (14 videos, 38:11 of video) |
| **Class periods** | 13 school days on the A/B calendar: 4 formative days plus 2–3 S days per section |
| **Summative** | the Grand Finale (video 5.14) exported, plus each mini-challenge practice file completed |
