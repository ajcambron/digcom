# Scope & Sequence: Processes of Digital Design (2026–27 draft)

{: .warning }
**Draft revision (unlisted).** Part of the first-draft PDD course revision. The live [Processes of Digital Design](../processes/index.md) pages and the Drive originals are unchanged. See the [revision overview](index.md).

Built backward from the ACP Photoshop exam as its own Unit 10 (Apr 20 – May 3, 2027), on the same calendar math as FDD and ADD: Units 1–9 are 4 formative lessons + 1 summative block each, the A/B schedule gives each section half of each unit's school days, and Units 11–12 are post-certification Extra Units. Competition skills both events judge most (studio lighting, B&W, still life, macro, the Photoshop editing task) get a first, rudimentary pass in Units 1–4, before winter break.

## Unit by unit

### Unit 1 | Camera Fundamentals & Exposure (Sep 1 – Sep 23, 2026)

Unit 1 puts a camera in every student's hands before Photoshop opens. Students learn to take control of exposure, lens, and composition in manual mode, and start the habit the whole year depends on: logging the settings behind every frame. That log becomes the metadata in their TSA statements and the vocabulary of the SkillsUSA written test.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Exam objectives** | 1.3.a (digital image terms), 1.4.a (rule of thirds, framing, aspect ratio, depth of field, field of view) |
| **Competition skills** | SkillsUSA K1–K3 (written test: photo basics, DSLR functions), K4 (on-location shooting), K6 (portrait, introduced); TSA T6 (student-choice image), T7 (settings log for statements), T11 (exposure and focus) |
| **Existing lessons used** | EX02 Intro to Digital Photography, EX03 Manual Photography (CameraSim), EX08 Focal Length/Crop Factor, EX09 Composition, EX05 Portrait Photography |
| **Class periods** | 5 per section: 4 formative + 1 for S1 |
| **Assessment** | F1–F4 exit tickets (rotating); F5 cumulative vocab quiz; F6 stinger grade; S1 Portrait Photography with a settings log |
| **GMetrix/BrainBuffet** | No GMetrix/BrainBuffet this unit. The lab setup and ACP pre-test happen in 2.1, the same pattern as FDD and ADD. |
| **Core (everyone)** | Every student shoots, logs settings, and turns in three portraits (S1). |
| **Competition track** | Competition-track students start a running SkillsUSA written-test flashcard deck (K1–K3) and tag one S1 frame as a possible TSA student-choice image. |
| **Literacy** | Frayer-model cards for aperture, shutter speed, ISO, and depth of field; reading the camera's own menu labels as technical text. |
| **Numeracy** | Shutter fractions (1/60 vs 1/250), one stop = double or half the light, f-number sequence, crop factor: 50 mm × 1.6 ≈ 80 mm equivalent. |

### Unit 3 | Photojournalism (Oct 13 – Oct 30, 2026)

Unit 3 sends students out to cover a real school event. It pairs the oldest photo genre with the most-tested ACP ideas in Domain 1: who owns an image, when you need a release, and what resolution and size a photo needs for its use. The cutline lesson also starts the written statements TSA requires for every portfolio image.

Unit 4 is the lighting unit, placed before Christmas on purpose: it gives every student a rudimentary pass at the skills both competitions judge most (studio lighting patterns and off-camera flash for SkillsUSA; black and white, macro, and still life for TSA's required image categories) before the winter break.
