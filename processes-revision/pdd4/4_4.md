## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

A **still life** is an arrangement of objects, lit and composed on purpose. Odd numbers, varied heights, and a triangle shape help. **Macro** photography shows a small subject life-size or larger. Up close, **depth of field** gets very shallow, so use a **tripod**, a smaller aperture, and focus carefully.

> "At this distance, f/5.6 gives me a sliver of focus. I'll go to f/11 and lock the camera down."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What is macro photography?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does depth of field get shallower as you move closer?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Design a still life that tells a story about a person without showing them."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Still life:** arrange three to five objects, light them with soft side light, and shoot on a tripod.
2. **Critique:** Turn and Talk with your team: what one change would improve the arrangement?
3. **Macro:** fill the frame with a small texture. Try f/5.6 and f/11.
4. **Log** both frames' settings.
