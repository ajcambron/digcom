# S | 4 | Mood Shoot: Light & Shadow

{: .note }
**Existing project, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX19 Mood Shoot: Light & Shadow. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

The culmination of this unit. You'll create three mood images (B&W, still life or macro, and a portrait with a named lighting pattern) that use light and shadow on purpose.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Teacher shows mood exemplars and lighting diagrams, then start your own.

## Project Work Time (~53 min)

### Deliverables checklist

- [ ] **Black-and-white** frame (adjustment layer, not mode change).
- [ ] **Still life or macro.**
- [ ] **Portrait** with a named lighting pattern.
- [ ] **Lighting diagram** for each.

{: .discussion }
**Check-in:** Studio teams of three rotate photographer, subject, and light roles; each frame gets one peer note before editing.

{: .important }
**Competition track (optional enrichment):** Mount one print to SkillsUSA spec.

## Save, Export & Submit (~5 min)

{: .reflection }
Reflection: what mood does each frame create, and what light choice made it?
