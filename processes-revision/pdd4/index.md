## Introduction

Unit 4 is the lighting unit, placed before Christmas on purpose: it gives every student a rudimentary pass at the skills both competitions judge most (studio lighting patterns and off-camera flash for SkillsUSA; black and white, macro, and still life for TSA's required image categories) before the winter break.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone shoots one B&W, one still life or macro, and one lit portrait.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** Mount one print to SkillsUSA spec (11×14 on 16×20 board with mat) and log a main/fill ratio with the light meter.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Short reading on Ansel Adams and the Zone System; lighting-pattern names as a picture vocabulary bank.
- **Numeracy:** Lighting ratios in stops (main f/8 + fill f/5.6 = 1 stop = 2:1), inverse-square falloff (double the distance, ¼ the light), the histogram as a data distribution.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Describe light by its quality, direction, and color, and set white balance to match
- Set up and name the basic portrait lighting patterns with an off-camera flash
- Convert an image to black and white with an adjustment layer and read its histogram
- Light and shoot a still life and a macro subject
