## Introduction

Unit 2 moves from the camera to Photoshop. It opens with the GMetrix setup and ACP pre-test day (the same 2.1 FDD and ADD run), then builds the three habits every later unit assumes: setting up a document correctly, working in named layers, and making a clean selection.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone: pre-test, workspace, layers, selections, About Me.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** A timed 'one-hour edit' drill on one of their Unit 1 portraits (SkillsUSA K9 format) during S2 time once About Me is submitted.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Reading procedural tutorial text (Classroom in a Book steps) with a step-checking partner.
- **Numeracy:** Pixel dimensions = inches × ppi (8 in × 300 ppi = 2400 px); document size presets compared.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Create a document with the right size, resolution, and color mode for its use, and save a preset
- Navigate and customize the workspace (Hand, Zoom, Rotate View, rulers, guides, preferences)
- Create, name, group, and reorder layers, and set opacity, fill, and blend mode
- Choose the right selection tool for a subject and refine the edge
