## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Each **layer** is a separate sheet. Name every layer (double-click its name), **group** related layers (⌘G), and drag to change the stacking order. **Opacity** fades everything on a layer; **Fill** fades the pixels but not the layer style. **Blend modes** change how a layer mixes with the ones below. **Free Transform** (⌘T) scales and rotates.

> "If I can't find a layer in three seconds, my file isn't organized yet."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "How do you rename a layer?"

> **Analysis:** "When would you lower Fill instead of Opacity?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Plan the layer structure for a magazine cover. What groups would you make?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Open** the Layer Basics start file and save a copy as yourname-layers.psd.
2. **Follow the tutorial** steps with your step-checking partner.
3. **Name and group** every layer as you go.
4. **Turn and Talk:** show your partner one blend mode and explain what it did.
