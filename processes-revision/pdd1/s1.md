# S | 1 | Portrait Photography

{: .note }
**Existing project, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX05 Portrait Photography. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

The culmination of this unit. You'll photograph three portraits in manual mode that apply exposure, depth of field, and composition on purpose, and log the settings for each.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Teacher shows three exemplar portraits and the settings log, then start your own.

## Project Work Time (~53 min)

### Deliverables checklist

- [ ] **Environmental portrait**: the subject in a place that says something about them.
- [ ] **Close-up portrait**: shallow depth of field, eyes sharp.
- [ ] **Free choice**: any technique from 1.4.
- [ ] **Settings log** for all three (camera, f-stop, shutter speed, ISO, focal length).

{: .discussion }
**Check-in:** Partners take turns as photographer and subject, and give one composition note per shot.

{: .important }
**Competition track (optional enrichment):** Tag your best frame as a TSA student-choice candidate.

## Save, Export & Submit (~5 min)

{: .reflection }
Reflection: which portrait best shows a Unit 1 skill, and which skill?
