# 1.3 | Focal Length, Crop Factor & Depth of Field

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, reordered, content unchanged.** From the current PDD course: EX08 Focal Length / Sensor / Crop Factor. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Zoom changes more than how big your subject looks. Today you'll see how lens and aperture shape the whole picture.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Two photos of the same face: one looks flattering, one looks distorted. What do you think changed?

## Direct Instruction (~12 min)

**Focal length** (in mm) sets the **field of view**: 18 mm is wide, 55 mm is tighter. Our cameras have a smaller sensor than full-frame, so they crop the view by about **1.6×**: a 50 mm lens looks like 80 mm would on a full-frame camera. **Depth of field** is how much of the scene is in focus. A wide aperture (small f-number), a longer focal length, and a closer subject all make it shallower.

> "I'm going to blur this background without touching Photoshop. Watch: f/5.6, zoom in, step closer."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does a shorter focal length do to the field of view?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does a portrait at 55 mm often look more flattering than one at 18 mm up close?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "You need a whole team photo sharp, front row to back. Which three settings would you choose, and why?"

## Guided Practice & Shoot (~50 min)

1. **Zoom pair:** photograph the same subject at 18 mm and at 55 mm, moving your feet so it fills the frame both times.
2. **Aperture pair:** in Av or M, photograph a subject with a background at f/5.6 and at f/22.
3. **Turn and Talk:** before checking the LCD, predict which frame has the blurrier background. Then check.
4. **Log it:** add both pairs to your settings log with focal length and f-number.

{: .discussion }
When would you want a deep depth of field instead of a blurry background?

{: .important }
**Competition track (optional enrichment):** Crop-factor practice: convert 18, 35, and 55 mm to full-frame equivalents for the flashcard deck.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~6 min)

{: .reflection }
How confident are you predicting depth of field before you take the shot? Explain with one of today's pairs.
