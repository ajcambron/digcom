## Introduction

Unit 3 sends students out to cover a real school event. It pairs the oldest photo genre with the most-tested ACP ideas in Domain 1: who owns an image, when you need a release, and what resolution and size a photo needs for its use. The cutline lesson also starts the written statements TSA requires for every portfolio image.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone covers one event, builds a contact sheet, and turns in five captioned images.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** Practice a 45-minute timed on-location theme assignment (SkillsUSA K4) and draft one TSA-style image statement from an S3 frame.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Cutlines (5 Ws), reading Creative Commons license terms and a release form closely.
- **Numeracy:** Resolution math (px ÷ ppi = inches), aspect ratios (3:2 camera vs 4:5 Instagram vs 16:9), crop percentages.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Plan a shoot around purpose, audience, and a shot list
- Crop for impact and set resolution and image size for print or screen
- Decide when an image is legal and ethical to use, and when a model or location release is needed
- Write an accurate cutline that answers the 5 Ws
