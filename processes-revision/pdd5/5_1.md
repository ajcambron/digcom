# 5.1 | Red Eye, Brighten, Liquify & Dodge/Burn

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX21 RedEye / Brighten / Liquify. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Retouching is about making a photo better, not fake. Today you'll learn the classic tools and the line not to cross.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

**Red Eye** fixes flash reflection in pupils. **Brighten** with a Curves or Levels adjustment layer. **Liquify** pushes pixels (use sparingly). **Dodge** lightens, **Burn** darkens, and **Sponge** changes saturation; work on a duplicate layer so the original stays safe.

> "I'll dodge just the eyes, 10% exposure. If you notice it, I went too far."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does the Burn tool do?"

> **Analysis:** "Why work on a duplicate layer?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Where's the line between retouching and misrepresenting a person?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Retouch** the tutorial portrait step by step.
2. **Apply** the same tools to one of your own portraits.
3. **Blink test:** your partner looks at before and after for two seconds each. Can they spot the edits?
4. **Dial back** anything that looks fake.
