## Introduction

Unit 5 teaches students to fix photos: the retouching and repair tools that fill the SkillsUSA Photoshop task and a large share of ACP Domain 5. Every exit ticket this unit includes one planted photo error to diagnose, building the SkillsUSA troubleshooting skill a little at a time.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone retouches, repairs, and turns in an original panorama shot on campus.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** During the S5 block (Dec 14 – Jan 4): TSA draft portfolio checkpoint (5 categories chosen, one statement drafted) and a SkillsUSA résumé draft.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Procedural text: students write their own 4-step 'how I fixed it' note for each repair (it becomes TSA's 'edits made' line).
- **Numeracy:** Degrees in the Straighten and Rotate tools; percent overlap (25–40%) between panorama frames.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Retouch a portrait with healing, red eye, Dodge/Burn/Sponge, and Liquify without over-editing
- Remove distractions with Content-Aware Fill, Remove, Patch, and Clone Stamp
- Apply blur and depth-of-field effects as Smart Filters
- Correct perspective and distortion, and merge a panorama
