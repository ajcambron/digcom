## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

**Free Transform** (⌘T) has modes: **skew**, **distort**, **perspective**, and **warp**. **Perspective Warp** reshapes a photo by defining planes. The **Perspective Crop** tool straightens a shape like a sign shot at an angle. **Lens Correction** fixes barrel distortion and converging verticals.

> "I draw the crop box on the four corners of the sign, press Return, and it's a flat rectangle."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does the Perspective Crop tool do?"

> **Analysis:** "Why do wide-angle lenses cause more distortion?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "How would you place a poster onto a wall photographed at an angle?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Straighten a building** with Lens Correction or Perspective Warp.
2. **Flatten a sign** with Perspective Crop.
3. **Place a graphic** onto a wall with Distort.
4. **Partner check:** does it look real?
