**Draft revision (unlisted).** Part of the first-draft PDD course revision. The live [Processes of Digital Design](../../processes/index.md) pages and the Drive originals are unchanged. See the [revision overview](../index.md).

## Introduction

The first Extra Unit returns to the journalism side of the course with the Teacher/Staff Profile, the existing project that was moved out of Unit 3 because it is four to five periods of writing with no ACP coverage. Competition-track students prepare for TSA's semifinal (24-hour shoot, presentation, interview) and SkillsUSA nationals.

{: .highlight }
Nothing in this unit is ACP-tested. Outline only in this draft: full lesson pages come after the course plan is approved.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone writes and photographs a staff profile.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** TSA semifinal practice: a mock 24-hour shoot-and-edit prompt with tripod and off-camera flash, a 5-minute presentation, and a 5-minute interview; SkillsUSA mock written test and job interview.
