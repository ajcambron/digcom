## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

**Shape tools** make editable vector shapes. The **Pen tool** draws paths with **anchor points**: click for corners, drag for curves. A path can become a **vector mask**. The **Frame tool** makes a placeholder that crops any image placed inside it.

> "Click, click, drag. Two anchors and a handle make this curve."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "Click or drag: which makes a curve?"

> **Analysis:** "Why would you use a vector mask instead of a pixel mask?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Plan the fewest anchor points for a heart shape."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Complete** the Pen practice sheet.
2. **Make** a vector mask from a path.
3. **Place** a photo into a frame.
4. **Partner check.**
