Light painting draws in the air with a flashlight while the shutter stays open.

{% include lesson-parts/stinger.md %}

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

A **long exposure** keeps the shutter open for seconds. Use a **tripod**, ISO 100, f/8, and 10–30 seconds. Focus manually on where the painter will stand.

> "Thirty seconds, starting now. Painter, go."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "Why is a tripod required?"

> **Analysis:** "What changes if you double the exposure time?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Plan a light painting that tells a story."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Set** the camera on a tripod.
2. **Focus** manually.
3. **Shoot** three attempts.
4. **Log** your settings.
