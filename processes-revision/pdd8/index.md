## Introduction

Unit 8 covers the brush engine and Photoshop's vector side. It adds light painting, a long-exposure technique that's a strong student-choice portfolio image, and closes the last ACP gaps in Domains 3 and 4 (Pen and shape tools, the Frame tool, and vector masks).

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone paints, shoots one light painting, and builds a Doodle with vector elements.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** Refine the light painting as a TSA student-choice candidate with a complete settings statement.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Short reading on Impressionism; artist statement for the Doodle.
- **Numeracy:** Long-exposure seconds and the reciprocal rule; anchor points as coordinates.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Paint with the Mixer Brush and custom brush settings
- Turn a photo into a painterly image with brushes, the History Brush, and filters
- Plan and shoot a long-exposure light painting
- Draw with the Pen and shape tools, and mask with frames and vector masks
