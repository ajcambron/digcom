# 8.1 | Mixer Brush

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, reordered, content unchanged.** From the current PDD course: EX36 Mixer Basics. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Today you'll paint with digital paint that behaves like the real thing.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
How is painting in Photoshop different from painting on paper?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

The **Brush** tool's size, hardness, and flow shape every stroke. The **Mixer Brush** mixes colors on the canvas like wet paint: **wet** sets how much it picks up, **load** how much paint it carries, **mix** the blend. Save a favorite as a **brush preset**.

> "Wet high, load low, and it smears the photo like a brush through wet paint."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does the Wet setting control?"

> **Analysis:** "Why save a brush preset?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Which settings would you use to turn a photo into an oil painting?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Open** the Mixer files.
2. **Follow the tutorial.**
3. **Save a preset.**
4. **Turn and Talk:** show one stroke you like.
