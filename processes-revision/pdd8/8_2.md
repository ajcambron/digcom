# 8.2 | Painting & Impressionism

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX37 Painting, EX38 Photoshop Impressionism. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Today you'll paint like an Impressionist, starting from your own photo.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Look at a Monet. What did he paint instead of details?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Impressionists painted light and color, not detail. The **History Brush** paints back from an earlier state; the **Art History Brush** paints stylized strokes from it. The **Eraser**, **Background Eraser**, and **Magic Eraser** remove pixels (destructively).

> "I blurred everything. Now the History Brush paints the eyes back in, sharp."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does the History Brush paint from?"

> **Analysis:** "Why is the eraser family destructive?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Which photo of yours would make the best painting, and why?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Pick a photo.**
2. **Block in** with filters or big brushes.
3. **Restore detail** with the History Brush.
4. **Gallery walk.**

{: .discussion }
Is a painted photo still a photograph?
