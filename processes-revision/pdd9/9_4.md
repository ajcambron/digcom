## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

An **11×14** print at 300 ppi needs **3300 × 4200 px**. A 16×20 mat with an 11×14 window leaves borders of 2.5 in (left/right) and 3 in (top/bottom). TSA's PDF order: title page, table of contents, theme summary, five images, statements, references, forms.

> "16 minus 11 is 5, split two ways: 2.5 inches each side."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "How many pixels for an 11×14 at 300 ppi?"

> **Analysis:** "Why soft-proof before printing?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Design a mat for an 8×10 print."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Solve** the mat math.
2. **Build** the PDF skeleton.
3. **Soft-proof** one image.
4. **Partner check.**
