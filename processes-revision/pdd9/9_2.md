## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Web images use **JPEG** (photos) or **PNG** (transparency) in **sRGB**. Keep files small. **Alt text** describes an image for screen readers. Text needs strong **contrast** against its background.

> "Alt text isn't 'photo.' It's 'Senior Maya Ortiz leaps for a header at the homecoming game.'"

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "Which format for a photo on the web?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does alt text matter?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Write alt text for your best photo."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Export** three images.
2. **Add** them to your site.
3. **Write** alt text.
4. **Phone test.**
