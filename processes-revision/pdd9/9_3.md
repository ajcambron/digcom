## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Save working files as **PSD**. Export **JPEG** for photos on screens, **PNG** for transparency, **TIFF** for print. Use **sRGB** for screens and the printer's profile for print. **Quick Export** is fast; **Export As** gives control. **Export Layers/Artboards** makes many files at once. **Package** gathers linked files.

> "Same image, five uses, five exports. The format follows the job."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "Which format keeps transparency?"

> **Analysis:** "Why do colors look dull when a CMYK file goes to the web?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Write the export settings for a 16×20 print and an Instagram post."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Export** one project five ways.
2. **Package** a file.
3. **Fix** the broken file.
4. **Partner test.**
