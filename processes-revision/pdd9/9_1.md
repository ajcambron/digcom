## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

A **brief** says what the client needs. You answer with **sketches**, **mood boards**, and **specs**. **Scope** is what's included; **scope creep** is extras that sneak in. A **timeline** sets deadlines. A file plan keeps versions straight.

> "'That's a great idea. It's outside our scope, so here's what it would add to the timeline.'"

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What is scope creep?"

> **Analysis:** "Why write a timeline before starting?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "How would you handle a client who wants a different style halfway through?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Read** the sample brief.
2. **Write** a project plan.
3. **Role-play** a client meeting.
4. **Swap roles.**
