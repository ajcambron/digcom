# 7.3 | Advanced Compositing

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, reordered, content unchanged.** From the current PDD course: EX34 Advanced Compositing Background. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

A great composite makes the viewer believe. Today you'll learn the five things that give fakes away.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Why do some composites look fake? List two giveaways.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Composite sources must match in **light direction**, **color**, **perspective**, and **edge softness**, and the subject needs a **shadow**. Clip an adjustment layer to one source so it changes only that layer.

> "The light in the background comes from the left; my subject is lit from the right. I'll flip her."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "Name the five believability checks."

> **Analysis:** "Why clip an adjustment layer?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "How would you light a subject for a background you haven't seen yet?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Complete** the tutorial.
2. **Swap** a subject onto a new background.
3. **Run the checklist** with your partner.
4. **Fix** the weakest item.

{: .discussion }
What composite giveaway is hardest to fix?
