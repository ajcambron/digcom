# 7.2 | Text-ure: Layer Styles & Materials

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX33 Text-ure. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Today you'll make type out of your own photos.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
How could you make text look like it's carved in wood, or made of a photo?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Clip a photo into type to fill the letters. **Layer styles** add bevels, shadows, and strokes, and stay editable. Save a combination to the **Styles** panel. **Materials** apply surface looks like metal or wood.

> "Double-click the layer. Every style is a checkbox I can turn off later."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "How do you fill type with a photo?"

> **Analysis:** "Why are layer styles nondestructive?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Design a title style for a photo exhibit."

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Set a title** in a heavy font.
2. **Clip** your photo into it.
3. **Add two layer styles** and save them.
4. **Apply a material** and compare.

{: .discussion }
When does a layer style help, and when does it look dated?
