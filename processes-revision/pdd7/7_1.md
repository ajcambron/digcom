## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

A **Smart Object** remembers its original pixels, so scaling down and back up doesn't lose quality. **Puppet Warp** adds pins so you can bend an arm or a tail. A Smart Object can be **embedded** in the file or **linked** to an outside file.

> "Pin the shoulder, pin the wrist, drag the elbow. The arm bends where I want it."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does a Smart Object protect?"

> **Analysis:** "When would you link instead of embed?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Plan a pose using Puppet Warp: where would the pins go?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Open** the Monster Maker files.
2. **Convert** parts to Smart Objects.
3. **Puppet Warp** one limb.
4. **Turn and Talk:** show your best pin placement.
