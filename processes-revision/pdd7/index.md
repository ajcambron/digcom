## Introduction

Unit 7 is the composite unit: building a believable image from several sources. It is the SkillsUSA fine-art composite, and it is also where students meet Photoshop's generative AI tools, which the ACP exam tests and TSA's honor statement bans. Students learn both the tools and where the line is.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone builds a composite and documents its sources.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** SkillsUSA-format composite print; for TSA, keep all source frames unaltered for the References page and leave GenAI out entirely.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Short argument paragraph: 'Should AI-generated images be allowed in photo contests?' using the TSA honor statement and Adobe's Content Credentials as sources.
- **Numeracy:** Scale percentages when matching subject size across sources; vanishing-point alignment.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Transform Smart Objects nondestructively, including Puppet Warp
- Apply and edit layer styles and materials
- Match light, color, and perspective across composite sources
- Use Generative Fill, Remove Background, and Sky Replacement, and explain when they may and may not be used
