## Introduction

Unit 6 is the nondestructive core of Photoshop: masks instead of erasers, saved selections in channels, and type that stays live. It ends with a magazine cover, which puts a student photograph under a real typographic hierarchy.

## Core vs. Competition Track

- **Core (everyone):** Everyone masks, builds a double exposure, and designs a cover around their own photo.
- **Competition track (enrichment):** Re-edit one TSA candidate image with masks only (no erasing) and log the edits for its statement.

## Literacy & Numeracy

- **Literacy:** Headline and deck writing for the cover; type vocabulary (kerning, tracking, leading) with picture cards.
- **Numeracy:** Point size and leading (12 pt type on 14.4 pt leading = 120%), trim size + 0.125 in bleed on each side.

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Hide and reveal with layer masks, refine edges in Select and Mask, and save selections to channels
- Blend two photos into a double exposure with masks, blend modes, and clipping masks
- Build and save gradients, swatches, and patterns
- Set type with a clear hierarchy using the Character and Paragraph panels
