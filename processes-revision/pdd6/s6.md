# S | 6 | Magazine Cover

{: .note }
**Existing project, reordered, content unchanged.** From the current PDD course: EX31 Magazine Cover. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

The culmination of this unit. You'll design a magazine cover around their own photo with a masked masthead and a clear typographic hierarchy.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Teacher deconstructs a real cover's hierarchy, then start your own.

## Project Work Time (~53 min)

### Deliverables checklist

- [ ] **Original cover photo.**
- [ ] **Masthead** partly behind the subject (mask).
- [ ] **Hierarchy** of headline, cover lines, and details.
- [ ] **Trim and bleed** set correctly.

{: .discussion }
**Check-in:** Midpoint peer critique: partners read the cover for five seconds and say what they read first, second, and third.

## Save, Export & Submit (~5 min)

{: .reflection }
Reflection: does the reading order on your cover match what you planned? What would you change?
