# 6.3 | Patterns, Gradients & Swatches

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, lightly edited.** From the current PDD course: EX29 Pattern. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Color and patterns you build once can be reused forever. Today you'll build a kit.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Find a pattern in the room. What's the smallest piece that repeats?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Sample colors with the **Eyedropper** and add them to the **Swatches** panel in a named group. Export swatches as an **ASE** file to share with other Adobe apps. The **Gradient tool** blends colors; save your own as a preset. **Edit → Define Pattern** turns a selection into a repeating fill. **Libraries** store all of these in the cloud.

> "Eyedropper, sample, new swatch. Do it five times and I have a palette straight from my photo."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does an ASE file store?"

> **Analysis:** "Why save a gradient as a preset?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Build a palette for a brand from one of your photos. Which five colors, and why?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Sample five colors** from one of your photos into a named swatch group.
2. **Export** the group as ASE.
3. **Build and save a gradient** from two swatches.
4. **Define a pattern** and fill a layer with it.

{: .discussion }
How could a photographer use a palette from their photos in their portfolio design?
