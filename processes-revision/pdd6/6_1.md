# 6.1 | Masks & Channels

{: .note }
**Existing lesson, reordered, content unchanged.** From the current PDD course: EX28 Masks & Channels. Draft revision page; the original files are untouched.

Masks are the most important skill in Photoshop. They let you hide anything without throwing it away.

## Stinger (~10 min)

{% include lesson-parts/stinger.md %}

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

A **layer mask** hides parts of a layer: paint **black** to hide, **white** to reveal, gray for partly. **Select and Mask** refines tricky edges like hair. Save a selection (**Select → Save Selection**) and it becomes an **alpha channel** you can reload any time.

> "I hid too much. No problem: switch to white and paint it back."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does black do on a layer mask?"

> **Analysis:** "Why is a mask better than the eraser?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "How could a saved channel help on a project you return to next week?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Open** the Lesson06 start file.
2. **Follow the tutorial** with your step partner.
3. **Save a selection** as a named channel.
4. **Turn and Talk:** show a mask edit you could undo.
