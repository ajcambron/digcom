## Camera Setup
1. Download the [Blackmagic Camera](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/blackmagiccamera){:target="_blank"} App for your phone, and configure it for compatibility:
    1. Set the codec to **H.264** for compatibility with your editing software.
    2. Set the resolution to **1080p (not 4K)**. 1080p is more than enough for every project in this course, and keeps your footage files 4–5× smaller.
    3. Turn on the guides so that you can frame your shots using the rule-of-thirds.
