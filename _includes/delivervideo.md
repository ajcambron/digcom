## Strike
Film sets "strike" (tear down) the set when shooting wraps — editors do the same with their drives. Once your final export is delivered and your portfolio page is published:
1. Delete the raw footage from the project folder on your computer. Your final export and project file are all you keep.
2. Empty the Trash — deleted footage still takes up space until you do.
3. Confirm your final export plays from Google Drive before you leave class.
