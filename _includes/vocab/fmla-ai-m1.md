**Anchor Point**
: A point on a path that controls the path's shape and direction, shown as a small square.

**Area Type**
: Type that flows inside a shape or text box and wraps to the next line at the box's edge.

**Artboard**
: The page-like canvas you design on in Illustrator. One document can hold several artboards.

**Fill**
: The color, gradient or pattern inside a shape.

**Handles**
: The lines that stick out of a selected anchor point on a curve. Dragging their round ends changes the curve.

**Landscape Orientation**
: A layout that is wider than it is tall.

**Pixel**
: One tiny square of color in the grid that makes up a raster image.

**Point Type**
: A single line of type that starts where you click and keeps going until you press Return.

**Portrait Orientation**
: A layout that is taller than it is wide.

**Raster (Bitmap)**
: An image made of a fixed grid of pixels. It gets blurry or blocky when enlarged.

**Stroke**
: The outline along a shape's path. Its thickness is the stroke weight.

**Swatches**
: Saved colors, gradients or patterns in the Swatches panel, ready to reuse.

**Vector**
: Artwork built from mathematical paths, so it can be scaled to any size without losing quality.
