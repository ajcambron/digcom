**Selection Tool**
: The solid black arrow (shortcut **V**) — selects and moves an entire object, group, or several
objects at once by their bounding box.

**Direct Selection Tool**
: The hollow white arrow (shortcut **A**) — selects and edits individual anchor points, segments,
or direction handles within a path, without moving the whole object.

**Lasso Tool**
: Selects anchor points or whole paths by freehand-dragging an irregular loop around them.

**Group Selection Tool**
: Nested with the Direct Selection tool — one click selects a single object inside a group, a
second click on the same object selects its whole group, and each additional click selects the
next group up.

**Isolation Mode**
: Entered by double-clicking a group or object — temporarily dims and locks everything else on
the artboard so you can edit inside the group without accidentally affecting the rest of the
artwork.
