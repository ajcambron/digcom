**Process Color**
: A standard saved color built from CMYK or RGB percentages — the everyday kind of swatch, as
opposed to a spot color or a global color.

**Spot Swatch**
: A swatch representing a single pre-mixed ink (see Spot Color) — used in the Swatches panel when
an exact brand or print color needs to be called out specifically rather than built from CMYK.

**Global Swatch**
: A saved swatch that stays linked everywhere it's applied — editing the swatch once updates
every object using it, which saves enormous time when a client's brand color is used across many
objects.

**Color Guide Panel**
: A panel that takes one base color and generates whole harmonious groups from it (complementary,
analogous, triadic, and more) — the same relationships from the color wheel, generated
automatically.

**Gradient**
: A smooth blend between two or more colors, applied as a fill or stroke. Illustrator's basic
gradient types are **linear** (blends along a straight line) and **radial** (blends outward from
a center point).

**Color Stop**
: One colored point along a gradient's ramp. Drag a stop to reposition it, double-click it to
change its color, or lower its opacity to fade it out.

**Gradient Mesh**
: An advanced tool that blends many colors across a shape along a flexible grid of points instead
of a straight line or circle — capable of photorealistic shading, but the most complex of
Illustrator's gradient tools.
