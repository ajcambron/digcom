**B-roll**
: Supplementary footage that provides visual context to a story, covering edits or illustrating the topic.

**Sound Bite (SOT)**
: A short, edited clip of an interview or speech used to convey a key point.

**Stand-up**
: The portion of a report where the reporter appears on camera to deliver information, usually from the scene of the story.

**VO (Voiceover)**
: The reporter's voice track, read live or recorded, laid over video footage.

**Nat Sound/Ambient Sound**
: Natural sound from the environment (traffic noise, crowd applause) used to add realism to a story.

**Editing**
: The process of cutting and arranging footage and audio into the final news story.
