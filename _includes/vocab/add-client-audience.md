**Client Goals**
: The outcomes a client wants a video to achieve — informing, persuading, entertaining, or
building their brand — that shape every creative decision on the project.

**Target Audience**
: The specific group of viewers a video is made for; understanding who they are shapes a video's
tone, pacing, and content choices.

**Demographics**
: Measurable characteristics of a target audience — age, location, education, interests — used
to describe and understand who a video is being made for.
