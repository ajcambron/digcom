**Portfolio**
: A curated collection of a designer's best work, organized and presented to show range,
growth, and skill to an audience such as a client, employer, or admissions reviewer.

**Curation**
: The deliberate act of selecting which pieces belong in a portfolio (and which don't) — a
strong portfolio is chosen, not simply everything a designer has ever made.

**Project Description (Artist Statement)**
: A short written summary accompanying a portfolio piece that names the project's goal, the
designer's process, and the tools/techniques used — context a viewer can't get from the image
alone.

**Presentation**
: The formal act of showing and explaining design work to an audience, including the poise,
pacing, and structure of how it's delivered, not just the work itself.

**Constructive Criticism**
: Feedback that is specific, objective, and kind — naming exactly what works and what could
improve, rather than vague praise or unfocused negativity.

**Plus/Delta**
: A two-column critique format — Plus (+) for what's working, Delta (Δ) for what should change —
used to structure peer feedback into something specific and actionable.

**Poise**
: Confidence and composure in how a presentation is delivered — steady pacing, eye contact, and
clear language, developed through practice and repetition.
