**Client Goals**
: What the person or organization commissioning the design actually needs the finished work to
accomplish.

**Target Audience**
: The specific group of people a design is created for.

**Demographics**
: Measurable traits of an audience (age, location, income, etc.) used to describe who it is made
up of.

**Accessibility**
: Designing so people with a range of abilities (vision, hearing, motor, cognitive) can use and
understand the work.

**Project Scope**
: The defined boundaries of what a project will and won't include.

**Scope Creep**
: When a project's requirements keep growing beyond what was originally agreed to.

**Project Timeline**
: The schedule of milestones and deadlines a project is expected to follow.
