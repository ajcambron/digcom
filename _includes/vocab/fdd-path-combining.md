**Eraser Tool**
: Removes parts of a path or shape by dragging across it, automatically re-closing whatever path
remains.

**Scissors Tool**
: Cuts a path at a clicked point, splitting it into two separate endpoints.

**Knife Tool**
: Freehand-cuts through one or more objects along the path you drag, dividing filled shapes into
separate pieces.

**Shape Builder Tool**
: Merges, subtracts, or extracts overlapping shapes — click a region to keep it, drag across
several regions to merge them, or click-and-hold Option/Alt to subtract.

**Live Paint**
: A mode that treats a group of intersecting paths as if painted with real ink — click any
enclosed region to fill it with color, or click any segment to give it its own stroke, all
without needing separate closed shapes.

**Compound Path**
: Two or more paths combined into a single, unified path — commonly used to punch a see-through
hole in a shape (like the counter of a letter "O").

**Pathfinder**
: A panel of one-click boolean operations — Unite, Minus Front, Intersect, Exclude, and more —
that combine multiple overlapping shapes into new, single shapes.
