**Driving Question**
: The specific mystery or conflict your podcast episode is trying to explore. Narrower than a
general topic.

**The "Not" List**
: Related topics you deliberately exclude to keep an episode focused and within its time limit.

**Interviewer (Host)**
: The talent role asking questions and guiding the conversation's arc.

**Guest**
: The talent role serving as the subject matter expert being interviewed.

**Floor Director**
: Manages the recording set, provides timing cues, and coordinates the crew.

**Video Engineer**
: Operates the live switcher to change camera angles and graphics during a live or live-to-tape
recording.

**Audio Engineer**
: Monitors volume levels and triggers music or sound effects during recording.
