**Gap in Timeline**
: An empty stretch of black or silence between clips on a track, usually accidental, caused by a
Lift edit or a clip that got moved. Easy to miss in the editor, obvious to a viewer.

**Safe Margins**
: The action-safe and title-safe zones near a frame's edges, areas that might get cropped off on
some TVs or when a video gets reframed for another platform. Keep important text and action
inside them.

**Audio Levels (QC)**
: Checking that a sequence's audio never clips and never drops out inaudibly quiet, a final pass
before delivery, not just during recording.

**Black Frame**
: A single frame with no image, sometimes left accidentally at the head or tail of a sequence
during assembly. A quick visual check catches it before export.
