**Storyboard**
: A sequence of sketches previsualizing a shot's framing and camera angle before filming — doesn't
need to be polished, just clear enough to communicate the plan.

**Shot List**
: A written, ordered list of every shot a production needs, often tied to specific timing or
story beats.

**Pre-Production**
: All the planning work that happens before filming — scripting, storyboarding, securing rights,
scheduling.

**Royalty-Free Music**
: Music licensed for a one-time fee (or free) that can be used repeatedly without ongoing
royalty payments — a safe choice when a production doesn't have rights to original music.

**Public Domain**
: Creative work no longer (or never) protected by copyright — free to use without permission or
licensing.

**Synchronization Rights**
: Legal permission to pair a piece of music with visual media — required any time you use someone
else's music in a video, even a school project.
