**Auto Reframe**
: A Premiere feature that uses motion tracking to automatically reposition a sequence's frame
when converting to a different aspect ratio, keeping the detected subject centered.

**Target Aspect Ratio**
: The frame shape a version of a video is being delivered for. 16:9 (widescreen), 9:16
(vertical/Stories/Reels), 1:1 (square).

**Motion Tracking**
: Software that follows a specific point or subject's movement across frames, the technology
underneath Auto Reframe and many visual effects.

**Clip Nesting**
: Placing an entire sequence inside another sequence as if it were a single clip, so it can be
reframed, retimed, or effected as one unit without altering the original edit.
