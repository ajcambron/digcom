**Continuity Photo**
: A reference photograph of a cast member's costume or a location's setup, taken before filming
so hair, wardrobe, and set dressing can be matched exactly if a scene is reshot or filmed out of
order across multiple days.

**Visual Reference**
: Any image (a film still, a painting, a photo) collected to help a team agree on a shared look,
mood, or tone before shooting begins, without needing every idea spelled out in words.

**Mise-en-Scene**
: Everything visible within the frame that was deliberately arranged. settings, props, costume,
lighting, color, and the positioning of people and objects. French for "placing on stage."
