Close-Up (CU)
: A shot framed tightly on a subject's face or a small detail.

Extreme Close-Up (ECU)
: A shot framed even tighter than a close-up, isolating a small detail like the eyes.

Medium Close-Up (MCU)
: A shot framed between a medium shot and a close-up, typically head and shoulders.

Medium Shot (MS)
: A shot that frames a subject from roughly the waist up.

Wide Shot (WS)
: A shot that shows a subject fully within their surrounding environment.

Long Shot (LS)
: A shot taken from a distance that shows the subject small within their environment.

Extreme Long Shot (ELS)
: A very distant shot emphasizing the vastness of a location over the subject.

Over-the-Shoulder (OTS)
: A shot framed from behind one subject's shoulder, showing another subject facing them.

Point of View (POV)
: A shot framed to show what a specific character is seeing.

Cutaway
: A shot that briefly interrupts the main action to show something else related to the scene.

Rule of Thirds
: A composition guideline that divides a frame into a 3x3 grid to place key elements along the lines or intersections.

Foreground
: The part of a composition that appears closest to the viewer.

Background
: The part of a composition that appears farthest from the viewer.

Depth of Field
: The range of a shot, front to back, that appears acceptably in focus.

Field of View
: The extent of a scene visible through a camera lens.
