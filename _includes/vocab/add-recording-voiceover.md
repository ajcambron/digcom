**Room Tone**
: The ambient sound of a specific recording space with no one talking. Recorded and kept for use
when smoothing edits later.

**Natural/Ambient Sound**
: The real sound of an environment, recorded to add realism and context to a scene or episode.

**Foley**
: Sound effects recreated and recorded in post-production to match on-screen or implied action.

**Levels**
: How loud or quiet an audio signal is, monitored while recording and during a mix.

**Gain**
: The setting that controls how much a microphone's signal is amplified before recording.

**Clipping**
: Audio distortion that happens when a signal's levels are pushed too high. A hard, harsh
digital error, not a stylistic effect.

**Sampling Rate**
: How many times per second an analog audio signal is measured/digitized. Higher sampling rates
capture more detail.

**Voice-Over Record**
: Recording a narration or host track separately from other audio, to be mixed in later.

**Scratch Track**
: A rough, temporary audio recording used as a placeholder during editing before a final version
is recorded.
