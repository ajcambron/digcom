Shot List
: A pre-production document that itemizes every shot needed for a production.

Scriptwriting
: The process of writing the dialogue, action, and structure of a video project.

Storyboarding
: Creating a sequence of drawings that visualize a video's shots before filming.

Edit List
: A document listing every clip, in and out point, and its place in the final edit.

Transcripts
: Written text versions of a video's spoken audio.
