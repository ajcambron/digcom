**Caption Track**
: A timeline track that holds subtitles or closed captions.

**Export**
: Saving the finished edit as a video file ready to play or upload.

**Label Colors**
: Colors assigned to clips in a bin or the timeline to keep them organized.

**Media Encoder**
: A separate Adobe app that queues and exports Premiere Pro sequences in the background.

**Patch Panel**
: The track controls at the left of the timeline that decide which tracks receive inserted or overwritten clips.

**Rolling Edit**
: Moving an edit point between two clips, so one gets longer and the other shorter by the same amount.

**Scratch Disk**
: The setting that decides where Premiere Pro stores previews and captured media.

**Sequence Preset**
: Saved sequence settings such as frame size, frame rate and codec.

**Sidecar File**
: A separate caption file (like .srt) delivered alongside a video, so viewers can turn captions on or off.

**Slide Edit**
: Moving a clip along the timeline while the clips on either side adjust, so the sequence length stays the same.

**Slip Edit**
: Changing which part of a clip plays without changing its length or position in the timeline.

**Sound Bridge**
: Audio that carries across a cut between two clips (a J-cut or L-cut).

**Text Panel**
: The panel that shows a transcript of the dialogue, so you can edit video by editing text.
