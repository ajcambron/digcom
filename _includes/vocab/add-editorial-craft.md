**Editorial Judgment**
: The set of decisions an editor makes that aren't dictated by the footage itself. which take,
which angle, how long to hold a shot, when to cut away. The difference between assembling clips
and actually editing.

**Continuity Editing**
: Cutting so that time, space, and action feel seamless and logical to a viewer, even though the
footage was shot out of order and from multiple angles.

**Invisible Edit**
: A cut a viewer doesn't consciously notice, because it follows the action or the eye naturally
(cutting on movement, matching an eyeline). The goal of most narrative editing.

**Cutting on Action**
: Making a cut in the middle of a movement (a door closing, a hand reaching) instead of before or
after it, so the motion itself hides the edit.

**Rhythm**
: The felt pace of a sequence created by shot length, the space between cuts, and how those
lengths change over time. Editing rhythm is closer to music than to writing.

**Tension**
: The sense of anticipation or unresolved energy a sequence builds, often through shortening shot
length, withholding information, or cutting away at the wrong (right) moment.
