**Enhance Speech**
: An Essential Sound tool that automatically improves dialogue clarity and reduces background
noise from field or podcast recordings.

**Loudness Auto-Match**
: A tool that automatically levels out inconsistent volume across multiple clips to a consistent
loudness target.

**Auto Ducking**
: Automatically lowering music volume whenever dialogue is present, then raising it back up when
dialogue stops.

**Repair**
: Essential Sound tools that remove specific audio problems—hum, reverb, background noise—without a full manual EQ pass.

**Remix**
: Automatically adjusting a music track's length and arrangement to fit a target duration.

**Conforming**
: Matching an audio file's format/sample rate to your project's settings so it plays back
correctly.

**Fade Handles**
: The draggable corner controls on an audio clip that create a fade in or fade out.

**Mono / Stereo Tracks**
: Mono audio is a single channel; stereo audio is two channels (left/right) that together create
a sense of space.

**Syncing Audio**
: Aligning a separately recorded audio track to match its corresponding video footage exactly.
