**White Balance**
: A camera or editing adjustment ensuring colors (especially white) render accurately under the
current light source.

**Color Correction**
: Adjusting recorded footage to shift colors back to an accurate, neutral baseline—fixing a
problem, not creating a look.

**Color Grading**
: Adjusting already-corrected footage to create an intentional look that enhances mood and tone—a creative choice, not a fix.

**Lumetri**
: Premiere Pro's built-in color tool, with Basic Correction (white balance, exposure), Creative
(style presets), and Curves/Color Wheels panels for grading.

**LUT (Look-Up Table)**
: A preset that remaps a shot's colors to a specific look—a fast starting point for grading,
usually refined further by hand.
