**Transparency Panel**
: The panel (Window > Transparency) that controls a selected object's Opacity and Blending Mode,
and is also where opacity masks are built by dragging a second object onto the mask thumbnail.

**Opacity Mask**
: A mask built in the Transparency panel where the *values* of a masking object (how light or
dark it is, not its color) control how transparent the artwork beneath it becomes — white areas
of the mask stay fully visible, black areas disappear completely, and gray areas partially fade.

**Blending Mode**
: A setting that changes how a selected object's colors mix with whatever is stacked beneath it
— for example, Multiply darkens by combining color values, while Screen lightens. The same
concept as blending modes in Photoshop.

**Clipping Mask**
: A mask made from a single vector shape (the topmost object in a selection) that crops
everything beneath it to that shape's outline. Unlike an opacity mask, a clipping mask only
knows "inside the shape" or "outside" — no partial transparency.
