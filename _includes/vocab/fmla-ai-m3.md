**Atmospheric Perspective**
: Showing depth by making faraway objects paler, less detailed and bluer than close ones.

**Blending Mode**
: A setting that controls how a layer's colors mix with the colors beneath it.

**Brush**
: A preset that applies a decorative style along a path, like grass or a painted stroke.

**Copyright**
: The creator's legal right to control how their work is copied, shared and used.

**Copyright Infringement**
: Using a copyrighted work without the owner's permission.

**Gradient**
: A gradual blend between colors. Illustrator has linear, radial and freeform gradients.

**Layer**
: A level in the Layers panel that holds artwork and controls its stacking order.

**Opacity**
: How see-through an object or layer is, from 0% (invisible) to 100% (solid).

**Pattern**
: A tile of artwork that repeats to fill a shape.

**Public Domain**
: Work that no one owns the copyright to, so anyone can use it.

**Sublayer**
: A layer nested inside another layer, used to organize related artwork.

**Symbol**
: A reusable piece of art stored in the Symbols panel. Every copy updates when the original changes.
