**Effect Preset**
: A saved combination of one or more effects with specific settings, applied in one click and
reusable across clips or projects.

**Adjustment Layer**
: A transparent layer that applies its effects to everything beneath it in the stack, without
altering the original clips directly.

**Nesting**
: Placing an entire sequence inside another sequence as a single clip. Useful for applying one
effect to a whole group of edited clips at once.

**Copy/Paste Attributes**
: Copying a clip's effects and settings, then pasting them onto other clips. A fast way to apply
the same look to many clips without rebuilding it each time.
