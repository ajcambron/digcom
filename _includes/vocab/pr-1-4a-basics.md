Frame Rate
: The number of individual frames displayed per second of video.

Frame Size
: The pixel dimensions (width by height) of a video frame.

Aspect Ratio
: The proportional relationship between a video's width and height.

Safe Areas
: The portion of a video frame guaranteed to be visible across all displays; important content should stay within them.

Video Codecs
: Software that encodes and decodes video during export and playback, such as H.264.

Pixels
: The smallest single units of color that make up a video frame's image.

Rendering
: The process of processing effects, transitions, and edits into a final viewable video.
