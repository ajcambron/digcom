**Shot List**
: A detailed, ordered list of every shot needed to complete a video, noting subject, framing, and
location — a production roadmap so nothing gets missed on shoot day.

**Scriptwriting**
: The process of writing what will be said and shown in a video, often organized in a
two-column format that separates visuals from audio/narration.

**Storyboarding**
: Sketching a video's key shots in sequence, panel by panel, to visually plan composition,
camera movement, and story flow before filming begins.

**Edit List**
: A written plan noting which clips go where, and in what order, in the finished edit — a bridge
between raw footage and the finished sequence in the editing software.

**Transcript**
: A written, word-for-word record of everything spoken in a video's audio — used for captioning,
translation, and reviewing dialogue before editing.
