**Video Essay**
: A visual argument told primarily through sequencing, natural sound, and music. Little to no
narration explaining what's on screen.

**Documentary**
: A nonfiction form built around interviews and voiceover (VO) guiding the viewer through real
events or subjects.

**News Package**
: A reporter-led nonfiction form (the Unit 3 form). A structured story with a reporter, sound
bites, and a clear angle.

**Music Video**
: A performance- or concept-driven piece cut to a track, where the visuals follow the music's
rhythm and mood rather than spoken narration.

**PSA (Public Service Announcement)**
: A short persuasive piece built around a cause, meant to change a viewer's belief or behavior.

**Commercial**
: A short persuasive piece built around a product, meant to make a viewer want to buy or use it.

**Narrative Short**
: A fictional story told through scripted scenes, characters, and dialogue (the Unit 7 form).
