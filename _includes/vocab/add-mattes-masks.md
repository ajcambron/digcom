**Matte**
: A shape or image used to define what part of a layer is visible or hidden.

**Mask**
: A path drawn directly on a clip to hide or reveal part of it—the hand-drawn version of a
matte.

**Track Matte**
: Using one clip's luminance or alpha channel to control the transparency of another clip layered
above or below it.

**Alpha Channel**
: The part of a pixel that stores transparency information, separate from its color (RGB) data.

**Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**
: A smaller video frame displayed within a larger one, commonly scaled and positioned using
keyframed transform properties.
