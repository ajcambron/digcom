**Pan / Tilt**
: A pan moves the camera left-right on its tripod head; a tilt moves it up-down. Both rotate the
camera without moving its position.

**Dolly / Truck**
: A dolly moves the whole camera forward or back; a truck moves it left or right — both reposition
the camera itself, not just its rotation.

**Pedestal**
: Raising or lowering the camera's height without changing its angle.

**Camera Angle**
: The vertical position of the camera relative to the subject — neutral (eye level), low (looking
up), high (looking down), or Dutch (tilted, for tension).

**Framing (Shot Size)**
: How much of the subject fills the frame — from an extreme close-up (ECU, part of the face) to an
extreme long/wide shot (subject small in the frame).

**Aperture**
: The camera's opening that controls how much light enters, measured in f-numbers — a lower
f-number lets in more light and creates shallower depth of field.

**Shutter Angle**
: The relationship between shutter speed and frame rate — 180° (paired with 24fps) is the standard
cinema look; larger angles blur motion more, smaller angles look crisper.

**Gain (ISO)**
: A camera setting that boosts sensitivity to light — useful in low light, but raising it too high
adds visible noise/grain.

**Depth of Field**
: How much of the shot (foreground to background) stays in sharp focus — controlled mainly by
aperture.
