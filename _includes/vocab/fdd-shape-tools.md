**Live Shape**
: A rectangle, rounded rectangle, ellipse, polygon, or star drawn with Illustrator's shape tools
that remains fully editable (exact dimensions, individual corner radii) until it's deliberately
expanded into a plain path.

**Bounding Box**
: The invisible rectangle marking an object's full width and height, used whenever the object is
scaled, rotated, or aligned.

**Corner Radius Widget**
: The small draggable control that appears at a Live Shape's corner, letting you round that
corner interactively without changing the underlying path.

**Polygon Tool**
: Draws a shape with straight sides, evenly spaced around a center point; tapping the Up/Down
arrow keys while dragging adds or removes sides.

**Star Tool**
: Draws a multi-pointed star shape; the same arrow-key trick adds or removes points, and holding
Option/Alt while dragging keeps the star's arms straight instead of curving inward.

**Isolation Mode**
: A temporary editing mode (entered by double-clicking a group or object) that dims everything
else on the artboard, letting you edit just that item without accidentally affecting the rest of
your artwork.
