**Appearance Panel**
: A panel (Window > Appearance) that lists every fill, stroke, and effect applied to a selected
object as a stack, in the order they're applied. Click any listed attribute to edit it, or drag
it to a new position in the stack to change the result.

**Eyedropper Tool**
: Samples the complete appearance of one object — fill color, stroke color and weight, and any
effects — and applies that whole look to another object with a single click.

**Recolor Artwork**
: A dialog (Edit > Recolor Artwork) that displays every color currently used in a selection as an
editable color group, letting you remap colors, reduce a design to fewer colors, or apply a
harmony rule to an entire illustration at once instead of editing each fill individually.
