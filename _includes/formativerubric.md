| Mark | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **✓+** (Check Plus) | Exceeds expectations. You did everything asked, and it shows real care or extra thinking. |
| **✓** (Check) | Meets expectations. You completed the task as assigned. |
| **✓−** (Check Minus) | Attempted, but missing key requirements or needs more effort. |

Most formatives (exit tickets, thumbnails, roughs, quick drills, and other daily classwork) are
graded this way. Fast, and focused on whether you did the thing, not a point-by-point score.
Auto-graded quizzes are the exception; those are scored automatically.
