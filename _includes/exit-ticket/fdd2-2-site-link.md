📤 **Exit Ticket: Post Your Portfolio Link.** In the discussion board below, post:

* the **published link** to your Google Sites portfolio (steps below), and
* a reflection in **at least 3 sentences (aim for 5)**: what you named your site, which theme you
  picked and why, and what you want your logo to say about you once it fills that empty slot.

*Stuck on how to start? Try: "I named my site ___ because ___. I picked the ___ theme because
___. When people see my logo, I want them to think ___."*

**How to publish your site and get its link**

**1. Click Publish.** It's the purple button in the top-right corner of the Google Sites editor.

{% include googlesites-menubar.html highlight="publish" %}

**2. Confirm it.** The first time, Google asks for a **web address**: type something simple, like
your first and last name, then click **Publish**. If you've published before, a **Review changes**
window opens instead; click **Publish** there too. Your changes aren't live until you do.

{% include googlesites-publish.html site_name="first-last-portfolio" %}

**3. Click the chain-link icon** (*Copy published site link*) in the top toolbar, just left of
Publish.

{% include googlesites-menubar.html highlight="link" %}

**4. Click Copy link.** Your site's public address is now copied.

{% include googlesites-copylink.html site_name="first-last-portfolio" %}

**5. Test it.** Open a new tab, paste the link, and press Return. You should see your site the way a
visitor does, with **no** editing sidebar and **no** Publish button. If you see the editor, you
copied the wrong link; go back to step 3.

**6. Paste the link** into the discussion board with your reflection.

⚠️ **Don't copy the link from the address bar.** While you're editing, the address bar shows the
*editor's* address (it ends in `/edit`), which only you can open. Your teacher will get an error.
