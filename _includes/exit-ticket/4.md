📤 **Exit Ticket: 3-2-1.** On a scrap of paper, write in **complete sentences**, not a list of
fragments: **3** sentences describing things you did or made today, **2** sentences naming
techniques or tools you used and how, and **1** sentence asking a question you still have.
That's 6 sentences total, well past the usual "at least 3, aim for 5."

*Stuck on how to start? Try: "Today I ___. I also ___. I used ___ to ___. I also used ___ to
___. I'm still wondering ___."*
