**Exit Ticket: Post Your Portfolio Link (FDD 2.2 only).** In the last few minutes, have students
post to the discussion board the **published** link to their Google Sites portfolio (copied with
the chain-link "Copy published site link" button, not the address bar) plus a reflection of at
least 3 sentences (aim for 5) on their site name, theme choice, and what they want their logo to
communicate. Click every link before grading: an editor link (ending in `/edit`) or an "access
denied" page means the site was never published or the wrong link was copied. Grade with the
check system (see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a working published link + an on-topic reflection
meeting the sentence count, ✓+ for a reflection that ties the site name or theme to a specific
choice they'll carry into the Geometric Logo project, ✓− for a link that doesn't open the
published site, or a one-line reflection. Keep the list of links: these are the sites S2's logo
and favicon get uploaded to.
