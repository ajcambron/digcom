**Exit Ticket 3 — Emoji Meter.** In the last 2 minutes, students draw one face (🙁/😐/🙂) rating
their confidence in today's skill, plus one word explaining why. Grade with the check system
(see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a face + a real word (not just "idk"), ✓+ if the word is
specific to today's skill, ✓− for a face with no word. Source: AVID's Emoji exit ticket
(avidopenaccess.org).
