**Exit Ticket 3: Emoji Meter.** In the last few minutes, students draw one face (🙁/😐/🙂) rating
their confidence in today's skill, then explain why in at least 3 sentences (aim for 5),
naming something specific from today rather than a general feeling. Grade with the check system
(see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a face + a real explanation meeting the sentence count, ✓+ if
the explanation names an exact moment or step from today, ✓− for a face with a one-word or vague
explanation (not just "idk"). Source: AVID's Emoji exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
