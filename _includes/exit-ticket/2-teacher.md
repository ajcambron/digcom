**Exit Ticket 2: Glow &amp; Grow.** In the last few minutes, students write one Glow (a success)
and one Grow (an improvement) about their own work today, in at least 3 sentences total (aim for
5), with real detail rather than a one-word answer. Grade with the check system (see
Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a specific Glow and Grow meeting the sentence count, ✓+ if the Grow
names a concrete next step, ✓− if either is vague ("it was good" / "nothing") or well short of 3
sentences. Skim for patterns. A Grow that shows up across many students flags something worth
revisiting next class. Source: AVID's Glow and Grow exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
