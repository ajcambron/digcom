**Exit Ticket 2 — Glow &amp; Grow.** In the last 2 minutes, students write one Glow (a success)
and one Grow (an improvement) about their own work today. Grade with the check system (see
Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a specific Glow and Grow, ✓+ if the Grow names a concrete next step,
✓− if either is vague ("it was good" / "nothing"). Skim for patterns — a Grow that shows up
across many students flags something worth revisiting next class. Source: AVID's Glow and Grow
exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
