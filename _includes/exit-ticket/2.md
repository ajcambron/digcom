📤 **Exit Ticket: Glow &amp; Grow.** On a scrap of paper, write about today's work in **at least 3
sentences (aim for 5)**: describe one **Glow** (something that went well) and one **Grow** (one
thing you'd do differently or improve next time), explaining each with real detail, not just a
phrase.

*Stuck on how to start? Try: "My Glow today was ___, because ___. My Grow is ___. Next time I'll
try ___."*
