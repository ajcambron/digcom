**Exit Ticket 4 — 3-2-1.** In the last 2-3 minutes, students write 3 things they did/made today,
2 techniques/tools they used, and 1 open question. The fullest of the 4 tickets — fitting for
the last formative lesson before the summative block. Skim the "1 question" column before the
project day starts; it often surfaces what to clarify before independent work time. Grade with
the check system (see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for all three parts filled in with real content,
✓+ if the question is specific enough to actually act on, ✓− if any part is missing or
generic. Source: AVID's 3-2-1 exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
