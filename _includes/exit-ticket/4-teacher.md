**Exit Ticket 4: 3-2-1.** In the last few minutes, students write 3 complete sentences on things
they did/made today, 2 complete sentences on techniques/tools they used, and 1 complete-sentence
question—6 sentences total, in full sentences rather than fragments. The fullest of the 4
tickets, fitting for the last formative lesson before the summative block. Skim the question
before the project day starts; it often surfaces what to clarify before independent work time.
Grade with the check system (see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for all three parts filled in with real
content as complete sentences, ✓+ if the question is specific enough to actually act on, ✓− if
any part is missing, generic, or written as a fragment instead of a sentence. Source: AVID's
3-2-1 exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
