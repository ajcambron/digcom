**Exit Ticket 1: Stoplight.** In the last few minutes, have students post to the discussion board
a G/Y/R (green/yellow/red) rating of their own confidence in today's skill, then explain the
rating in at least 3 sentences (aim for 5)—what specifically makes them feel that way, plus their
actual question if yellow or red. Not graded for correctness. There's no wrong feeling. Grade
with the check system (see Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a rating + a real, on-topic explanation
meeting the sentence count, ✓+ for a specific, thoughtful explanation that names an exact moment
or step from today, ✓− for a rating with a one-word or off-topic explanation, or a yellow/red
with no real question. Then use the results: any cluster of yellow/red tells you who to check in
with first thing next class. Source: AVID's Stoplight exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
