**Exit Ticket 1 — Stoplight.** In the last 2 minutes, have students write G/Y/R (green/yellow/
red) rating their own confidence in today's skill, with a one-sentence question if yellow or
red. Not graded for correctness — there's no wrong feeling. Grade with the check system (see
Resources > Rubrics): ✓ for a rating + honest question if yellow/red, ✓+ for a specific,
thoughtful question, ✓− for a rating with no question when one was needed. Then use the
results: any cluster of yellow/red tells you who to check in with first thing next class.
Source: AVID's Stoplight exit ticket (avidopenaccess.org).
