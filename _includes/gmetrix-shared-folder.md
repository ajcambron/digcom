Every Mac in this lab has one building called **Users**. Inside that building, each person gets
their own room, named with their student ID number. Next door to your room is a shared room called
**Shared** that everyone can walk into. The GMetrix files live there.

~~~
Macintosh HD
└── Users
    ├── 123456        ← your folder (your student ID)
    └── Shared
        └── GMetrix   ← the practice files
~~~

Your folder and **Shared** sit side by side inside **Users**. To get from your folder to GMetrix,
you go *up* one level to Users, then *down* into Shared, then into GMetrix.

### The fast way

1. Click on the desktop so Finder is active.
2. In the menu bar, choose **Go > Go to Folder…** (or press **Shift + Command + G**).
3. Type `/Users/Shared/GMetrix` and press **Return**.

### The click-through way

1. Open Finder and choose **Go > Computer**.
2. Open **Macintosh HD**, then **Users**, then **Shared**, then **GMetrix**.

### Keep it handy

Drag the GMetrix folder into the Finder sidebar under **Favorites**. Next time it's one click away.

### Important: you can look, but you can't change

GMetrix is **read-only**. You can open and copy files, but you can't save, rename, or delete
anything inside it. When an assignment asks you to work on a file, **copy it into your own folder
first** (`/Users/your ID number`), then open the copy.

{: .note }
If you try to save and get a "permission" error, that's the lock doing its job. Use **File > Save
As** and save into your own folder.
