# 7.2 | Purpose & Description

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-client-audience.md %}

Before you film a single shot, you need to be able to answer two questions in writing: why does
this project exist, and what will it actually include?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add7-purpose-description-reference" title="7.2 Guided Notes: Purpose & Description" presentation="/assets/presentations/add7-2.html" %}

A **purpose statement** answers why. the main goal or message, the audience you're trying to
reach, and why this topic or concept. A **description** answers what and how. a brief summary of
the storyline or structure, the key creative choices (camera angles, editing techniques, sound),
and the production process. Keep purpose to one short paragraph and description to one to three.

> "If someone read only your purpose statement and never saw the video, could they guess what
> it's actually about? If not, the purpose is too vague, too many projects say 'to make people
> think' without saying think about what, or for whom."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between a purpose statement and a description?"

> **Analysis:** "Why might a festival require both a purpose statement and a description instead
> of just a synopsis?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Your group's short film changes direction halfway through
> production. What in your purpose statement would need to change, and what would stay the
> same?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Draft your purpose statement** as a group: main goal/message, target audience, why this
 topic. One short paragraph.
2. **Draft your description** as a group: storyline/structure summary, key creative choices,
 production process. One to three paragraphs.
3. **Swap with another group.** Can they tell you the purpose and audience just from reading your
 description, without reading your purpose statement separately?
4. **Revise** based on that feedback.

{: .discussion }
Which was easier to write: the purpose (why) or the description (what/how)? Why do you think
that is?
