# 6.2 | Keyframes: Motion & Opacity

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-keyframes.md %}

A static title just appears and disappears. A keyframed title moves in, holds, and moves out —
and that difference is entirely about time, not new tools.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add6-keyframes-reference" title="6.2 Guided Notes: Keyframes: Motion & Opacity" presentation="/assets/presentations/add6-2.html" %}

> "Every keyframe animation is the same idea: set a value here, set a different value there,
> Premiere fills in everything in between. Position, opacity, scale — it's always the same
> mechanic underneath."

Walk through: setting **keyframes** in the Effect Controls panel, **temporal interpolation**
(speed over time) vs. **spatial interpolation** (path through space), **ease in/ease out**, and
animating **opacity** for fades and reveals.

**Scaffolded questions — cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between temporal and spatial interpolation?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does linear interpolation often look robotic compared to eased
> interpolation?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If you wanted a title to feel urgent and sudden vs. calm and gentle,
> how would your keyframe interpolation choices differ?"

## Guided Practice — Animate a Title (~23 min)

1. **Add a title graphic** to your sequence.
2. **Set 2 position keyframes** to animate it moving on screen.
3. **Set 2 opacity keyframes** so it fades in rather than hard-cutting into view.
4. **Apply ease in/ease out** to at least one keyframe pair and compare it to linear.
5. **Partner critique:** compare your eased vs. linear animation with a partner — describe the
   difference in one sentence each.

{: .highlight }
If your animation still looks robotic after adding ease in/out, check that you actually applied
it to BOTH keyframes in the pair, not just one.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** a classmate's title animates instantly from 0% to 100% opacity with no
in-between. What's missing from their keyframe setup?
