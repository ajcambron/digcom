# 6.4 | Mattes, Masks & Picture-in-Picture

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-mattes-masks.md %}

Today combines everything else this unit has built—keyframes, keying, and effects—into three
techniques you'll see in almost every reaction video, tutorial, and title sequence.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add6-mattes-masks-reference" title="6.4 Guided Notes: Mattes, Masks & Picture-in-Picture" presentation="/assets/presentations/add6-4.html" %}

> "A mask hides or reveals part of ONE layer. A track matte uses one layer to control a SECOND
> layer's visibility. Picture-in-picture just shows two videos at once, scaled and positioned.
> Three different tools, three different jobs."

Walk through: drawing a **mask**, building a **track matte** using one layer's luminance to reveal
another, understanding the **alpha channel**, and constructing a **picture-in-picture (PiP)**
layout.

**Scaffolded questions—cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between a mask and a track matte?"

> **Analysis:** "Why would you use a track matte instead of a simple cross-dissolve to reveal a
> new clip?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "How could you combine 6.3's keying skill with today's picture-in-picture
> technique in one shot?"

## Guided Practice—Build All Three (~23 min)

1. **Mask:** draw a mask on a clip to hide or reveal a specific part of the frame.
2. **Track matte:** stack a shape or text layer above a video clip and use it as a track matte to
   reveal the video through the shape.
3. **Picture-in-picture:** scale and position a second video clip into a corner of the frame, then
   **keyframe it to move** during the shot.
4. **Group teach-back:** in small groups, each member demos one technique to the rest of the group
   in under 60 seconds.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** you want text to reveal a video clip through its letters, animated. Which
technique from today does that, and what controls the reveal's shape?
