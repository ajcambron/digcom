# S | 6 | Title Sequence / Effects Reel

Today you build one final piece that proves every Unit 6 skill together: effect presets and
adjustment layers, keyframed motion, keying, and mattes/masks/picture-in-picture.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
Choose your format: a **title sequence** (an animated opening for a real or fictional project) or
an **effects reel** (a short demo showcasing your compositing skills). Both need the same 4
techniques. Just applied differently.

Review the required-technique checklist together before groups/individuals begin:

- [ ] An effect applied via an adjustment layer or copy/paste attributes (6.1)
- [ ] Keyframed motion and/or opacity, with eased interpolation (6.2)
- [ ] A keyed (green screen) composite (6.3)
- [ ] A mask, track matte, or picture-in-picture layout (6.4)

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

1. **Plan your sequence**: sketch or list the order your 4 required techniques will appear.
2. **Build each technique**, checking it off the list as you go.

4. **Apply your Revision Plan**, prioritizing critical fixes first.
5. **Write a one-sentence technique breakdown** identifying where each required technique appears.
