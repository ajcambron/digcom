# Unit 6 | Effects, Keyframes & Compositing

## Introduction

A Premiere-native replacement for the old After Effects unit — applying and reusing effects,
animating properties with keyframes, keying out a green screen, and combining mattes, masks, and
picture-in-picture — building toward a polished title sequence or effects reel.

{% include unit-video.html %}

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Apply effects via presets, adjustment layers, and copy/paste attributes
- Animate position and opacity using keyframes with eased interpolation
- Key out a green screen and composite over a new background
- Build masks, track mattes, and a keyframed picture-in-picture layout
