# 11.3 | Lower Thirds & Titles

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-after-effects-basics.md %}

A lower third has about three seconds to tell a viewer who's talking. Every design and animation
choice here serves that one job: fast, legible, gone before it overstays its welcome.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add11-lower-thirds-reference" title="11.3 Guided Notes: Lower Thirds & Titles" presentation="/assets/presentations/add11-3.html" %}

A **lower third** sits in the bottom third of frame: a background bar plus a name and title,
positioned inside safe margins so it survives a crop or reframe. Animate it in with a quick,
eased motion (a slide, a fade, a wipe), hold it long enough to actually read, then animate it out
the same way. A **title card** uses the same visual language (color, font, motion style) for a
full-frame opener instead of an overlay.

> "If a viewer can't finish reading the name before it animates off, the lower third failed at
> its one job. Design for reading speed, not for how good the animation looks in isolation."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What two pieces of information does a standard lower third usually show?"

> **Analysis:** "Why do safe margins matter more for a lower third than for most other motion
> graphics?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Your interview subject's name is unusually long. What design or
> animation choice would you make to keep the lower third legible?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Design a lower third** (background bar, name, title) for a hypothetical interview subject.
2. **Check it against safe margins.**
3. **Animate it in** with eased keyframes (slide, fade, or wipe).
4. **Animate it out**, matching the in-animation's style.
5. **Turn and Talk:** critique a partner's lower third for legibility and hold time.

{: .discussion }
How much on-screen time does a lower third actually need to be readable, and how did that
constraint shape your design?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Design and animate a matching title card, full-frame, same color/font/motion style as your lower
third, so the two feel like one cohesive graphics package.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~5 min)

Save your file, then screen-record or export your animated lower third.
