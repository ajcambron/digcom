# 11.4 | Building a Graphics Package

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-after-effects-basics.md %}

Three separate pieces don't make a package until they're assembled with consistent timing and
actually rendered out as one file.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add11-graphics-package-reference" title="11.4 Guided Notes: Building a Graphics Package" presentation="/assets/presentations/add11-4.html" %}

Build a **master composition** and **precompose** each of your existing elements (the Suns and
Moons loop, the lower third, the title card) into it, then order them in time so they don't
awkwardly overlap. When it's ready, add the master comp to the **Render Queue**: choose your
render and output module settings, then render, After Effects' equivalent of Premiere's Media
Encoder.

> "A graphics package isn't just three good pieces, it's three pieces that feel like they came
> from the same designer. Consistent color, consistent motion style, consistent timing."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does precomposing let you do with multiple existing elements?"

> **Analysis:** "Why might two graphics elements that look great individually feel disconnected
> when played back to back?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "You're 10 seconds over your package's target runtime. Which element
> would you trim first, and why?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Create a master composition.**
2. **Precompose and import** your Suns and Moons loop (11.2), lower third, and title card (11.3)
 as layers.
3. **Order them in time** so each element plays cleanly without overlapping the next.
4. **Small-group review:** flag any element in a classmate's timing that overlaps awkwardly.

{: .discussion }
What changed about each individual element once it had to work alongside the others?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Refine your master comp's timing based on today's feedback, then add it to the **Render Queue**
and export your finished graphics package.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~5 min)

Save your file and submit your rendered export.
