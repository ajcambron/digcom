# Unit 11 | Motion Graphics (After Effects)

## Introduction

Unit 11 is the first of ADD's two Extra Units, post-certification enrichment that introduces
Adobe After Effects, keyframe animation, and motion graphics: the animated intro, lower thirds,
and titles that give a finished video its polish.

{: .highlight }
Nothing in this unit is ACP-tested (the Premiere Pro exam doesn't cover After Effects). The unit
still carries full compliance rigor. 78-minute timing, The 7, a real Differentiation Matrix, real
Organize criteria. The students are still in the room, even though the exam is behind them.

{% include unit-video.html %}

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Animate a layer's position, opacity, and scale using keyframes with eased interpolation
- Build a cyclical animation (Suns and Moons) demonstrating looping motion and easing
- Design an animated lower third and title graphic
- Composite a small motion-graphics package and render it through the Render Queue
