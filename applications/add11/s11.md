# S | 11 | Motion Graphics Package

Today you assemble and finish what 11.1 through 11.4 built: an animated intro, a lower third, and
a title card, one consistent package.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
This isn't a new build. Review your requirements together: all three elements need to share a
color palette, font, and motion style, so the package reads as one designer's work, not three
separate exercises stapled together.

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

1. **Review each element** (intro loop, lower third, title card) and adjust colors, fonts, or
 timing so all three feel consistent.
2. **Refine the weakest element** first, the one that feels least connected to the others.
3. **Plus/Delta peer review** on the set of three elements together:

{% include peerreview.md %}

{: .discussion }
Which single design choice, color, font, or motion style, did the most to make your package feel
cohesive?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Assemble your final master composition (11.4) and add it to the **Render Queue**.

## Deliver, Export & Submit (~5 min)

Confirm your render completed successfully, then submit it to your portfolio with a one-sentence
description of its intended use.
