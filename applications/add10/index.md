# Unit 10 | ACP Review, Practice Exams & Certification (Apr 20 – May 3, 2027)

{: .highlight }
Unit 10 introduces **no new content**. Every ACP-tested objective for ADD (Premiere Pro) was
already covered across Units 1–9 (Sep 1, 2026 – Apr 16, 2027). This unit's entire job is to get
every student from "has seen it once" to "certifies on it": targeted review, full-length
practice exams, and the actual Adobe Certified Professional exam itself.

{% include unit-video.html %}

## Why this page doesn't follow the standard lesson schema

Every other unit in this course (`lesson:` front matter, `X_Y.md` / `X_Y-teacher.md` pairs, four
formative lessons cycling the Exit Ticket Bank, a summative block) is built around teaching and
assessing **new** content. Unit 10 has no new SWBAT, no new `_includes/vocab/` terms, and nothing
that fits the check/+/− formative system. Same call FDD made for its own Unit 10, for consistency
across both courses.

## Calendar context

Runs **Apr 20 – May 3, 2027, 10 school days**, MP4, the same window FDD's Unit 10 uses, the ACP
certification window has to land between Access Testing and AP Testing regardless of course.

## The 10 meetings

| Day | Focus | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Diagnostic** | Short diagnostic quiz pulled from the full-year ACP vocab/question banks, covering all domains. Review each student's own F1–F4 formative history from Units 1–9 | Identifies each student's weakest domain(s) before spending review time |
| 2 | **Domain 1 & 2 Review**: Project Setup, Organization | Station rotation: a quick re-teach mini-lesson per domain + targeted GMetrix/BrainBuffet module practice filtered to that domain | Pull from Units 1, 2, 8 |
| 3 | **Domain 3 Review**: Ingest & Editing Fundamentals | Station rotation, same format | Pull from Units 2, 3, 8 |
| 4 | **Domain 4 Review**: Effects, Color & Audio | Station rotation, same format. Budget extra 1:1 time here, ADD's largest, most-tested domain | Pull from Units 4, 5, 6, 8 |
| 5 | **Domain 5 Review**: Output & Delivery | Station rotation, same format | Pull from Unit 9 |
| 6 | **Full-Length Practice Exam #1** | Timed, full-exam-conditions GMetrix/BrainBuffet practice exam covering all domains | Save the domain-by-domain score breakdown |
| 7 | **Targeted Re-Review** | Small-group re-teach built directly from Practice Exam #1's weakest domains per student/class | Group by this week's data, not the Day 1 diagnostic |
| 8 | **Full-Length Practice Exam #2 + Final Study Plans** | Second timed practice exam. Each student leaves with a one-page personal "what to double-check" list | Last day before the real exam. Keep this low-stress |
| 9 | **ACP Certification Exam** | The actual proctored Adobe Certified Professional exam | Follow district testing-security procedures. Not a classroom-graded day |
| 10 | **Makeup Testing / Buffer + Unit 11 Preview** | Makeup slot for any student who missed Day 9. For everyone else, a short reflection and a preview of Units 11–12's post-certification project work | Real testing logistics make a buffer day necessary, not optional |

## How to use each day's data

- **Day 1's diagnostic** and each student's Unit 1–9 formative history (F1–F4 check marks in
  Schoology) together tell you who needs which domain.
- **GMetrix/BrainBuffet's own domain-tagged reporting** should drive which specific modules get
  assigned on Days 2–5 and which get re-assigned after Practice Exam #1 (Day 7).
- **Practice Exam #1 and #2 scores** are the real signal for Day 7–8 grouping.

## Assessment & grading

Unit 10 has no F1–F6 formatives and no summative. What still needs a grade in the gradebook:

- **Completion/participation** for the review stations (Days 2–5) and both practice exams.
- **Growth**, if your gradebook supports it: Day 1 diagnostic domain score → Practice Exam #1 →
  Practice Exam #2.
- **The certification exam result itself** is a reporting/record-keeping item, not a classroom
  grade. Check current district policy before entering it as one.

## Differentiation notes (general, not a per-lesson matrix)

- **IEP/504:** extended time on both practice exams and the certification exam (confirm each
  student's accommodation plan covers Certiport, not just in-class practice). Chunk each domain's
  review station into smaller sub-checklists.
- **ELL/Multilingual:** GMetrix/BrainBuffet's own glossary/definition features during practice
  where available. Confirm Certiport's actual accommodation policy for language supports before
  test day.
- **Advanced/GT:** students who diagnose as strong across all domains by Day 1 can move into early
  independent full-exam attempts and peer-tutor a station they've already mastered.

## Materials

- GMetrix/BrainBuffet access for every student
- Each student's Schoology formative history (F1–F4 records) from Units 1–9
- District ACP/Certiport testing-day logistics. Coordinate with the testing coordinator before
  Day 9
- Printed or digital domain-breakdown reports from GMetrix/BrainBuffet for Days 1, 6, and 8
