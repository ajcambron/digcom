# S | 2 | Barbershop Social Promo

The culmination of this unit: using everything from 2.2–2.4, you'll finish the **Barbershop
Social Promo**, the Premiere Pro Module 1 project you started in GMetrix/BrainBuffet. It's a
short promotional video for a barbershop's social media: a main promo with a scripted voice-over,
plus a vertical/square version for phone screens.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Watch the teacher show the finished promo and walk through the deliverables checklist below,
then open your own copy of the project.

{: .highlight }
The project's footage and audio live in **`/Users/Shared/GMetrix`** on every lab Mac (Finder →
**Go > Go to Folder…**, or **Shift + Command + G**). That folder is read-only. Copy the project
file you're working on into your own project folder and work on the copy. The footage can stay
where it is. Lost? See [2.1, Finding the GMetrix Folder](2_1.md#finding-the-gmetrix-folder).

{% include organizevideo.md %}

## Project Work Time (~53 min)

Work through Module 1's video lessons in order, starting wherever you left off. Pause the video
and do each step in your own project before moving on.

### Deliverables checklist

- [ ] **Main promo**, edited from the provided footage, with a clean rough-cut-to-final
  structure (2.3's In/Out points, Insert/Overwrite, trimming).
- [ ] **Scripted voice-over**, recorded and placed under the picture, with audio levels that
  let it read clearly over music.
- [ ] **Vertical/square version** made with **Auto Reframe**, checked so nothing important gets
  cropped out of frame.
- [ ] **Exports** of each version, with settings that match where they'll be posted (2.4's frame
  rate, resolution and codec).
- [ ] **Premiere project file** saved in your own project folder and backed up to Google Drive.

{: .discussion }
**Halfway check-in:** once your rough cut is in place, trade seats with a partner and watch their
promo with no explanation. In the first few seconds, can you tell what's being promoted? Flag
anything confusing before they move on.
