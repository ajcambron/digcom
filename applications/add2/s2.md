# S | 2 | Television Scene Edit

The culmination of this unit: using everything from 2.1–2.4, you'll edit a real scene from a
television show — from raw footage and a script, through three deliberate editing passes, to a
finished, delivered export.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Watch the teacher demo one Assembly Edit pass on a sample scene, narrating each choice out loud:
which clips get Inserted vs. Overwritten, and why this pass isn't trying to be perfect yet.

1. **Gather your resources:**
   - Script
   - Footage
   - Graphics
   - Sound effects

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

### Assembly Edit

1. Import your footage into your bins, following 2.1's bin structure.
2. Use your script to create an offline edit of the scene, using Insert and Overwrite from 2.2.
3. Maintain the original spirit of the scene — stay as true to the original vision as possible.
4. Don't worry about being frame-accurate yet. The goal is a sequence that tells the story.

### Offline Edit

1. Fine-tune the edit to clean up transitions and pacing, applying 2.3's L/J cuts and cutaways
   where they genuinely help the scene flow.
2. Bring in music, sound effects, and audio to bring the story to life.

### Online Edit

1. Perfect your cuts, checking for accidental jump cuts.
2. Mix audio and apply filters/effects.
3. Adjust color correction to balance the look between shots.
4. Add graphics and titles.
5. Crossfade all audio stems.
6. Fade in and out to black.

{: .discussion }
**Halfway check-in:** trade seats with a partner after your Assembly Edit pass. Does their scene
still tell the same story the script describes? Flag anything confusing before they move on.
