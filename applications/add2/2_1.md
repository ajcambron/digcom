# 2.1 | GMetrix Setup & ACP Pre-Test

Today is different from every other day this year. No new Premiere content, no worksheet. The
entire period is about getting you set up in the platform you'll use for the rest of the course,
and finding out where you're starting from.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Before a driving instructor lets you behind the wheel for real lessons, they usually watch you
drive once first, without teaching you anything yet. Why would they do that instead of just
starting to teach? What does that first drive tell them that a first lesson couldn't?

## GMetrix Account Setup (~15 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="gmetrix-pretest-steps-add" title="GMetrix Setup & ACP Pre-Test Steps" presentation="/assets/presentations/add2-1.html" %}

> "Every ACP course this year runs alongside GMetrix/BrainBuffet. Today's the day we get you
> actually into it."

1. **Open Schoology** and find today's GMetrix/BrainBuffet classroom join code, posted in the
 course materials.
2. **Go to the GMetrix/BrainBuffet site** (link posted alongside the code) and create an account
 using your **school email address**. Not a personal one.
3. **Enter the classroom code** to join our class. If it doesn't work, double-check for typos
 before asking. Codes are case-sensitive.

{: .highlight }
If your school-email verification email doesn't arrive, flag the teacher right away instead of
troubleshooting alone. First-day sign-up snags are common and worth solving live, not something
to quietly sit stuck on.

## ACP Pre-Test (~40 min)

1. **Once you're in,** find the **ACP Pre-Test** (sometimes labeled "Pre-Assessment") and start
 it. This is separate from Module 1's regular lessons.
2. **Work quietly and independently.** This isn't graded on correctness, and it isn't something
 you've been taught yet, so guessing is expected on plenty of questions. Answer everything you
 can reason through, and don't stress over the rest.
3. **Finish the whole pre-test** before moving on to anything else. Don't start Module 1 today.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check, once most students finish:** what's one question you genuinely had no idea
about? That's completely fine to say out loud. That's exactly what this test is for.

## Post Results & Reflect (~8 min)

1. **Find your results/score screen** once the pre-test submits.
2. **Take a screenshot** showing your score clearly.
3. **Save it to your Digital Design Drive folder**, in the Screenshots folder.

{: .highlight }
A low score today is not a bad grade. It's the whole point. Module 1 exists to teach you
everything this pre-test just asked about.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~5 min)

{: .reflection }
Which part of the pre-test felt hardest, or most unfamiliar? No wrong answer here, just be honest.
