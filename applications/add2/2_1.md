# 2.1 | GMetrix Setup & ACP Pre-Test

Today is different from every other day this year. No new Premiere content. The entire period
is about getting you into the platform you'll use for the rest of the course, and finding out
where you're starting from.

## GMetrix SMS Login (~15 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="gmetrix-pretest-steps-add" title="GMetrix SMS: Launch the Premiere Pro Practice Exam" presentation="/assets/presentations/add2-1.html" %}

> "Every ACP course this year runs alongside GMetrix/BrainBuffet. Today's the day we get you
> actually into it."

1. **Open GMetrix SMS** from the Applications folder (**GmetrixSMSe**).
2. **Click Login with Google** and sign in with your **school Google account**. Don't create a
 new account, and don't use a personal Google account.
3. **Find our class code on Schoology:** open our class's Schoology homepage and scroll down to
 **How to Contact Me**. The code is on the **Gmetrix:** line.
4. **Redeem the code:** in GMetrix SMS, click **Redeem Code** in the left sidebar, type the code
 exactly (including the dash), and click **Redeem Code**. You only do this once.

{: .highlight }
If Login with Google won't take your school account, flag the teacher right away instead of
troubleshooting alone. First-day login snags are common and worth solving live.

## Pre-Launch Setup (~10 min)

Do these steps **every time you log on to a computer**, not just today. They get Premiere Pro and
GMetrix's plugin ready so the exam actually opens (handout, Part 3).

{: .important }
**Be patient.** Steps 4, 5, and 6 each ask for an **administrator password**. Raise your hand and
wait: your teacher has to type it on every computer in the room, one at a time. Don't click
Cancel, and don't try to guess a password.

1. **Open Premiere Pro in Rosetta:** open the **Creative Cloud** app, go to **Apps → Installed
 apps**, click **••• (More actions)** next to Premiere Pro's **Open** button, and choose **Open in
 Rosetta**.
2. **Confirm the GMetrix extension loaded:** in Premiere Pro, go to **Window → Extensions** and check that GMetrix is listed. If it isn't, tell your teacher.
3. **Quit Premiere Pro** (**⌘ Command + Q**).
4. **Delete the test resource archive:** in GMetrix SMS, open **Settings**, click the **Tasks**
 tab, and click **Execute** next to **Delete Test Resource Archive**.

   ![GMetrix SMS Tasks tab: Delete Test Resource Archive, with an Execute button]({{ '/assets/images/fdd2/gmetrix-tasks-delete-archive.png' | relative_url }})

5. **Install the Premiere Pro plugin:** click the **Plugins** tab, choose **Premiere Pro** in
 the left list, and click **Execute** next to **Install Premiere Pro Plugin**.
6. **Reinstall the workspace files:** on the same page, click **Execute** next to **Reinstall
 Premiere Pro Plugin Workspace Files**.

   ![GMetrix SMS Plugins tab, shown with Illustrator selected: Premiere Pro is in the same left-hand list, and its page has the same Install Plugin and Reinstall Plugin Workspace Files rows, each with an Execute button]({{ '/assets/images/fdd2/gmetrix-plugins-illustrator.png' | relative_url }})

   *This screenshot shows Illustrator selected. Click **Premiere Pro** in the same list; its rows
   work the same way.*

can find on your own: **`/Users/Shared/GMetrix`**.

## ACP Pre-Test (~35 min)

1. **Launch the Premiere Pro practice exam** from GMetrix SMS in Testing Mode (handout, Part 4).
 GMetrix opens Premiere Pro for you. This is separate from Module 1's regular lessons.
2. **Work quietly and independently.** This isn't graded on correctness, and it isn't something
 you've been taught yet, so guessing is expected on plenty of questions. Answer everything you
 can reason through, and don't stress over the rest.
3. **Finish the whole pre-test** before moving on to anything else. Don't start Module 1 today.

{: .note }
Premiere sometimes looks frozen right at the start of a question while it converts that
question's sample project files. That conversion doesn't count against the test timer, so let it
finish instead of clicking around.

{: .highlight }
If the exam doesn't open properly (Premiere Pro opens but the GMetrix panel never shows up, or the
exam won't start), quit Premiere Pro (⌘Q), redo **Pre-Launch Setup** from step 1, then start the
exam again from GMetrix SMS.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check, once most students finish:** what's one question you genuinely had no idea
about? That's completely fine to say out loud. That's exactly what this test is for.

## Post Results & Reflect (~8 min)

1. **Find your results/score screen** once the pre-test submits.
2. **Take a screenshot** showing your score clearly: press **⌘ Command + Shift + 3** to capture
 the whole screen. It saves to your Desktop.
3. **Move it to your Digital Design Drive folder**, in the Screenshots folder.

{: .highlight }
A low score today is not a bad grade. It's the whole point. Module 1 exists to teach you
everything this pre-test just asked about.
