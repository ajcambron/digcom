# 1.3 | Two-Column Script

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-preproduction.md %}

For this lesson, you'll draft a **two-column script** to plan your explainer video shoot — one
column for what's on screen, one column for what's heard.

## Part 1 — Guided Notes: Preproduction Paperwork (~12 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add1-guided-notes-1-3" title="1.3 Guided Notes: Preproduction Paperwork" presentation="placeholder" %}

{: .discussion }
Reference your Design Brief from 1.2 — which of its criteria and constraints does your script
need to satisfy?

## Part 2 — Two-Column Script Draft (~48 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="two-column-script-worksheet" title="Two-Column Script" %}

1. **Draft (~40 min):** row by row, write what's happening on screen (Video) and what's being
   said or heard (Audio) at the same time. Your first row should be your **intro**; your last row
   should be your **outro** — both are required by your Design Brief's criteria.
2. **Partner read-through (~8 min):** trade scripts with a neighbor. They read your Audio column
   aloud while you follow along on the Video column — does everything you planned to show still
   make sense with what's being said?

{: .highlight }
Want a digital copy to revise or share later? An optional [Two-Column Script Template](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wpLAEK3re_8oUa8XXz6c9KWsSztMGiQUc3LhVCl0LI8/copy) is
available once you have reliable computer access — not required for today.

## Wrap-Up (~8 min)

{: .reflection }
What almost got missed in your script until your partner's read-through caught it?
