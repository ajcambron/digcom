# S | 1 | Explainer Video

{: .highlight }
This is the one part of Unit 1 that touches a device — you're in the computer lab today, with
your phone. Bring your Design Brief, Two-Column Script, and labeled Shot List from 1.2–1.4.

Using the pre-production materials you've prepared this unit, you're now tasked with shooting
and editing your explainer video.

## Plan

1. Gather everything you need to make your shoot a success.
2. Begin shooting early enough that you'll have time in class to edit and ask questions.

{% include camerasetup.md %}

{% include organizevideo.md %}

## Shoot (~40 min)

1. Work your way through the shots on your labeled Shot List from 1.4.
2. Try to work efficiently — shoot all the shots that take place in the same location at the
   same time.
3. Give your clips pre- and post-roll so you have room to cut before and after the action.
4. Match each shot to the shot type you planned — if a CU isn't working, that's fine, just note
   the change so your edit still matches your script.

## Edit (~10 min)

1. Import your footage into your web-based editor of choice:
   1. [Google Vids](https://docs.google.com/videos){:target="_blank"}
   2. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/video-editor/){:target="_blank"}
   3. [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/create/video){:target="_blank"}
2. Edit the video in a logical order that begins with an introduction and ends with a conclusion,
   following your two-column script.
3. Add transitions and effects to ease the flow of the content and keep your audience engaged.
