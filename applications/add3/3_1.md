# 3.1 | Plan

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-eng-roles.md %}

Every real news package starts the same way ENG (Electronic News Gathering) crews have worked for
decades: a small team, clear roles, and a plan before anyone picks up a camera.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add3-eng-roles-reference" title="3.1 Guided Notes: ENG Roles & Planning" presentation="/assets/presentations/add3-1.html" %}

> "A two-person ENG crew can cover a real news story because each role is clearly defined—the
> reporter drives the story and talks to people, the photojournalist runs the camera and audio.
> On a bigger crew, a producer and engineer join in to manage logistics and equipment."

Walk through each role's job on a real ENG crew, then the ENG Report Planner's sections: story
angle, interview subject(s), and the shots/interviews the story will need.

**Scaffolded questions—cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between what a reporter does and what a photojournalist
> does?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does a news crew plan its story angle before shooting, instead of just
> 'seeing what happens' on camera?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If your group only had 2 people instead of 4, which roles would you
> combine, and why?"

## Guided Practice—Team Planning (~23 min)

1. **Form your group** (3-4 members).
2. **Review the ENG crew roles** and assign one to each group member—try to match people to the
   role that fits their strengths.
3. **[Create your copy of the ENG Report Planner](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_B_BntemrsL8EM_Dfg_TxZKm-ym3iYI2msegBPQKXc8/copy)**
   and share it with your group.
4. **Complete the planner together:** story angle, interview subject(s), and the shots your story
   will need.
5. **Each member submits the document on Schoology** individually to receive credit.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** your group has 3 people but the plan calls for 4 ENG roles. What do you do?
