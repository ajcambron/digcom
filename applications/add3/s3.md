# S | 3 | News Story

Today you finish what 3.1–3.4 built: a real news story, from planning through a captioned, polished
final export—with one structured round of peer feedback in between.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
Before you review anyone's work, review the process. Peer review only works if feedback is
**specific, objective, and kind**—"I didn't like the ending" doesn't help anyone revise.

Review the Plus/Delta process together as a class before groups begin.

### Finish & Polish

1. **Apply your Revision Plan**, prioritizing Level 1 (critical/technical) fixes first, then
   Level 2 (pacing/clarity), then Level 3 (polish).
2. **Double-check your captions** are proofread and accurate after any edit changes.
3. **Confirm both deliverables are ready:** the burned-in captioned export and the sidecar
   caption file.

{: .discussion }
Which single piece of peer feedback changed your edit the most, and why?
