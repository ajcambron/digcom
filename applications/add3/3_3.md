# 3.3 | Edit & Text-Based Editing

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-eng-editing.md %}

Professional editors rarely scrub through raw footage by eye first. They read the transcript,
find what to keep, and cut from there. Today you'll edit the same way.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add3-editing-reference" title="3.3 Guided Notes: Edit & Text-Based Editing" presentation="/assets/presentations/add3-3.html" %}

> "An auto-generated transcript turns your footage into searchable text. You can spot every
> 'um,' every dead pause, every place the story rambles, just by reading, instead of scrubbing
> back and forth for twenty minutes."

Walk through: **A-roll** (interviews/stand-ups) vs. **B-roll** (supporting footage), generating a
transcript and marking **filler words** for removal, layering B-roll over VO, and adding a
**lower third** graphic to identify an interview subject.

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between A-roll and B-roll?"

> **Analysis:** "Why is reading a transcript usually faster than scrubbing through raw footage to
> find the best sound bite?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "A transcript can show you filler words, but what kind of editing
> problem *can't* a transcript catch on its own?"

## Guided Practice | Edit Your Story (~23 min)

1. **Import your footage** from 3.2 into your Adobe Express project.
2. **Generate a transcript** of your interview footage and mark filler words for removal.
3. **Assemble your timeline:** A-roll interview clips, with B-roll layered on top over VO where
 it helps tell the story.
4. **Add a lower third** identifying your interview subject's name and title.
5. **Partner check:** trade transcripts with a partner and confirm each other's cut list before
 committing the edits.

{: .highlight }
Cutting every single "um" can make a voice sound unnaturally clipped. Leave natural pauses that
help the story breathe, and only cut what genuinely distracts.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** your interview subject gives a great answer, but pauses for 4 full seconds
partway through to think. Does the transcript alone tell you what to do with that pause?
