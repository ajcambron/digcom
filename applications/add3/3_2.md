# 3.2 | Make

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-eng-shooting.md %}

Today you shoot what your ENG Report Planner called for. And the difference between amateur and
professional footage almost always comes down to a few consistent habits, not fancy equipment.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add3-shooting-reference" title="3.2 Guided Notes: Shooting for News" presentation="/assets/presentations/add3-2.html" %}

> "Camera at eye-level, subject filling most of the frame in a medium close-up, and just enough
> headroom. Not too much, not too little. These three habits alone separate a professional-looking
> shot from an amateur one."

Walk through: tripod stability, camera **eye-level** with the subject, **medium close-up** framing,
correct **headroom**, and checking **white balance** and **focus** before rolling.

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What does 'headroom' mean, and what happens if there's too much of it?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does a shaky, handheld shot undercut a news story's credibility, even if the
> content is accurate?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If you were only allowed 2 of these 4 checks before rolling. Tripod,
> eye-level, white balance, focus. Which 2 would you never skip, and why?"

## Guided Practice | Shoot Practice Footage (~23 min)

1. **Set up your tripod** at your subject's eye-level.
2. **Frame a medium close-up** with correct headroom. Subject filling most of the frame.
3. **Check white balance and focus** before recording.
4. **Rotate roles** within your group: camera operator, interview subject, director. Everyone
 gets a turn framing a shot.

{% include organizevideo.md %}

5. **Share your project folder** with your group members.
6. **Log into Adobe Express**, create a new video project, and import your footage from Drive.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** you review your footage and the subject's eyes are right at the very top of
the frame with almost no headroom. What would you fix before the real shoot?
