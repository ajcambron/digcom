A brilliant edit in a project nobody else (including future you) can navigate isn't actually
finished. Today's about the unglamorous habits that make a project reusable.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="guided-notes-8-4" title="8.4 Guided Notes: Asset Management & Submission" presentation="/assets/presentations/add8-4.html" %}

**Bins** organize footage, audio, and graphics the way physical trim bins organized film reels.
**Labeling clips** with color adds a fast visual scan (all B-roll one color, all interviews
another). A consistent **naming convention** (`LastName_FirstName_Project_v2.mp4`) means anyone
can identify a file without opening it. **Offline media** shows up as a red placeholder when
Premiere can't find a moved or renamed file, **relinking** manually points it back to the right
location.

When a project's finished, **Collect Files** (part of Premiere's Project Manager) gathers only
the clips a sequence actually uses into a new folder, then **consolidate and transcode** converts
everything to one standard format, an **archive** built to reopen cleanly a year from now.

> "If you can't find a specific clip in your own project within 30 seconds, neither can anyone
> you hand it to. That's not a minor inconvenience, on a real production it's a missed deadline."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between Collect Files and simply copying the whole project
> folder?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does a consistent naming convention matter more on a team project than a
> solo one?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "You open a project a year from now with no bins, no labels, and three
> offline clips. What's your first move?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Open the provided messy project**: unorganized bins, at least one offline clip.
2. **Build a bin structure** and a naming convention, then sort every asset into it.
3. **Resolve the offline clip** by relinking it to its correct file.
4. **Archive the project** using Project Manager's Collect Files/consolidate and transcode.
5. **Partner check:** trade projects. Can your partner find a specific clip you name within 30
 seconds?
