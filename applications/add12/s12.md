# S | 12 | Production Book & Portfolio Capstone

This is the last deliverable of the year: a complete, bound record of how your Short Film actually
got made, and a finished, public portfolio showing everything you built.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
Nothing new gets created today. Every section of the Production Book pulls from work you've
already done, this is assembly and finishing, not a new assignment.

Review the required sections together:

- **Contacts**: your group's contact information (real or fictional)
- **Pre-Production**: your script, storyboards, and purpose/description statement (7.1, 7.2)
- **Art**: your continuity photos and visual reference board (12.1)
- **Rights & Clearances**: your compiled attribution and release record (12.2)
- **Production Plan**: your call sheets and shooting schedule (7.3)
- **Post-Production**: your export/delivery log and shot list (Unit 9)
- **Deliverables**: where the final film lives, and confirmation it was submitted
- **Self-Evaluation**: each group member's own rubric

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

1. **Assemble each section** in order, pulling directly from prior units' work.
2. **Check for gaps**: is any section missing a document it needs?
3. **Each group member completes** the sections tied to their own crew role.

{: .discussion }
Which section of the Production Book took the most work to assemble, and why do you think that
one specifically?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Print, three-hole-punch, and bind the completed book. In parallel, finalize your portfolio site
(12.4): confirm every project page is complete and the site is published.

## Deliver, Export & Submit (~5 min)

Confirm your bound Production Book is complete, and that your portfolio site is published and
accessible.

Complete your self-evaluation rubric individually.
