# Unit 12 | Production Book, Music Video & Portfolio

## Introduction

ADD's second and final Extra Unit. Two threads that close out the year: documenting a production
the way a real studio would (a production book, the kind of record that makes a project reusable
years later), and a lighter, faster music-video mini-project that puts cinematography skills to
use one more time before building out a finished portfolio.

{: .highlight }
Nothing in this unit is ACP-tested. The unit still carries full compliance rigor. 78-minute
timing, The 7, a real Differentiation Matrix, real Organize criteria.

{% include unit-video.html %}

## Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this unit, you will be able to:

- Document a production's talent, locations, and rights/clearances the way a professional team would
- Plan a short music video's mise-en-scène and shot list from a chosen song
- Build out a complete, submission-ready portfolio site
- Compile a production book recording an entire project's process, start to finish
