# 12.2 | Rights & Clearances Documentation

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-production-legal.md %}

You collected releases and made rights decisions back in 7.4. Today they become a real,
cross-checked record, part of your Production Book, not scattered files nobody could find later.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add12-rights-clearances-reference" title="12.2 Guided Notes: Rights & Clearances Documentation" presentation="/assets/presentations/add12-2.html" %}

Proper **Creative Commons attribution** follows a fixed order: **Title, Author, Source, License**
(TASL). Missing any one of the four makes the attribution incomplete, even if the intent was
right. A complete rights/clearances record isn't just "we have releases somewhere," it's every
release and every attribution, matched against the actual assets used in the finished film, filed
together in one place.

> "'I'm pretty sure we cleared that' is exactly the sentence a rights record exists to make
> unnecessary. If it's not written down and matched to a specific asset, it's not documented."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What are the four parts of proper Creative Commons attribution, in order?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does a rights record need to be matched against the FINAL film's actual
> assets, not just the original shot list from 7.4?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "Your group swapped one piece of music during editing. What does that
> change about your rights record?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **Gather every release and rights decision** from 7.4.
2. **Format every Creative Commons attribution** using Title-Author-Source-License.
3. **Build one compiled record** listing every asset, its release/attribution, and its source.
4. **Partner check:** cross-check a classmate's attribution formatting for completeness.

{: .discussion }
Did your group find any asset in the final film that was missing a release or attribution? How
did you resolve it?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Cross-check your compiled record against the group's actual final edited film, scene by scene, to
confirm every used asset has a matching entry.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~5 min)

Save your file, then screenshot your completed record.
