# 12.4 | Portfolio Build-Out & Year-End Reel

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-client-audience.md %}

An outside viewer judges your whole year of work by whatever's actually visible on your portfolio
site. Today's about making sure that's everything, not just whatever got uploaded first.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add12-portfolio-reference" title="12.4 Guided Notes: Portfolio Build-Out" presentation="/assets/presentations/add12-4.html" %}

A complete portfolio page needs three things for every project: a **description** (purpose and
process, from 7.2's format), the **final export** itself, and enough context that a stranger
understands what they're looking at without asking. A **year-end reel** compiles your strongest
clips into one sequence, ordered deliberately (usually strongest work first and last, weaker
material in the middle where it's less memorable).

> "A portfolio with five finished projects and two blank pages doesn't read as 'five finished
> projects,' it reads as 'unfinished.' Every gap counts against the whole site, not just that one
> page."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What three things does a complete portfolio page need for each project?"

> **Analysis:** "Why might reel order (what plays first and last) matter more than which clips
> are objectively 'the best'?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "A viewer will only watch the first 15 seconds of your reel. What goes
> there, and why?"

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

1. **List every major project** from the year (S1-style summatives through the Short Film).
2. **Audit each portfolio page** against the checklist: description, final export, clear context.
3. **Flag every gap** you find.
4. **Partner check:** audit a classmate's site too. Would a stranger understand every project?

{: .discussion }
Looking back at the whole year, which single project best represents your growth?

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

Fix every gap found in your audit, then assemble a year-end reel sequencing your strongest clips
in deliberate order.

## Exit Ticket / Wrap-Up (~5 min)

Save your file, then screenshot your completed audit checklist.
