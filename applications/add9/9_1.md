# 9.1 | Art 

## Talent Reference Photos
* Bring in Costumes for you cast members.
* Photograph your cast members in costume to maintain continuity during your shoot.

## Location Photos
* Photograph the locations you plan on filming in to maintain continuity during your shoot.

## Visual Reference
* Collect visual material that helps your team pre-visualize your story
   * Images from other films.
   * Examples from Art.
   * Images that establish the mood/tone of your project.
   
# Checklist Before Submitting
- [ ] Photographs of all cast members.
- [ ] Photographs of all locations.
- [ ] Visual Reference material that helps your team pre-visualize your story.
