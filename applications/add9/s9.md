# S | 9 | Multi-Platform Delivery

This is delivery day for the Short Film. Everything built since Unit 7 gets quality-checked,
reframed for multiple platforms, exported, and archived, correctly, not just "good enough."

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
A film that's brilliant but never actually delivered correctly isn't finished. Review today's
checklist together before splitting up roles: QC pass, 3 platform versions, batch export,
archive.

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

1. **Run a full QC pass** (9.1): gaps, audio levels, safe margins, black frames.
2. **Auto Reframe into 3 versions** (9.2): 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1. Fix any tracking errors by hand.
3. **Assign roles**: one group member manages QC, another Auto Reframe, another the export queue
 and archive.
