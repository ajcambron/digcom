# S | 4 | Video Essay

This is a solo project: you plan it, shoot it, and edit it — no group. And no narration. Every bit
of storytelling has to come from sequencing, natural sound, and your music choice.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
No narration means no safety net — if a shot doesn't communicate on its own, sequencing has to
carry the weight. That constraint is the whole point of this project.

Review the constraints together: solo work, no narration, original or royalty-free music only
(confirmed in 4.3), and a color pass that's been corrected before it's graded.

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

1. **Shoot against your 4.3 storyboard and shot list** — apply the camera technique from 4.2
   deliberately, not by default.
2. **Assemble your sequence** so it tells the story through order and pacing alone.
3. **Color correct, then grade** your final sequence using 4.4's workflow.
4. **Plus/Delta peer review:**

{% include peerreview.md %}

5. **Apply your Revision Plan**, prioritizing critical fixes first.
6. **Write a one-sentence artist statement** naming your video essay's intended mood.

{: .discussion }
Telling this story with no narration — what did you have to change about how you shot or
sequenced it, compared to a reporter-led story like your Unit 3 News Story?
