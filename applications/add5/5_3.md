# 5.3 | Asset Management: Organizing & Submitting Your Raw Media

In the world of professional production, "Data Wrangling" is the critical process of moving, backing up, and organizing raw files so they don't get lost. In this lesson, you will learn how to manage your unedited "rushes" (raw recordings) and submit them for review.

{% include organizevideo.md %}

## Upload Your Raw Media
1. Add your raw recordings ("rushes") to the `Source Footage` folder, and your script, album artwork, music, and B-roll to their matching folders.
2. Raw video files are large. If you upload in the browser, do not close the tab until the "Upload Complete" checkmark appears.

{% include shareandsubmit.md %}

{: .warning }
Verify your submission: click your own link after submitting to make sure it opens the folder for viewers. A link your teacher cannot open is a missing assignment.
