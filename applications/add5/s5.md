# S | 5 | Podcast Episode

Today you deliver: export your mixed episode as both a video and audio master, package it with
real branding, and close the project out the way a professional production team would—with an
honest look back at how the work actually went.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

{: .highlight }
Delivery isn't just "finish the edit." It's exporting the RIGHT file types and building key art
that meets real platform specs—get either wrong and the episode isn't actually deliverable.

Review master export settings and key art specs together before groups begin.

## Project Work Time (~23 min)

### Master Export Settings

Export two master versions—one for video platforms, one for audio-only platforms:

| Format | Codec/Type | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Video** | **H.264 (.mp4)** | YouTube, Social Media, Portfolio |
| **Audio** | **WAV (.wav)** | Spotify, Apple Podcasts (High Quality) |

### Key Art

Build two pieces of branded artwork:

- **Album Artwork:** 2400×2400 px, PNG, must include the podcast title and high-contrast imagery.
- **YouTube Thumbnail:** 1920×1080 px, PNG, readable at a small size—bold text, expressive
  visuals.

### Delivery Checklist

- [ ] Video Master: `Podcast_Final_H264.mp4`
- [ ] Audio Master: `Podcast_Final_HighQual.wav`
- [ ] Square Art: `Album_Art_2400.png`
- [ ] Thumbnail: `YouTube_Thumb_1080.png`
- [ ] Access: link set to "Anyone with the link can view"

{% include shareandsubmit.md %}

{: .warning }
Verify your submission: click your own link after submitting to confirm it opens for viewers. A
link your teacher can't open is a missing assignment.

## GMetrix/BrainBuffet (~30 min)

Finish **Module 3**, including its module-completion checkpoint, due this unit.

## Reflect, Evaluate & Submit (~5 min)

Create a copy of the Group Project Evaluation Form and complete it individually:

1. **Self-Reflection:** growth, contribution, professionalism, what went as planned, what you'd
   change.
2. **Peer Evaluation:** score every group member (1-5) on Ideation, Openness, Cooperation, Time
   Management, Technical Skill, and Resilience—any score of 1 requires a written explanation.
3. **Submit individually**—every member submits their own copy, even if one person did the
   final export.
