# 5.2 | Podcast Script

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/add-podcast-scripting.md %}

Writing for the ear is different than writing for the eye. Script every word and you'll sound
like a robot reading at your guest. Script nothing and you'll ramble. Today's the "Goldilocks
Zone" in between.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="add5-podcast-scripting-reference" title="5.2 Guided Notes: Podcast Script" presentation="/assets/presentations/add5-2.html" %}

> "A Two-Way is jazz. Improvisation within a structure. Write bullet points for the facts you
> can't afford to get wrong, then let the actual conversation happen live."

Walk through: **bullet-point scripting** (facts as bullets, not full sentences), **attribution**
("According to a study from..."), building **the arc** (a logical question order), and treating
every mic as a **hot mic** from the moment you sit down.

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's the difference between scripting your questions and scripting the guest's
> answers?"

> **Analysis:** "Why does writing out a guest's answers word-for-word usually make an interview
> sound worse, not better?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If a guest says something surprising that isn't on your script, what
> should the host do?"

## Guided Practice | Draft & Roadmap (~23 min)

1. **Draft your script** using the sample structure: Opening / Research / Personal / Conclusion,
 with bullet-point key facts under each.
2. **Plan at least one out-loud attribution** for a researched fact.
3. **Roadmap with a partner:** without looking at your notes, tell them the 3 most interesting
 things you learned. If they look confused, simplify.
4. **Check attributions:** did you actually mention your sources out loud during the Roadmap?

{: .highlight }
If your script has full sentences written out for what your guest will say, that's the "Amateur
Play" sound. Cut it back to bullet points and trust the conversation.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** your partner's Roadmap got tangled in technical jargon and you got confused.
What's the fix before recording day?
