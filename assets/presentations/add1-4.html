Tightest to widest, what comes after ECU?

Shot types are named by how much of the subject they show, tightest to widest: ECU → ______ → ______ → ______ → ______ → ______ → ELS.
Five blanks: CU, MCU, MS, WS, LS in order.
ADD 1.4 · 1 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #1
CU · MCU · MS · WS · LS
Close-Up → Medium Close-Up → Medium Shot → Wide Shot → Long Shot, a full progression from tightest to widest.

What frames one person from behind another?
