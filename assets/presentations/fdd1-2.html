What does repetition mean in design?

Repetition means: reusing a visual element (a color, shape, or texture) consistently throughout a piece so it reads as one connected design.

What does the Rule of Thirds suggest?

The Rule of Thirds suggests dividing a composition into a 3x3 grid and placing key elements along those lines or their intersections, rather than dead-center.
