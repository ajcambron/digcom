What's an abrupt direction change?

An anchor point where a path changes direction abruptly, with no linked curve handles, is a ______ ______.
FDD 4.1 · 3 of 5
GUIDED NOTES #3
CORNER POINT
Sharp direction changes, no smooth curve through it.

What's a continuous curve through a point?

An anchor point where a path curves continuously through it, with linked symmetrical handles, is a ______ ______.
FDD 4.1 · 4 of 5
GUIDED NOTES #4
SMOOTH POINT
Linked, symmetrical handles keep the curve flowing continuously through the point.
