What's a raster image made of?

A raster (or bitmap) image is built from a fixed grid of colored squares called ______. Zoom in far enough and you'll see the individual squares — this is why enlarging a raster image makes it look ______.

GUIDED NOTES #1
PIXELS / BLOCKY
A fixed grid of pixels — zoom in enough and the individual squares show, which is why enlarging looks blocky.

3GUIDED NOTES

Converting vector to pixels?

Rasterizing means converting a vector object into a fixed grid of pixels (a one-way trip). Rendering is the more general process of a program calculating and displaying an image.
FDD 2.1 · 3 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #3
RASTERIZING / RENDERING
Rasterizing is a one-way trip; rendering is the general display/calculation process for any image.
