Client vs. audience — what's the difference?

Every design project has a ______ (who is asking for the work) and a ______ ______ (who the work is actually for) — and they aren't always the same person.
FDD 1.3 · 1 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #1
CLIENT / TARGET AUDIENCE
The client asks for the work; the target audience is who it's actually for — often two different people.

What happens when a project's boundaries keep growing?

A project's ______ ______ is its defined boundaries — what it will and won't include. When those boundaries keep growing after the project has started, that's ______ ______.
FDD 1.3 · 4 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #4

Project scope defines what's in and out; when it keeps growing after the project starts, that's scope creep.
