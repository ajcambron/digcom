Typeface or font?

A ______ is one weight/style/size within a type family; the family itself is called a ______.
FDD 5.1 · 1 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #1
FONT / TYPEFACE
Typeface is the whole family (e.g. Helvetica); font is one specific weight/style/size within it (e.g. Helvetica Bold 12pt).

What's the small line at a stroke's end?

The small line finishing off the end of a letter's main strokes is called a ______. A typeface without these is called sans-serif.
FDD 5.1 · 2 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #2
SERIF
Serif typefaces have these finishing strokes; sans-serif ("without serif") doesn't.

3GUIDED NOTES

Above the x-height or below the baseline?

The part of a lowercase letter extending above the x-height (like on a "b" or "h") is called an ______. The part extending below the baseline (like on a "p" or "g") is a descender.
FDD 5.1 · 3 of 4
GUIDED NOTES #3
ASCENDER
Ascenders reach up past the x-height; descenders drop below the baseline.

What's the enclosed space inside a letterform?

The fully or partially enclosed space inside a letterform (like the hole in an "o") is called a ______.

GUIDED NOTES #4
COUNTER
