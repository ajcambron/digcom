# S | 6 | Dobson National Park Trifold Brochure

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Finished Product.** Submit the Dobson National Park trifold brochure (two artboards, printed back to back), saved as a PDF in Schoology.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 58 min | Finish the Module Project |
| 5 min | Submit |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Look at your trifold from across the room. What still needs fixing before it's ready to print?

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**S day 1:** finish video 4.21 (The Trifold). **The rest of the S days:** finish, export and submit your project.

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 4.17 | 8:12 | Foliage and Pictures |
| 4.18 | 7:32 | The LAST Panel! |
| 4.19 | 2:48 | The Last Three Pictures |
| 4.20 | 6:14 | Wrap It Up and Save! |
| 4.21 | 5:38 | The Trifold |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Fold a sheet of paper into a mini zine (search "mini zine" for the fold) and design one in Illustrator about a topic you choose.

## Finished Product Checklist

Your module project: **the Dobson National Park trifold brochure (two artboards, printed back to back), saved as a PDF**.

- [ ] Every video in the module is finished
- [ ] The project file is saved in **Project Files** and backed up to Drive
- [ ] The final version is exported and saved in **Exports**
- [ ] It's submitted in Schoology

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course and go to **Module 4**. Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
