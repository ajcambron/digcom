# Unit 6 | GMetrix Illustrator 4: Dobson National Park Trifold (Jan 5 – Jan 22, 2027)

## Introduction

Module 4 designs a two-sided trifold brochure for Dobson. It covers model release forms, copyright and Creative Commons, bleeds and guides, preferences, placing images and text, clipping masks, text wrap and leading.

This unit replaces the regular Foundations of Digital Design Unit 6 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Illustrator Module 4.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BrainBuffet module** | Illustrator Module 4: Dobson National Park Trifold (22 videos, 142:09 of video) |
| **Class periods** | 13 school days on the A/B calendar: 4 formative days plus 2–3 S days per section |
| **Finished product** | the Dobson National Park trifold brochure (two artboards, printed back to back), saved as a PDF |
