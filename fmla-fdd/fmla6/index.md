# Unit 6 | Illustrator Modules 5–6: Business Card & Generative AI (Jan 5 – Jan 22, 2027)

## Introduction

Module 5 designs a business card for Dobson's park manager. It covers raster vs. vector and JPG vs. PNG, Image Trace, Share for Review and client feedback, printing a sample, outlining text and strokes, and Asset Export.

Module 6 introduces Illustrator's newest tools: Generate Vectors, Generative Shape Fill, Mockup and Adobe Firefly, plus test-taking tips for the certification exam.

This unit replaces the regular Foundations of Digital Design Unit 6 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Illustrator.
