## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 4.14: Pictures, Clipping Masks, and Captions**.

Today's videos (23:04 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 4.12 | 8:45 | Add Some Images |
| 4.13 | 6:14 | Text Wrapping and Hyphenation |
| 4.14 | 8:05 | Pictures, Clipping Masks, and Captions |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Fold a sheet of paper into a mini zine (search "mini zine" for the fold) and design one in Illustrator about a topic you choose.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course (Module 4 and Module 5). Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Module 4 Worksheet (last ~25 min)

Complete the **Creative Commons Worksheet** (build the five Creative Commons license symbols with shapes and Pathfinder, and label each with its initials). Video 4.11 walks through it step by step. Download it from Module 4's resources in BrainBuffet.

{: .note }
If you haven't reached video 4.11 yet, watch 4.10 and 4.11 now: they introduce Creative Commons and walk through this worksheet.

Save it in your **Project Files** folder and submit it (or a PDF of it) in Schoology before you leave.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
