# Unit 4 | Illustrator Modules 2–3: Branding Style Guide & National Park Poster (Nov 4 – Dec 1, 2026)

## Introduction

Module 2 introduces the running client, Dobson National Park. You build its branding style guide: color codes (HSB, RGB, CMYK, HEX), swatches, type classifications, visual hierarchy, logo usage and logo no-gos.

Module 3 uses your style guide to design a national park poster. It introduces the Layers panel, plus gradients, effects, the Pen tool, symbols, brushes, patterns, opacity and blending modes.

This unit replaces the regular Foundations of Digital Design Unit 4 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Illustrator.
