# 4.3 | Branding Style Guide, Part 3: Module Worksheet

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Module Worksheet.** Complete and submit this module's worksheet.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 25 min | Module Worksheet |
| 33 min | BrainBuffet Module Work |
| 5 min | Submit & Wrap-Up |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Why does a color look different on your screen than when it's printed? Use RGB and CMYK in your answer.

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 2.16: Titles/Headings, Subtitles, Body Copy, and Special Text**.

Today's videos (16:03 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 2.15 | 9:11 | More Type Classifications! |
| 2.16 | 6:52 | Titles/Headings, Subtitles, Body Copy, and Special Text |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Make a branding style guide for yourself as if you were a famous designer, athlete or artist: your logo, color scheme, fonts and design elements.

## Module Worksheet (~25 min)

Complete the **Module 2 Worksheet** (make five color swatches with swatch info cards, then activate a font for each type classification and type its name in that font) and the **Module 2 Note Catcher**. Download it from the module's resources in BrainBuffet.

{: .note }
If Illustrator asks to activate fonts, choose **Activate**. You'll need Module 1's 25 Dog in this module too; BrainBuffet includes a finished copy if yours is missing.

Save it in your **Project Files** folder and submit it (or a PDF of it) in Schoology before you leave.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course and go to **Module 2**. Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
