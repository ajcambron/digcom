## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 2.20: Preparing to Reorganize with Visual Elements**.

Today's videos (23:33 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 2.17 | 8:46 | Visual Hierarchy |
| 2.18 | 8:16 | Bringing in the 25 Dog! |
| 2.19 | 2:37 | Organizing Our Logo Usage |
| 2.20 | 3:54 | Preparing to Reorganize with Visual Elements |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Make a branding style guide for yourself as if you were a famous designer, athlete or artist: your logo, color scheme, fonts and design elements.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course (Module 2 and Module 3). Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Module 2 Worksheet (last ~25 min)

Complete the **Module 2 Worksheet** (make five color swatches with swatch info cards, then activate a font for each type classification and type its name in that font) and the **Module 2 Note Catcher**. Download it from Module 2's resources in BrainBuffet.

{: .note }
If Illustrator asks to activate fonts, choose **Activate**. You'll need Module 1's 25 Dog in this module too; BrainBuffet includes a finished copy if yours is missing.

Save it in your **Project Files** folder and submit it (or a PDF of it) in Schoology before you leave.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
