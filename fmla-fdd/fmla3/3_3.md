# 3.3 | Getting Started, Part 3: Module Worksheet

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Module Worksheet.** Complete and submit this module's worksheet.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 25 min | Module Worksheet |
| 33 min | BrainBuffet Module Work |
| 5 min | Submit & Wrap-Up |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Which Illustrator keyboard shortcut have you used most so far, and how much time do you think it saves you?

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 1.09: Point Type**.

Today's videos (15:14 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 1.08 | 9:02 | Adding Artboards and Area Type |
| 1.09 | 6:12 | Point Type |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Look up "animals made out of numbers" and build a second number animal, the same way the 25 Dog is built from a 2 and a 5.

## Module Worksheet (~25 min)

Complete the **Module 1 Worksheet** (an Illustrator practice file: draw shapes, reshape them, set and reshape type, then design one graphic of your own) and the **Keyboard Shortcuts note catcher**. Download it from the module's resources in BrainBuffet.

{: .note }
The practice file is an Illustrator template (.ait), so Illustrator makes you **File › Save As** a copy. If Illustrator asks to activate fonts, choose **Activate**. The guides inside the file only show up in Illustrator, not in a Drive preview.

Save it in your **Project Files** folder and submit it (or a PDF of it) in Schoology before you leave.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course and go to **Module 1**. Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.

## Wrap-Up

Save, back up your project file, and log out of GMetrix SMS.
