# Unit 3 | Illustrator Module 1: Getting Started (Oct 13 – Oct 30, 2026)

## Introduction

Module 1 tours Illustrator from the ground up: documents, the workspace, shapes, color, type, the Direct Selection tool, and saving. It ends with you building an animal of your own from basic shapes.

This unit replaces the regular Foundations of Digital Design Unit 3 lessons while your teacher is on leave. Every class follows the same routine: a stinger, a pace check, then self-paced work in BrainBuffet Illustrator.

| | |
|---|---|
| **BrainBuffet videos** | 1.01–1.17 (17 videos, 134:44 of video) |
| **Class periods** | 13 school days on the A/B calendar: 4 formative days plus 2–3 S days per section |
| **Summative** | your 25 Dog logo and your own animal built from basic shapes, saved as an .ai file and a PDF (50 pts) |
