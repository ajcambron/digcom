# 3.1 | Getting Started, Part 1: Exit Ticket

{: .highlight }
Today's graded assignment: **Exit Ticket.** Post your Pace Check Exit Ticket in Schoology at the end of class.

## Today's Plan

| Time | What you do |
|---|---|
| 10 min | Bell Ringer / Stinger |
| 5 min | Pace Check |
| 58 min | BrainBuffet Module Work |
| 5 min | Exit Ticket |

## Bell Ringer: Stinger (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Name something in this room that would have to be a vector graphic, not a raster image. Why?

Answer on your **Stinger Response Sheet** ([download]({{ '/assets/downloads/stinger-response-sheet.pdf' | relative_url }}) if you need a new one): rewrite the question, answer it in at least 3 sentences, share with a neighbor, copy their answer, then write 1–2 sentences on what your answers had in common.

## Stay on Pace

{: .important }
**Pace goal:** by the end of class, finish **video 1.04: Stroke Weights and Zooming**.

Today's videos (29:17 of video):

| Video | Length | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 1.01 | 8:18 | Getting Started |
| 1.02 | 8:45 | Navigating the Interface |
| 1.03 | 7:32 | Selection Tool, Rectangle Tool, and Rasters vs. Vectors |
| 1.04 | 4:42 | Stroke Weights and Zooming |

- **Behind?** Start at the first video you haven't finished and keep going. Watch with captions on and skip rewatching parts you already did.
- **Ahead?** Check your work against the video, then try the extension challenge: Look up "animals made out of numbers" and build a second number animal, the same way the 25 Dog is built from a 2 and a 5.

## BrainBuffet Work Time

1. Open **GMetrix SMS** and log in with Google (school account).
2. Open the BrainBuffet **Illustrator** course and go to **Module 1**. Start at the first video you haven't finished.
3. Open **Illustrator**. Put the video on one side of the screen and Illustrator on the other, and pause the video to do each step yourself.
4. Save every BrainBuffet file in your Digital Design Drive folder, in **Project Files**, named the way the video names it.
5. Use headphones. Captions and transcripts are available on every video.
