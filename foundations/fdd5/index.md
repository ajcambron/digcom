# Unit 5 | Typography

Unit 5 covers typography in Adobe Illustrator: reading and classifying letterforms, controlling
spacing and rhythm with the Character panel, working with type on a path and other special-type
objects, and managing longer text with threading, wrapping, and outlines — all building toward the
unit's summative: an original typographic poster.

## Lessons
- [5.1 – Type Anatomy & Classification](5_1.md) — reading letterforms and classifying typefaces
- [5.2 – Character Settings](5_2.md) — kerning, tracking, leading, and baseline shift
- [5.3 – Type on a Path & Special Type](5_3.md) — point type, area type, and type on a path
- [5.4 – Text Flow & Converting to Outlines](5_4.md) — threading, wrapping, styles, and outlines
- [S5 – Typographic Poster](s5.md) — summative project, with a structured peer review pass

## Source
Wholly new unit content, written directly from the Adobe Illustrator Certified Professional exam
objectives' typography domain (FDD outcome 5) — no prior course material to adapt.

## ACP Alignment
Objective 1.4.b (type classification), objective domain 4 (Objects, Paths & Text) — 4.3
(character/type formatting) and 4.4 (text flow and outlines). See
`_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md` for the full ACP tag breakdown per lesson.

## Assessment Rhythm
Each lesson (5.1–5.4) carries its own formative. On S5's project days, students also complete a
cumulative vocabulary quiz covering this unit's terms, receive a grade compiled from the unit's
daily bellringers, and complete a structured Plus/Delta peer review pass on their in-progress
poster.
