# Unit 7 | Layers & Appearance

Unit 7 moves past building individual shapes and into controlling how a whole composition is
organized and how each object *looks*: the Layers panel for organization (7.1), the Appearance
panel for stacking fills and strokes on a single object (7.2), non-destructive Effects for
shadows, glows, and 3D (7.3), and opacity/blending/masking for how objects interact with what's
beneath them (7.4). The unit closes with a project combining all four into one finished album
cover or gig poster.
