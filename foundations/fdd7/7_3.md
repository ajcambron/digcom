# 7.3 | Effects: Shadows, Glows & 3D

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-effects-glows.md %}

> Every effect in this lesson is non-destructive — it lives in the Appearance panel as an
> editable line, not baked into your artwork, until you choose to expand it.

Flat shapes read as flat. Today you'll add depth and atmosphere to your work using **Effects** —
Illustrator's built-in, always-editable ways to add shadows, glows, blur, and even the illusion
of three dimensions, all without redrawing a single path.

## [10 Mins] Bell Ringer / Hook

{: .discussion }
Look at these two identical circles — one has a soft shadow behind it, one doesn't. Which one
looks like it's floating above the page, and why?

> "That shadow isn't a second shape someone drew — it's one Effect, and it's still fully
> editable. Let's see what else Effects can do."

## [10 Mins] Direct Instruction

**"I Do":** using a single flat star shape, the teacher live-demos, one at a time:

1. **Effect > Stylize > Drop Shadow** — adjusting offset, blur, and opacity while the class
   watches the live preview.
2. **Effect > Stylize > Feather** — softening the star's hard edge into transparency.
3. **Effect > Stylize > Outer Glow** — adding a colored halo, then swapping it for **Inner Glow**
   to compare.
4. **Effect > 3D and Materials > Extrude & Bevel** — turning the flat star into a dimensional
   object, adjusting extrude depth and rotation.
5. Opening the Appearance panel throughout to show every effect listed as its own editable line —
   then demonstrating **Object > Expand Appearance**, and pointing out that afterward, the
   effects disappear from the panel because they're now permanent path geometry.

{: .highlight }
**Check for Understanding**
**Recall:** "Where do you go to adjust an effect's settings after you've already applied it?"
**Analysis:** "Why did the Drop Shadow disappear from the Appearance panel after I used Expand
Appearance?"
**Synthesis:** "You're designing a logo for a nighttime music festival. Which effect from today
would you reach for first, and what would you set its color to?"

## [23 Mins] Guided Practice

{% include worksheet.html file="effects-reference" title="Effects Reference" presentation="placeholder" %}

**Think-Pair-Share (2 min):** before touching your file, tell your partner which one effect you
predict will look best on your own 7.2 logo mark, and why.

1. Open your saved 7.2 logo mark.
2. Apply **at least 2 effects** from today (Drop Shadow, Feather, Glow, or 3D and Materials),
   adjusting each one's settings — don't leave anything at its default.
3. Check the Appearance panel to confirm both effects are listed as separate, editable lines.
4. Do **not** expand your appearance yet — you'll want these effects still editable for 7.4's
   opacity and blending work.
