# S | 2 | Geometric Logo

You've defined the problem, researched the real constraints, generated ideas, and developed the
strongest one. Today is **Implement**: finish the mark, export it at both required sizes, and
actually publish it to your own portfolio's empty logo and favicon slots.

## Project Briefing (~10 min)

Watch the teacher export one sample artboard as a PNG and write real alt text for it, describing
what the image shows and its purpose, not just its file name. The exact workflow you'll repeat
for both of your own artboards.

## Project Work Time (~53 min)

1. **Sketch your chosen concept at final scale** on scrap paper before touching Illustrator
 further, no new ideas today, only refinement of what 2.4 already chose.
2. **Finish the Favicon artboard**: precise geometric shapes, tested for readability at a tiny
 size (shrink your artboard view and check it still reads clearly).
3. **Finish the Header Logo artboard**: your mark combined with your site's name, matching the
 5.625:1 proportions researched in 2.3.
4. **Halfway check-in:** trade screens with a partner and run the favicon-readability test on
 each other's mark. Flag anything that gets muddy at small size.
5. **Export both artboards** as PNG (File > Export > Export As, "Use Artboards" checked).
6. **Upload both images** in Google Sites: click the **gear icon (Settings)** in the top toolbar,
 then **Brand images**. Upload the header logo to **Logo** and the favicon to **Favicon**, the
 exact slots you found empty back on 2.2.
7. **Write real alt text** for each image as you upload it, one plain sentence describing what
 it shows and its purpose. "Logo" and "Favicon" are not alt text.
8. **Publish** your site again so the new logo and favicon actually go live.

{: .discussion }
Now that your logo and favicon are live on your actual site, does either one still work as well
as it did in your 2.4 sketch, or did something change once it was real?
