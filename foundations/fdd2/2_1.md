# 2.1 | GMetrix Setup & ACP Pre-Test

Today is different from every other day this year. No new Illustrator content. The entire period
is about getting you into the platform you'll use for the rest of the course, and finding out
where you're starting from.

## Bell Ringer / Hook (~10 min)

{: .discussion }
Before a driving instructor lets you behind the wheel for real lessons, they usually watch you
drive once first, without teaching you anything yet. Why would they do that instead of just
starting to teach? What does that first drive tell them that a first lesson couldn't?

## GMetrix SMS Login (~15 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="gmetrix-pretest-steps" title="GMetrix SMS: Launch the Illustrator Practice Exam" presentation="/assets/presentations/fdd2-1.html" %}

> "Every ACP course this year runs alongside GMetrix/BrainBuffet. Today's the day we get you
> actually into it."

1. **Open GMetrix SMS** from the Applications folder (**GmetrixSMSe**).
2. **Click Login with Google** and sign in with your **school Google account**. Don't create a
 new account, and don't use a personal Google account.

{: .highlight }
If Login with Google won't take your school account, flag the teacher right away instead of
troubleshooting alone. First-day login snags are common and worth solving live.

## ACP Pre-Test (~40 min)

1. **Launch the Illustrator practice exam** from GMetrix SMS in Testing Mode (handout, Part 2).
 GMetrix opens Illustrator for you. This is separate from Module 1's regular lessons.
2. **Work quietly and independently.** This isn't graded on correctness, and it isn't something
 you've been taught yet, so guessing is expected on plenty of questions. Answer everything you
 can reason through, and don't stress over the rest.
3. **Finish the whole pre-test** before moving on to anything else. Don't start Module 1 today.

{: .highlight }
If the exam doesn't open properly (Illustrator opens but the GMetrix panel never shows up, or the
exam won't start), use the handout's troubleshooting steps: quit Illustrator (⌘Q), open it with
**Open in Rosetta** from the Creative Cloud app, then start the exam again from GMetrix SMS.

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check, once most students finish:** what's one question you genuinely had no idea
about? That's completely fine to say out loud. That's exactly what this test is for.

## Post Results & Reflect (~8 min)

1. **Find your results/score screen** once the pre-test submits.
2. **Take a screenshot** showing your score clearly: press **⌘ Command + Shift + 3** to capture
 the whole screen. It saves to your Desktop.
3. **Move it to your Digital Design Drive folder**, in the Screenshots folder.

{: .highlight }
A low score today is not a bad grade. It's the whole point. Module 1 exists to teach you
everything this pre-test just asked about.
