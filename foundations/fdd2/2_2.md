# 2.2 | Google Sites Portfolio Setup

## Why Today, Before Any Design Work

Every summative project this year publishes to one place: your own Google Sites portfolio. That
site also has two slots sitting empty right now, a **logo** and a **favicon**, the small icon
browsers show next to your page title. You'll design what goes in both slots later, in this
unit's Geometric Logo project. Today is just about getting the site itself built, published, and
ready to receive it.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="fdd2-2-google-sites-setup-checklist" title="2.2 Google Sites Portfolio Setup Checklist" presentation="/assets/presentations/fdd2-2.html" %}

Google Sites is **WYSIWYG** (What You See Is What You Get), you build directly on the page, no
code. Three tabs run the whole editor: **Insert** (text, images, embeds), **Pages** (your site's
navigation), and **Themes** (colors and fonts). Your **logo** and **favicon** aren't in any of
those tabs. They live in the site's settings: click the **gear icon (Settings)** in the top
toolbar, then choose **Brand images**.

> "Watch where I click. It's not the Themes tab. Gear icon, top toolbar, then Brand images. This
> is where your site's logo and favicon actually live. They're empty right now, and they'll stay
> empty until your Geometric Logo project is finished. I want you to see that gap with your own
> eyes today, not just take my word for it."

**Scaffolded questions. Cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What do the three main tabs in Google Sites do: Insert, Pages, and Themes?"

> **Analysis:** "Why might a visitor trust a site less if its browser tab just shows a generic
> gray icon instead of a real favicon?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If you were judging someone else's portfolio site in 10 seconds,
> what's the first thing you'd actually look at?"

## Guided Practice | Build Your Site (~23 min)

1. **Create the site**: go to `sites.google.com`, sign in with your school account, click
 **Blank**.
2. **Name your site** using your real name, not a nickname, so it's easy to find later.
3. **Find the logo and favicon slots:** click the **gear icon (Settings)** in the top toolbar,
 then click **Brand images**. The **Logo** upload is at the top and the **Favicon** upload is
 below it. Leave both empty for now, then close Settings.
4. **Turn and Talk:** compare site names with a neighbor, then open **Settings → Brand images**
 on each other's sites. Confirm you can both find the logo and favicon slots.

{: .discussion }
Now that you've seen the empty slots yourself, what do you think makes a strong favicon,
something that has to work at a genuinely tiny size?

## Guided Practice, Continued (~30 min)

1. **Create your About Me page**: add a new page titled "About Me," write 5–7 sentences
 introducing yourself as a designer.
2. **Choose a starting theme** from the Themes tab, colors and fonts. You'll refine this later,
 don't overthink it today.
3. **Publish** your site using the purple **Publish** button, then confirm.
4. **Copy your published link** using the chain-link icon (Copy Published Site Link), not the
 address bar, and record it on your checklist.

{: .highlight }
If you finish early, explore one Insert option you haven't used yet (a Content Block, an Image
Carousel, an Embed) and try adding it somewhere on your site.
