# S | 4 | Low-Poly Stained-Glass

Over the next several class meetings, you'll design and build an original **low-poly
stained-glass** piece in Adobe Illustrator — blending the traditional storytelling and color of
stained glass windows with the modern, geometric facets of low poly art.

## Project Idea

| Driving Question | How can we blend the modern aesthetic of low poly art with the traditional craft of stained glass window design using digital tools? |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Learning Goals** | Apply the Pen tool's corner and smooth points to construct geometric facets; use Join, Simplify, Shape Builder, and Pathfinder to refine and combine paths into a finished composition; apply a resolved color palette that supports the piece's meaning. |
| **Project Summary** | Students design an original stained-glass window using low-poly geometric facets, built entirely from vector paths in Adobe Illustrator, starting from their own 4.1–4.3 planning work. |
| **Major Products** | A finished .ai file and exported portfolio image; optionally, printed or vinyl-cut window clings showcasing the artwork. |
| **Making it Public** | Published to each student's portfolio website; shared with classmates in a gallery walk. |
| **Key Project Documents** | General Rubric for Individual Projects (see Resources > Rubrics) |

## Project Path

### Intro (~15 min, Day 1)

- Recap the historical/cultural significance of stained glass windows and the geometric,
  faceted nature of low poly art (from 4.1's analysis).
- **Essential Question Brainstorm:** What stories or emotions can be conveyed through geometric
  art?

### Research & Set Up (~45 min, Day 1)

### Develop (remaining project days)

- Continue building out your composition, facet by facet, referring back to your color palette
  from 4.3.
- Use **Join**, **Simplify**, **Shape Builder**, and **Pathfinder** to keep your paths clean and
  to combine smaller facets into larger, more resolved shapes as your composition develops.

**Structured Peer Review.** Once your draft has enough facets built out to read as a composition
(even with some gaps), you'll do a full Plus/Delta peer review pass with a partner:

{% include peerreview.md %}

Use your partner's Delta notes to prioritize your remaining build time — critical fixes first
(broken paths, missing facets), then message/composition issues, then color polish.

### Implement (final project day)

- Finalize your composition: adjust colors, refine remaining rough facets, and double-check for
  stray anchor points with **Simplify**.
- Write your **artist statement** — a short reflection on your design process, the meaning behind
  your artwork, and what you learned.
- Export your finished piece as a PNG for your portfolio:
