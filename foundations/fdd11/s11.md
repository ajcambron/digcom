# S | 11 | Brand Package — Promotional Spread + Product Mockup

{: .highlight }
Today you'll finish and submit both pieces of your Unit 11 brand: a promotional piece and a
packaging mockup, built on the same grid and sharing the same brand identity. This is a single
focused meeting — plan your time.

## Choose

Look back at your 11.3 brand-concept sketches. Commit to **one** logo mark, one product label
direction, and one dieline shape — you're building the real thing today, not still exploring
options.

## Set Up Your Document

{% include illustrator-new-doc.html preset="Letter" category="Print" width="8.5" height="11" units="in" units_full="Inches" orientation="portrait" color_mode="CMYK Color" raster_effects="High (300 ppi)" %}

Add a second artboard alongside your first — artboard 1 is your **promotional piece** (a flyer
or poster), artboard 2 is your **packaging mockup/dieline**. Set up the same grid and margins on
both, per 11.2.

## Build (~40 min)

1. **Promotional piece (artboard 1):** apply your brand's logo, color palette, and a tagline,
   with a clear hierarchy (headline, supporting text, a call to action).
2. **Packaging mockup (artboard 2):** build your dieline shape, then place your product label
   and logo onto it, showing where each panel folds or wraps.
3. Keep your brand identity — the same logo, the same colors, the same type — consistent across
   both artboards. That consistency IS the assignment.

## Peer Review — Plus/Delta (~10 min)

{% include peerreview.md %}

Give your partner one specific **Plus** and one specific **Delta**, naming an exact element
(not "I like the colors" — instead, "the tagline is the strongest part because it's the biggest
thing on the page after the logo"). Spend the last few minutes of Build time on your partner's
Delta if you can.

{: .reflection }
How did your peer's Delta feedback change (or confirm) a choice you'd already made?

## Save, Export & Submit (~8 min)

{% include organizedesign.md %}

1. Save your file as `LastName_FirstName_S11_BrandPackage.ai`.
2. Export both artboards as separate PNG files for your portfolio — with **Use Artboards**
   checked, Illustrator exports one PNG per artboard automatically:

{% include illustrator-export-png.html filename="LastName_FirstName_S11_BrandPackage.png" %}

3. Write a one-paragraph brand rationale: what's the brand, who's it for, and why did you make
   the design choices you made?
