# Unit 6 | Refinement & Output Prep

Unit 6 is the "last mile" unit — every lesson is a finishing skill needed to take a piece from
"looks done" to "actually correct for where it's headed": choosing the right file format, fixing
color-gamut mismatches, polishing paragraph-level type, and applying transformations. The unit's
summative is a different kind of project: instead of building something new, students revise and
finalize a piece they already made in an earlier unit.

{: .highlight }
This is a brand-new unit added by the Sep 2026 restructure — the calendar's 9-units-with-9-
summatives math required one more unit than the original 8-unit draft had, and this unit is built
entirely from lessons that floated out of other units because their original summative didn't
strictly require them (see `_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md`'s FDD Unit 6 rationale).

## Lessons
- [6.1 – Saving & File Formats](6_1.md) — choosing the right format for a destination
- [6.2 – Digital Color: Modes & Gamuts](6_2.md) — out-of-gamut colors and print-safe correction
- [6.3 – Paragraph Settings & Area Type](6_3.md) — alignment, indents, spacing, hyphenation
- [6.4 – Transformations & Live Corners](6_4.md) — scale, rotate, reflect, shear, Live Corners
- [S6 – Portfolio Polish Project](s6.md) — summative revision of an earlier FDD piece

## Source
Wholly new unit, assembled from lessons that floated out of the old Unit 2 (file formats), Unit 3
(color/gamut), Unit 5 (paragraph settings), and Unit 6 (transformations) drafts, plus a new
revision-based summative — see `_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md` for the full rationale.

## ACP Alignment
Objective 2.4.a (saving/file formats), 1.3.b (color modes/gamut), objective domain 4 (Objects,
Paths & Text) — 4.3.c and 4.4.b (paragraph/area type) — and 5.1.b/4.2.d (transformations). See
`_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md` for the full ACP tag breakdown per lesson.

## Assessment Rhythm
Each lesson (6.1–6.4) carries its own formative. On S6's project days, students also complete a
cumulative vocabulary quiz covering this unit's terms and receive a grade compiled from the
unit's daily bellringers. Unlike this course's other summatives, S6 also keeps a check-graded
Organize line (the Portfolio Polish Revision Checklist) alongside the project rubric, since it
produces one discrete, checkable deliverable rather than an open-ended new piece.
