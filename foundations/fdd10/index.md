# Unit 10 | ACP Review, Practice Exams & Certification (Apr 20 – May 3, 2027)

{: .highlight }
Unit 10 introduces **no new content**. Every ACP-tested objective for FDD (Illustrator) was
already covered across Units 1–9 (Sep 1, 2026 – Apr 16, 2027). This unit's entire job is to get
every student from "has seen it once" to "certifies on it" — targeted review, full-length
practice exams, and the actual Adobe Certified Professional exam itself.

## Why this page doesn't follow the standard lesson schema

Every other unit in this course (`lesson:` front matter, `X_Y.md` / `X_Y-teacher.md` pairs, four
formative lessons cycling the Exit Ticket Bank, a summative block) is built around teaching and
assessing **new** content. Unit 10 has no new SWBAT, no new `_includes/vocab/` terms, and nothing
that fits the check/+/− formative system — there's no fresh skill to screenshot-receipt or
fix-this-file on. Forcing this unit into 4 formative lessons + 1 summative would mean inventing
fake "lessons" with no real learning target, which is worse than just describing what actually
happens. Per `CLAUDE.md`'s Academic Calendar & Scope Context section, this unit exists to "shift
pacing to targeted review and GMetrix/BrainBuffet practice-test work, not new instruction" — so
this page describes that structure directly, as a planning reference, rather than pretending it's
five more content days.

A single well-organized index page (this one) is a deliberate choice over building 10 separate
`day1.md`–`day10.md` pages: the day-by-day plan below is genuinely just "which ACP domain(s) does
today target, and in what format" — a teacher planning this week needs a reference table, not ten
thin pages that all say some version of "review + GMetrix." If a future pass wants standalone day
pages (e.g., to hand a sub a single self-contained page), the table below is written so it could
be split out mostly as-is — but doing that today would be padding, not added value.

## Calendar context

Per `_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md`'s Calendar Math section: Unit 10 runs **Apr 20 –
May 3, 2027, 10 school days**, MP4. The ACP certification window has to land between Access
Testing and AP Testing (AP Exams run May 3–7 and May 10–14, 2027) — Unit 10 is the only clean gap,
which is why it's built with real slack (10 days) rather than the old plan's ~4 meetings.

| 2 | **Domain 1 & 2 Review** — Design Industry; Document Management | Station rotation: a quick re-teach mini-lesson per domain + targeted GMetrix/BrainBuffet module practice filtered to that domain | Pull materials from FDD Units 1, 2, 6, 9 (see cross-reference table below) |
| 3 | **Domain 3 Review** — Workflow & Interface | Station rotation, same format | Pull from FDD Units 2, 7, 8 |
| 4 | **Domain 4 Review** — Objects, Paths & Text | Station rotation, same format — budget extra 1:1 time here; it's FDD's largest, most-tested domain | Pull from FDD Units 3, 4, 5, 8 |
| 5 | **Domain 5 Review** — Appearance & Position | Station rotation, same format | Pull from FDD Units 3, 6, 7 |
| 6 | **Full-Length Practice Exam #1** | Timed, full-exam-conditions GMetrix/BrainBuffet practice exam covering all 5 domains | Simulates real test conditions; save the domain-by-domain score breakdown |

## How to use each day's data

- **Day 1's diagnostic** and each student's Unit 1–9 formative history (F1–F4 check marks in
  Schoology) together tell you who needs which domain — don't run identical whole-class review
  when the data shows an uneven spread.
- **GMetrix/BrainBuffet's own domain-tagged reporting** should drive which specific modules get
  assigned on Days 2–5 and which get re-assigned after Practice Exam #1 (Day 7) — the platform
  already knows a student's per-domain accuracy; use it instead of re-diagnosing from scratch.
- **Practice Exam #1 and #2 scores** are the real signal for Day 7–8 grouping — a student who
  tests strong on Domain 4 by Day 6 doesn't need more Domain 4 time on Day 7, even if they
  struggled with it back on Day 1.

## Assessment & grading

Unit 10 has no F1–F6 formatives and no summative — there is nothing new to grade with the
check/+/− system, and the certification exam itself is scored by Adobe/Certiport, not the
teacher. What still needs a grade in the gradebook:

- **Completion/participation** for the review stations (Days 2–5) and both practice exams (Days
  6, 8) — did the student engage with the assigned modules, not "did they get every question
  right."
- **Growth**, if your gradebook supports it: Day 1 diagnostic domain score → Practice Exam #1 →
  Practice Exam #2, as a simple improvement metric, is more meaningful than any single day's raw
  score.
- **The certification exam result itself** (pass/fail, and per-domain breakdown from Certiport)
  is a reporting/record-keeping item, not a classroom grade — check current district policy
  before entering it as one.

## Differentiation notes (general — not a per-lesson matrix)

Because this page isn't a single lesson, it doesn't carry the standard per-lesson Differentiation
Matrix — but real students are still in the room for all 10 days, so plan for:

- **IEP/504:** extended time on both practice exams (already a standard testing accommodation —
  confirm each student's accommodation plan covers the actual certification exam, not just
  in-class practice); chunk each domain's review station into smaller sub-checklists rather than
  one long list.
- **ELL/Multilingual:** GMetrix/BrainBuffet's own glossary/definition features, where available,
  should be enabled during practice (not during the real exam, if testing rules restrict it) —
  confirm Certiport's actual accommodation policy for language supports before test day.
- **Advanced/GT:** students who diagnose as strong across all 5 domains by Day 1 can move straight
  into early independent GMetrix/BrainBuffet full-exam attempts and use freed-up station time to
  peer-tutor a station they've already mastered, rather than sitting through review they don't
  need.
