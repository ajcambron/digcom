# 1.3 | Texture Assignment

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-audience-goals.md %}

No computers today — guided notes, then the third element assignment.

## Part 1 — Guided Notes: Purpose, Audience & Client Goals (~12 min)

Copy the outline below onto your notes handout.

{% include worksheet.html file="guided-notes-1-3" title="1.3 Guided Notes: Purpose, Audience & Client Goals" %}

1. Every design project has a **______** (who is asking for the work) and a **______**
   ______ (who the work is actually for) — and they aren't always the same person.
2. **Demographics** are ______________________________ used to describe an audience.
3. **Accessibility** means designing so people with a range of ______ (vision, hearing, motor,
   cognitive) can use and understand the work.
4. A project's **______** ______ is its defined boundaries — what it will and won't include.
   When those boundaries keep growing after the project has started, that's **______** ______.

{: .discussion }
Think of a poster, ad, or app you've seen recently that clearly *wasn't* made for you. How could
you tell?

## Part 2 — Texture Assignment (~50 min)

Same process again — thumbnail → rough → comp — this time using **texture**.

{% include worksheet.html file="thumbnail-sheet" title="Thumbnail Sheet (16 boxes)" %}

1. **Thumbnails (~12 min):** draw **16 thumbnails**, each exploring a different texture (e.g. a
   cross-hatched basket weave, a "sandy" texture of closely-drawn dots). Look around the room for
   inspiration — chalk, carpet, ceiling tiles — and imagine how you'd recreate that texture
   graphically with a marker.
2. **Select (~2 min):** choose your **3 strongest** thumbnails.
3. **Roughs (~15 min):** develop each into a rough sketch.
4. **Select (~1 min):** choose your single strongest rough.
5. **Comp (~20 min):** develop it into a finished comp — pencil first, then black marker.

## Wrap-Up (~6 min)

Photograph your finished comp — you now have 3 of your 4 element comps done.

{: .reflection }
If a client had specifically asked for a "calm" texture design vs. an "energetic" one, would
your comp fit either request? Why or why not?
