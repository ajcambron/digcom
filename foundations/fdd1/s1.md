# S | 1 | Illustrator Recreation

{: .highlight }
This is the one part of Unit 1 that touches a device — you're in the computer lab today. Bring
the photo you took of your strongest comp (from Line, Shape, Texture, or Color).

You've spent this unit generating and refining paper designs through thumbnails, roughs, and a
final comp. Today, you'll try to recreate your **single strongest comp** in Adobe Illustrator —
your first time opening the software. You won't have had a formal lesson on the Illustrator
interface yet (that's Unit 2) — today is about exploration and a first hands-on try, not mastery.

## Choose

1. Look back across all four of your comps (Line, Shape, Texture, Color).
2. Pick the **one** you're proudest of, or the one you think will translate best to a computer
   screen. You'll be recreating this one — not all four.

## Recreate

Using only these basic tools, try to approximate your chosen comp as closely as you can:

1. **Shape tools** (Rectangle, Ellipse) to build up any geometric forms.
2. **Line Segment tool** for straight lines; hold Shift while dragging to keep them
   perfectly horizontal, vertical, or at a 45° angle.
3. **Fill color** — click a shape, then pick a color from the Swatches panel to color it in,
   matching your paper piece as closely as you can.
4. **Selection tool** to move, resize, and layer your shapes on top of each other.

Don't worry about matching your comp exactly, and don't worry about tools you haven't learned
yet (the Pen tool, gradients, effects — all coming in later units). The goal is to see how much
of your idea you can rebuild with just these four basics.
