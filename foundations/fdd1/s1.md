# S | 1 | Design Elements Exercises

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-design-process.md %}

{: .highlight }
This project is the one part of Unit 1 that touches a device — everything else in this unit is
done entirely on paper, since network/computer access is unreliable this early in the year.
You'll only need a phone or tablet at the very end, to photograph your finished comp for your
portfolio.

Go through the design process — thumbnails, then a rough, then a final **comp** — for each of
the 4 Elements of Art below: Line, Shape, Texture, Color. You already practiced this exact
process at a smaller scale in Lesson 1.2 (your Thumbnail Warm-Up); today you'll run through it
for real, four times, then pick your single best piece to finish as a full comprehensive design.

{: .highlight }
**One class period.** After the 10-minute bellringer, you have about 57 minutes of working time
(rounds + comp below) before the last ~10 minutes go to photographing and submitting. Move
quickly through thumbnails — they're meant to be fast and rough, not precious. Watch the clock
per round below.

## Line Round (~10 min)

1. **Thumbnails (4 min):** on plain paper, draw **6 thumbnails** using only line — vary
   thickness, direction, and spacing in each box. Experiment with as many different types of
   line as you can — thick, thin, wavy, dotted.
2. **Select (1 min):** circle your **2 strongest** thumbnails.
3. **Rough (5 min):** pick your favorite of the 2 and develop it into one rough sketch — neater
   and more careful than a thumbnail, pencil first, refined with pen or marker.

## Shape Round (~10 min)

1. **Thumbnails (4 min):** draw **6 thumbnails**, each using 4 shapes with similar qualities
   (e.g. all geometric, or all organic). Arrange the shapes so the viewer's eye can move easily
   from one to the next.
2. **Select (1 min):** circle your **2 strongest**.
3. **Rough (5 min):** develop your favorite into one rough sketch.

## Texture Round (~10 min)

1. **Thumbnails (4 min):** draw **6 thumbnails**, each exploring a different texture (e.g. a
   cross-hatched basket weave, a "sandy" texture of closely-drawn dots).
2. **Select (1 min):** circle your **2 strongest**.
3. **Rough (5 min):** develop your favorite into one rough sketch.

## Color Round (~12 min)

1. **Thumbnails (5 min):** using colored pencil, draw **6 thumbnails** of abstract patterns,
   drawing on what you noticed in the Line/Shape/Texture rounds. Apply two contrasting colors of
   the purest hue (e.g. purple vs. violet-red, green vs. yellow-green) — think about what
   happens where they overlap, or if you leave an area white.
2. **Select (1 min):** circle your **2 strongest**.
3. **Rough (6 min):** develop your favorite into one rough, using different hues of your chosen
   colors to create a sense of motion/energy or calm/stillness.

## Final Comp (~15 min)

1. Look back across all **4 roughs** (Line, Shape, Texture, Color) and pick the **single
   strongest one** — the piece you're proudest of.
2. Develop it into a full **comprehensive design ("comp")** — a clean, final, neatly-executed
   sheet of plain paper. If your chosen rough is from Line/Shape/Texture, finish it in black
   marker; if it's from the Color round, finish it with your full set of colored pencils.

{: .highlight }
Placing a clean sheet of paper between your hand and your work as you go keeps it from smudging.

## Document/Save (~8 min)

* Photograph your final comp (and your favorite roughs, if you'd like) for your portfolio.
