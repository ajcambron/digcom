No computers today — guided notes, then the second element assignment.

## Part 1 — Guided Notes: Elements & Principles of Design (~12 min)

Copy the outline below onto your notes handout.

{% include worksheet.html file="guided-notes-1-2" title="1.2 Guided Notes: Elements & Principles" %}

1. The **elements of design** are the raw building blocks every design is made of: line, shape,
   texture (from 1.1), plus ______ / ______ and ______/______.
2. The **principles of design** are the rules for *arranging* those building blocks:
   ______, ______, ______, ______, and ______ (fill in from the vocabulary above).
3. **Repetition** means ________________________________________________.
4. The **Rule of Thirds** suggests ______________________________________________.

{: .discussion }
Look at your comp from 1.1 (Line day). Can you find one principle of design already at work in
it, even though you weren't thinking about principles when you made it?

## Part 2 — Shape Assignment (~50 min)

Same process as Line day — thumbnail → rough → comp — this time using **shape**.

{% include worksheet.html file="thumbnail-sheet" title="Thumbnail Sheet (16 boxes)" %}

1. **Thumbnails (~12 min):** draw **16 thumbnails**, each using 4 shapes with similar qualities
   (e.g. all geometric, or all organic). Arrange the shapes in each box so the viewer's eye can
   move easily from one to the next.
2. **Select (~2 min):** choose your **3 strongest** thumbnails.
3. **Roughs (~15 min):** develop each into a rough sketch.
4. **Select (~1 min):** choose your single strongest rough.
5. **Comp (~20 min):** develop it into a finished comp — pencil first, then black marker.

## Wrap-Up (~6 min)

Photograph your finished comp — keep it alongside your Line comp; you'll pick your overall
strongest from all four elements on the S | 1 project day.

{: .reflection }
Which felt more natural to generate ideas for — line or shape? Why do you think that is?

📤 **Exit Ticket:** turn in your guided notes, your 16 thumbnails, your 3 roughs, and your
finished comp.
