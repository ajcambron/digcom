## The 7 — how this lesson hits each element

| Element | This lesson |
|---|---|
| Organization for Learning | Guided notes outline + the thumbnail/rough/comp worksheet sequence. |
| Connection to Learning | Opening discussion asks students to spot a design clearly not made for them, before formally defining audience/demographics. |
| Target for Learning | SWBAT identify a design's client, audience, and goal, and apply the element of texture through the full thumbnail-rough-comp process to a finished design. |
| Collaboration around Learning | Students may look around the room together for texture inspiration and discuss how to recreate it graphically. |
| Evidence of Learning | Completed guided notes + 16 thumbnails + 3 roughs + comp, collected as the exit ticket. |
| Summarization of Learning | Reflection connecting the comp to a hypothetical client request (calm vs. energetic). |
| Accommodations to the Plan | See Differentiation Matrix below. |

## Turn-In Grading

Check system (see Resources > Rubrics): **✓** = guided notes complete, all 16 thumbnails
attempted, 3 roughs, and a finished comp. **✓+** = same, plus the comp shows real refinement
(resolved composition, deliberate use of texture) beyond the minimum. **✓−** = guided notes
incomplete, thumbnail count noticeably short, or comp missing/unfinished.

## Differentiation Matrix

| Cohort | Modification |
|---|---|
| IEP/504 | Reduce to 8 thumbnails instead of 16; provide a short list of texture examples (basket weave, sand, wood grain) to choose from rather than open-ended brainstorming. |
| ELL/Multilingual | Word bank for the guided notes blanks; bilingual glossary card for client/audience/demographics/accessibility. |
| Advanced/GT | Require thumbnails to explore textures from at least 3 different real-world material categories (e.g. fabric, stone, organic) rather than variations on one. |

## Materials
- Guided notes handout (paper, one per student)
- Thumbnail sheet — 16 boxes (paper, one per student)
- Plain paper for roughs and comp
- Pencils and black markers
