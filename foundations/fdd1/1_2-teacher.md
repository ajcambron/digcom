## Timing (fills the full 78 minutes — no GMetrix all unit)

| Segment | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Bell Ringer | 10 | Paper stinger |
| Guided Notes: Elements & Principles | 12 | Direct instruction + fill-in-blank notes, whole-class |
| Shape Assignment (thumbnails → roughs → comp) | 50 | Independent work, teacher circulating |
| Wrap-up / photograph | 6 | Save for reference on the S1 project day |

## The 7 — how this lesson hits each element

| Element | This lesson |
|---|---|
| Organization for Learning | Guided notes outline + the thumbnail/rough/comp worksheet sequence. |
| Connection to Learning | Opening discussion asks students to find a principle already at work in yesterday's Line comp, before formally naming the principles. |
| Target for Learning | SWBAT name the elements and principles of design and apply the element of shape through the full thumbnail-rough-comp process to a finished design. |
| Collaboration around Learning | Students may compare shape arrangements with a neighbor before selecting their strongest thumbnails. |
| Evidence of Learning | Completed guided notes + 16 thumbnails + 3 roughs + comp, collected as the exit ticket. |
| Summarization of Learning | Reflection comparing how natural line vs. shape felt to generate ideas for. |
| Accommodations to the Plan | See Differentiation Matrix below. |

## Differentiation Matrix

| Cohort | Modification |
|---|---|
| IEP/504 | Reduce to 8 thumbnails instead of 16; provide the guided notes with the element/principle word bank visible throughout. |
| ELL/Multilingual | Bilingual vocab card pairing each principle with a simple visual example, kept visible during the assignment. |
| Advanced/GT | Require the comp to demonstrate 2 principles instead of 1, labeled on the back. |

## Materials
- Guided notes handout (paper, one per student)
- Thumbnail sheet — 16 boxes (paper, one per student)
- Plain paper for roughs and comp
- Pencils and black markers
