# 3.2 | Shape Tools

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-shape-tools.md %}

You've used Rectangle, Ellipse, and Line before (S1) — today you'll go deeper, with real control
over exact size, corners, and two new tools.

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="guided-notes-3-2" title="3.2 Shape Tools Reference & Notes" presentation="placeholder" %}

Every shape you draw with a shape tool is a **Live Shape** — it stays fully editable (drag the
little corner-radius widget to round a corner, or use the Control Panel to type exact width and
height) until you tell Illustrator to expand it into a plain path.

> "Watch: I'll draw a rectangle by dragging, then click once instead and type in exact numbers.
> Same tool, but now I have pixel-perfect control instead of guessing by eye."

Add the two new tools: **Polygon** (tap the Up/Down arrow keys mid-drag to add or remove sides)
and **Star** (same arrow-key trick for points; hold Option/Alt to keep the arms straight). Every
shape also has a **bounding box** — the invisible rectangle Illustrator uses whenever you scale,
rotate, or align it.

**Scaffolded questions — cold-call through all three:**

> **Recall:** "What's a Live Shape, and how is it different from a plain path?"

> **Analysis:** "Why might typing exact dimensions be better than dragging by eye for some
> projects, but not others?"

> **Synthesis/Extension:** "If you had to build a stop sign using only these 5 tools, which one(s)
> would you reach for, and what modifier would you need?"

## Guided Practice — Build an Icon (~23 min)

1. **Pick a simple subject** — a house, a robot face, a coffee cup, anything recognizable as
   basic geometric shapes.
2. **Build it using only** Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon, Star, and Line Segment — no freehand
   drawing, no Pen tool.
3. **Use at least 2 keyboard modifiers** (Shift to constrain, Option/Alt to draw from center or
   keep a star's arms straight, arrow keys to add/remove sides or points).
4. **Enter at least one shape's dimensions numerically** instead of dragging it by eye.
5. **Turn and Talk:** show a partner your in-progress icon (cover the label) — can they guess what
   it's supposed to be?

{: .discussion }
**Cold call check:** which of the 5 shape tools did you NOT end up using, and could your icon have
worked without one of the tools you did use?
