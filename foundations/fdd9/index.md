# Unit 9 | The Working Designer: Copyright, Assets & Export

Unit 9 is the course's final content unit before Unit 10's ACP review and certification window —
it shifts from making artwork to delivering it like a working designer would: judging what's
legally safe to use, including AI-generated content (9.1); packaging a finished project for
client handoff (9.2); placing and linking external assets correctly (9.3); and exporting
correctly for screen versus print (9.4). The unit closes with a full client brand project
delivered as a packaged, exported handoff.

- [9.2 – Managing & Packaging the Client Project](9_2.md)

New content, written for the ACP Illustrator objective domains 1 (Design Industry) and 2
(Document Management).

## ACP Alignment
ACP 1.2 (copyright & AI-content ethics), 1.1.d (client project management), 2.4.c (packaging),
2.3 (placing/linking), 2.4.b (export) — see `_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md` for the
full per-lesson tag breakdown.

## Assessment Rhythm
Each lesson (9.1–9.4) carries its own formative and cycles through Exit Tickets 1–4 in order. On
the S9 project block, students also complete a cumulative vocabulary quiz covering this unit's
terms and receive a grade compiled from the unit's daily bellringers.
