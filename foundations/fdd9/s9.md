# S | 9 | Client Brand Project

{: .highlight }
This is Unit 9's project block, and the last new-content project before Unit 10's ACP review and
certification window. Deliver this exactly like a real client handoff — packaged, correctly
exported, and with every asset's licensing checked.

## Design Brief

{% include designbrief.md %}

{: .highlight }
This brief's template is shared across DigCom's courses, so a few fields read video-specific —
adapt them to this project: your "Director" is you, the designer; your "Opportunity Statement" is
the small business or organization you're designing for; your "Criteria" and "Deliverables" are
the brand identity pieces below, not a video.

**This project's actual deliverables:**

1. A primary logo mark (built using techniques from across the course).
2. At least one applied piece using that logo (a business card, social media template, or
   letterhead).
3. A complete, packaged project folder (File > Package) with a sourcing log for every
   non-original asset.
4. A screen-ready export set and a print-ready export of the finished identity.

## [10 Mins] Bell Ringer / Hook

Today's stinger: name one real brand's logo you recognize instantly, and one specific design
choice (shape, color, or typography) that makes it memorable.

## [15 Mins] Project Brief & Demo

> "Here's a packaged, exported deliverable I built for a made-up client — watch how the folder
> is organized, what the export set includes, and how the sourcing log documents every asset that
> wasn't originally mine."

## [45 Mins] Studio Time

1. Choose or invent your client and sketch initial logo concepts on paper.
2. Build your logo and at least one applied piece in Illustrator, drawing on techniques from
   across the whole course.
3. Keep a running **sourcing log** as you work — every stock image, font, or AI-assisted element
   gets a one-line entry naming its license and why it's clear to use.
4. **Structured Peer Review (middle project day):** trade screens with a partner and run a
   Plus/Delta pass, standing in for the brief's second client meeting.

{% include peerreview.md %}

5. Revise based on peer feedback before your final project day.

## [30 Mins] GMetrix / BrainBuffet Self-Guided Strand

Continue **GMetrix Practice Test 1** (training mode) during earlier project days; students who
finish it may begin **Practice Test 2** (testing mode) ahead of Unit 10.

## [8 Mins] Package, Export & Submit — final project day

{% include organizedesign.md %}

1. Run **File > Package** on your finished project and review the summary report for any
   restricted fonts.
2. Export your screen-ready set (Asset Export panel, Use Artboards) and a print-ready PDF.
3. Save your packaged folder as `LastName_FirstName_S9_ClientBrand/`.
