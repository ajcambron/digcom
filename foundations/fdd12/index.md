# Unit 12 | Scale, Sketchbook & Portfolio

Unit 12 is FDD's second and final Extra Unit (May 4 – Jun 4, 2027) — post-certification
enrichment closing out the course. It covers two of the course's Learning Outcomes that never
got a dedicated unit earlier in the year (scale/measurement/sketchbook practice, outcome 2), and
ends with students curating and presenting a portfolio of their best work from across the entire
year — the natural capstone to the whole FDD course, not just this unit.

{: .highlight }
Nothing in this unit is ACP-tested — see each lesson's `standard:` field, which reads "Not
ACP-tested — post-certification enrichment" instead of an ACP objective tag. The unit still
carries full compliance rigor (78-minute timing, The 7, a real Differentiation Matrix, real
Organize criteria) — the students are still in the room, even though the exam is behind them.

## Lessons
- [12.1 – Scale & Measurement in Design](12_1.md) — scale ratios, scale rulers, converting actual
  to scaled dimensions
- [12.2 – Sketchbook Practice & Ideation](12_2.md) — divergent/convergent thinking, morgue files,
  rapid ideation
- [12.3 – Portfolio Curation](12_3.md) — selecting and organizing a year's worth of work
- [12.4 – Portfolio Presentation & Critique](12_4.md) — presenting work and giving/receiving
  structured critique
- [S12 – Final Portfolio Presentation](s12.md) — summative capstone

## Source

`_planning/2026-27-scope-and-sequence.md`'s "FDD Extra Units" section gives only a rough bullet
list to build from — scale, measurement & sketchbook work (FDD outcome 2), and a portfolio
build-out using `_includes/portfolio.md` — with no per-lesson table like every other unit. The
breakdown above is this unit's own first-pass answer to that gap.

**Rationale for the 4+1 split:** scale/measurement (12.1) and sketchbook/ideation (12.2) are two
distinct, self-contained skills from the course's Learning Outcome 2 that never got their own
lessons earlier in the year (they were folded into other units' project days instead) — pairing
them at the start of this unit finally gives each its own dedicated lesson. The portfolio work
needed **two** lessons rather than one (12.3 curation, 12.4 presentation/critique) because
selecting and organizing a year's work is a genuinely different cognitive task from presenting
and defending those choices out loud — compressing both into a single 78-minute period would
shortchange whichever half went second. 12.4 is deliberately the unit's critique lesson and uses
Structured Peer Review (`peerreview.md`) directly as its day's core activity, which doubles as
rehearsal for S12's real presentation. Scale/measurement and sketchbook/ideation don't feed
directly into the portfolio pair the way Unit 11's page-layout and packaging strands fed into its
summative — they're included here because they're real, ungraded-elsewhere course outcomes that
belong somewhere before the year ends, and pairing them with the portfolio work in one final unit
(rather than scattering them earlier) keeps FDD's Extra Units mechanism doing exactly the job
Sep 2026's restructure gave it: a home for real content that isn't on the exam.

## ACP Alignment

None — this is post-certification enrichment content, not on the Adobe Certified Professional
Graphic Design & Illustration exam. See the Foundations course's general Learning Outcomes
(`foundations/index.md`, outcome 2: scale/proportion/measurement; outcome 6: presentation and
critique skills) for the standards this unit still serves.

## Assessment Rhythm

Each formative lesson (12.1–12.4) carries its own lesson-level formative (F1–F4, or Structured
Peer Review for 12.4 — see each lesson's Notes field for which one and why) and cycles through
the Exit Ticket Bank in the standard order (Stoplight → Glow & Grow → Emoji Meter → 3-2-1). On
the S12 project day, students also complete a cumulative vocabulary quiz covering this unit's
terms and receive a grade compiled from the unit's daily bellringers, same as every other unit.
