# 12.4 | Portfolio Presentation & Critique

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-portfolio-presentation.md %}

A portfolio isn't finished until someone else has looked at it — today you'll give and get real
feedback, then get your portfolio presentation-ready.

## Bell Ringer (~10 min)

Self-check: rate your own portfolio draft **G/Y/R** against yesterday's ranked shortlist. If
yellow or red, what's still missing?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Guided notes: **poise** in a presentation comes from pacing, eye contact, and clear language —
all things you can practice, not just talents you either have or don't. **Constructive
criticism** is specific, objective, and kind — watch your teacher critique a sample portfolio
piece out loud, naming an exact **Plus** and an exact **Delta**.

{: .discussion }
What's the difference between "I like this piece" and useful feedback on this piece? What makes
one specific and the other not?

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

{: .highlight }
As a class, practice giving Plus/Delta feedback together on one shared sample piece. Push
yourselves to cite specific design vocabulary from across the whole year — hierarchy, alignment,
brand identity, whatever actually applies.

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

{% include peerreview.md %}

1. Spend the first part of this block finalizing your portfolio page from 12.3.
2. In small groups, run a Structured Peer Review circuit: each person presents their portfolio
   briefly, and the group gives Plus/Delta feedback citing specific vocabulary.
3. If time allows, make at least one real change to your portfolio based on feedback you
   received.

{: .reflection }
What's one specific piece of Delta feedback you received today that you'll actually act on
before S12?
