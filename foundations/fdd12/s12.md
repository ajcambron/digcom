# S | 12 | Final Portfolio Presentation

{: .highlight }
This is it — the final graded day of FDD. You'll present your curated, critiqued portfolio to a
small group, field questions at three different levels, and write a final reflection on your
growth as a designer this year.

## Bell Ringer (~10 min)

With a partner, rehearse your presentation once out loud: introduce yourself, highlight 2-3
pieces, explain your process, and close. Time yourselves — aim for about 4 minutes.

## Presentation Kickoff (~10 min)

Watch your teacher model a full sample presentation. Notice the structure: **intro → 2-3
highlighted pieces → process → close**. Review the 3 question levels your group will ask each
presenter:

- **Recall:** "What tool did you use for this piece?"
- **Analysis:** "Why did you arrange/choose it that way?"
- **Synthesis/Extension:** "How would you change this piece for a different audience?"

## Small-Group Presentations (~40 min)

In your small group, take turns presenting (~4 minutes each). After each presentation, the group
asks one question at each of the 3 levels above. Presenters: answer specifically, citing your
actual process — not "I don't know, it just looked good."

{: .discussion }
As an audience member: what made a classmate's answer to an Analysis or Synthesis question
genuinely convincing?

## Final Reflection (~10 min)

Write a reflection citing **two specific, concrete moments** from your work this year — not
generic praise of yourself. For example: *"In my Unit 4 stained-glass piece, I used the Pathfinder
tool to merge overlapping shapes for the first time, and that's the technique I reused in my
Unit 11 packaging mockup."*

{: .reflection }
What are you most proud of from this year, and what's one thing you'd still like to get better
at?
