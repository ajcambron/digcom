# 12.2 | Sketchbook Practice & Ideation

## Key Terms

{: .vocab }
{% include vocab/fdd-sketchbook-ideation.md %}

Every designer needs a way to have a LOT of ideas fast, without getting stuck on the first one.
Today's lesson gives that process a name — and a workout.

## Bell Ringer (~10 min)

Journal for a few minutes: when was the last time you had a genuinely good idea? Where were you,
what were you doing, and what were you looking at right before it came to you?

## Direct Instruction (~10 min)

Guided notes: **ideation** means generating a large volume of ideas before judging any of them —
**divergent thinking** comes first (as many different directions as possible), then
**convergent thinking** narrows the field down to the strongest one. A **morgue file** (idea
library) is a working designer's personal, organized collection of references saved for future
inspiration — look at a real example.

{: .discussion }
Why might generating 9 mediocre-looking ideas actually lead to a BETTER final result than
carefully drawing just 1 idea from the start?

## Guided Practice (~23 min)

As a class, we'll try one round of rapid ideation together on a simple shared prompt. Then, with
a partner, try a short mini-round on your own before independent work begins.

## Studio/Project Work Time (~30 min)

{% include worksheet.html file="sketchbook-ideation-sheet" title="Sketchbook Ideation Sheet" %}

{: .highlight }
**Today's constraint:** listen for your teacher's announced constraint at the start of this
block — it has to show up in at least one of your 9 ideas.

1. **Divergent pass (~10 min):** fill as many of the 9 boxes as you can with a different idea for
   today's prompt. Work fast — don't stop to judge, just keep generating.
2. **Convergent pass (~2 min):** circle the ONE idea you'd most want to develop further.
3. **Justify (~remaining time):** write one sentence explaining why that idea is the strongest,
   then begin refining it further if time allows.

{: .reflection }
Which idea did you circle as your strongest, and what made it stand out from the other 8?
