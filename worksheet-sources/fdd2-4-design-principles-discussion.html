2.4 | Reading Discussion: The Four Basic Principles

Name

Questions below are based on Robin Williams, The Non-Designer's Design Book, 3rd edition, Chapter 1 ("The Joshua Tree Epiphany"). Read the assigned pages before answering.

  1. What four design principles does this chapter introduce?
  2. What gift did the author receive that led to the Joshua Tree story?
  3. The chapter gives a short one-line description for each of the four principles. Pick two and explain, in your own words, how they're different from each other.
  4. The chapter says the four principles are "really interconnected" and you'll rarely apply just one. Based on the one-line descriptions given, pick two principles and explain a way they might work together on the same page.
  5. The author's three-step process is: learn the principles, recognize when you're not using them, then apply them. Why might step 2—naming the problem —be harder than step 1?
