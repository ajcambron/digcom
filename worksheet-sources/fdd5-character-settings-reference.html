Setting What it controls Watch out for
KerningSpace between one specific pair of letters.Fix awkward gaps (like "AV" or "To") one pair at a time.
TrackingSpace evenly across a whole selection.Great for headlines; don't overdo it on body text.
LeadingVertical space between lines, baseline to baseline.Too tight = hard to read; too loose = lines feel disconnected.
Horiz./Vert. ScaleStretches or squishes letterforms.Avoid this — distorts the type designer's original shapes. Use a different weight instead.
Baseline ShiftMoves characters up/down without resizing.Used for superscript/subscript effects.
