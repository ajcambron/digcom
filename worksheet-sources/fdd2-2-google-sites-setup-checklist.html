FDD 2.2 | Google Sites Portfolio Setup Checklist

Name
Check each box as you complete it. This site is yours for the rest of the year, every summative project gets published here.
  1. Create the site. Go to sites.google.com, sign in with your school account, and click Blank to start a new site.
  2. Name your site. Click the untitled text at the top left and enter a name. Use your real name so your teacher can find it, not a nickname.
  3. Add your logo slot. Click the Themes tab in the right sidebar, then find where a header logo image can be uploaded. Leave it blank or upload a placeholder for now, you'll add your real one after S2.
  4. Add your favicon slot. In the same Themes area, find the favicon image setting. Leave it blank or upload a placeholder for now.
  5. Create your About Me page. Add a new page, title it "About Me," and write 5–7 sentences introducing yourself as a designer.
  6. Choose a theme. Pick a starting color/font theme from the Themes tab. You can refine this later, don't overthink it today.
  7. Publish. Click the purple Publish button, top right, then confirm.
  8. Copy your link. Click the chain-link icon (Copy Published Site Link), not the address bar, and paste it below.
