Rulers, Guides, Grids & Views

  1. Rulers ( + R) run along the top and left of the document window; drag from the corner where they meet to reset the (the document's 0,0 point) anywhere you like.
  2. Guides are non-printing reference lines you drag out from a ruler. Turn on Smart Guides and Illustrator will pop up its own temporary alignment hints as you drag objects near each other.
  3. A Grid (View > Show Grid) tiles the whole artboard with evenly spaced lines; turn on Snap to Grid to have objects jump to the nearest line.
  4. View modes change how your artwork is displayed, not how it's actually built:
    • — shows only the wireframe paths, no fills or strokes. Useful for finding an object hidden behind another.
    • — previews how your vector art will look once rasterized to the pixel grid (handy for screen/web work).
    • — a fullscreen, no-interface slideshow of your artboards, in order — great for presenting to a client.
Discuss: Your artboard has three overlapping shapes and you can't click the one on the bottom. Which view mode could help you find and select it, and why?
