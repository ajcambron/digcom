  • ✐
    Calligraphic Brush Mimics an angled pen nib — the stroke gets thicker or thinner depending on the direction you draw.
  • ✨
    Scatter Brush Scatters copies of an object randomly along a path — good for leaves, stars, or texture.
  • →
    Art Brush Stretches a single piece of art along the entire length of a path — good for ribbons, rope, or a single continuous line texture.
  • 🖌
    Bristle Brush Simulates a real paintbrush's bristles, including pressure and angle — the most painterly of the five brush types.
  • ▦
    Pattern Brush Repeats a set of tiles along a path, with separate artwork for straight sides and corners — good for borders and frames.
  • 🔄
    Generate Pattern Object > Pattern > Make. Live-tiles your artwork as you adjust tile type, spacing, and overlap, before saving it as a swatch.
A pattern's tile is its repeating unit — watch for the dashed bounding box in Generate Pattern mode to see exactly where one repeat ends and the next begins.
