Bauhaus teacher Johannes Itten taught that the three basic geometric forms each carry a different felt character. Before you draw, act each one out: trace a square in the air (corner to corner, tense), a triangle (sharp, all angles), a circle (one continuous curve). Which one matches the site you're building?
Square
tense, corner-to-corner movement
90° angles · red
Triangle
sharp, full variety of angles
45° angles · yellow
Circle
evenly curved, continuously moving
135°/curve · blue
Kandinsky paired each angle with a color by temperature: acute (45°) = yellow (sharp, active), right (90°) = red (objective, direct), obtuse (135°, heading toward a curve) = blue (passive, cold). Use this as a starting rule for your own mark, you can break it, but only with a reason.
