anchor point  •  composition  •  ease in / ease out  •  keyframe  •  layer  •  opacity
  • 🎥
    Composition After Effects' workspace. Layers stacked and animated over a timeline.
  • 🔑
    Keyframe Locks a property's value at a point in time. Two or more, and AE animates between them.
  • 🔄
    Ease In / Ease Out Gradually speeds up or slows motion instead of abrupt starts/stops.
  • 📌
    Anchor point The fixed point a layer rotates/scales around.
Nothing in the real world starts or stops instantly. Linear keyframes ignore that; eased ones don't.
