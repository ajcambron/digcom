continuity editing  •  cutting on action  •  editorial judgment  •  invisible edit  •  rhythm  •  tension
  • 🧠
    Editorial judgment Every decision not dictated by the footage itself: which take, how long to hold, when to cut away.
  • 🔄
    Continuity editing Time, space, and action feel seamless despite being shot out of order.
  • 👁️
    Invisible edit A cut the audience doesn't consciously notice.
  • 🎬
    Cutting on action Cut mid-movement so the motion itself hides the edit.
A good edit isn't the one people compliment. It's the one nobody notices, because it never pulled them out of the story.
