FDD 8.3 · Digital Communications
Name
Date
  • 🖼
    Trace Preset A built-in starting point (Black and White Logo, High Fidelity Photo, 3 Colors, Line Art) that sets sensible defaults for your kind of source image.
  • □
    Ignore White Treats white areas of the source as transparent instead of tracing them as opaque shapes — useful for a logo on a colored background.
  • ◒
    Threshold In Black and White mode, the brightness value (0–255) deciding whether a pixel becomes black or white. Raise it to convert more midtones to black.
  • ✎
    Paths / Corners / Noise Fine-tuning sliders: Paths controls how tightly the trace follows the original edge, Corners how sharp turns stay, Noise how small a speck is ignored.
  • 🔁
    Expand Converts the live trace into real, editable vector paths. Before expanding, it's just a preview linked to the placed raster image.
A live trace isn't finished artwork — you can't select or edit its individual paths until you click Expand.
