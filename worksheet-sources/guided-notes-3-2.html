FDD 3.2 | Shape Tools: Reference & Notes

Name
  • ▣
    Rectangle / Rounded Rectangle Click-drag to draw; click once to type exact width/height. Drag a Live Shape's corner widget to round its corners without changing the underlying path.
  • ◯
    Ellipse Hold Shift for a perfect circle; hold Option/Alt to draw outward from the center point instead of a corner.
  • ⬢
    Polygon Click-drag, then tap the Up/Down arrow keys while still dragging to add or remove sides.
  • ★
    Star Same arrow-key trick adds/removes points; hold Option/Alt while dragging to keep the star's arms straight instead of curving inward.
  • ╱
    Line Segment Hold Shift to constrain to horizontal, vertical, or 45° angles.
  1. A Live Shape remembers that it's a rectangle/ellipse/polygon and stays editable (corner radius, exact dimensions) — until you tell Illustrator to expand it into a plain path.
  2. Every shape has a box — the invisible rectangle that marks its full width and height, used when you scale or align it.
Try it: build a simple face or icon using only these five tools — no freehand drawing.
