180-degree rule  •  cutaway  •  J cut  •  jump cut  •  L cut  •  match cut  •  montage
TechniqueWhat it does
L cutAudio from the outgoing shot continues over the new video
J cutAudio from the upcoming shot starts before its video does
Match cutTwo shots linked by similar shape, motion, or action
CutawayBrief shot away from the main action, hides a rough edit point
Jump cutSame subject, same framing, cut together. Usually a mistake
An L/J cut only "works" if audio and video are genuinely offset on the timeline. If they still start/stop at the same frame, you haven't made one yet.
