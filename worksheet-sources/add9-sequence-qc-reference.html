audio levels  •  black frame  •  gap  •  safe margins
Run the pass in order, every time: gaps, then audio, then safe margins, then a black-frame scan.
  1. Gaps: scrub the whole timeline for accidental black/silent stretches. Found?
  2. Audio levels: check for clipping and inaudibly quiet sections. Found?
  3. Safe margins: is any text or action too close to the frame edge? Found?
  4. Black frame: check the head and tail of the sequence. Found?
