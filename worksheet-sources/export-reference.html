    For Screen Format: PNG, JPG, or SVG. Color mode: RGB. Resolution: 72–150 ppi is typical; SVG scales infinitely since it stays vector.
  • 🖶
    For Print Format: PDF or EPS. Color mode: CMYK. Resolution: at least 300 ppi for any raster content.
  • 📂
    Asset Export Panel Window > Asset Export. Collect objects or artboards, then batch-export them all at multiple scales/formats in one click.
  • 🎬
    Use Artboards Export option that saves each artboard as its own file, named automatically — the standard way to export a multi-artboard document.
Wrong-export warning signs: RGB colors look dull when printed; low-resolution raster art looks blurry at full size; a screen graphic that needed transparency exports with a solid white box instead.
