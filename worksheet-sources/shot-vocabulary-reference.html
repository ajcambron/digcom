Name
Date
ECU — Extreme Close-UpIsolates one detail (an eye, a hand) for maximum emphasis.
CU — Close-UpFills the frame with a face (chin to forehead) or small object.
MCU — Medium Close-UpChest up — the standard framing for most interviews.
MS — Medium ShotWaist up — shows gesture and body language, face still clear.
WS — Wide ShotFull body, plus a good amount of the surrounding environment.
LS — Long ShotSubject small in frame, emphasizing their place in the environment.
ELS — Extreme Long ShotEstablishes a location — any subject is tiny within it.
OTS — Over-the-ShoulderForeground shoulder/head, looking toward another subject beyond.
POV — Point of ViewShows exactly what a character sees, from their own eye line.
CutawayA brief shot of something else — adds context or hides an edit.
