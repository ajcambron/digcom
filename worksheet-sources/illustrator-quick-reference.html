Four tools to recreate your paper comp digitally — that's it for today.

  • ▣
    Shape Tools (Rectangle, Ellipse) Build up any geometric forms in your design.
  • ╱
    Line Segment Tool Draws straight lines. Hold Shift while dragging to keep them perfectly horizontal, vertical, or at a 45° angle.
  • 🎨
    Fill Color (Swatches Panel) Click a shape, then pick a color from the Swatches panel to color it in.
  • ↖
    Selection Tool Move, resize, and layer your shapes on top of each other.
Not covered today: the Pen tool, gradients, and effects — those come in later units. Just these four for now.
