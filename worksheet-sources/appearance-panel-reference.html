Appearance Panel Reference

FDD 7.2 · Digital Communications
Name
Date
  • 📜
    Appearance Panel Window > Appearance. Lists every fill, stroke, and effect on the selected object, in order. Click any item to edit it.
  • ➕
    Add New Fill / Add New Stroke Buttons at the bottom of the panel. Stack as many fills or strokes as you want on one object — each is independently editable.
  • ↕
    Reorder Drag an item up or down in the stack. Items higher in the list render in front of items below them, on that one object.
  • ⧉
    Duplicate Item Copies a selected fill or stroke so you can adjust the copy independently — useful for a subtle second tint or an offset second stroke.
  • 💧
    Eyedropper Tool Click one object with it, then click another — copies the entire appearance (every fill, stroke, and effect) in one step.
  • 🎨
    Recolor Artwork Edit > Recolor Artwork. Shows every color in a selection as one editable group — remap or shift the whole palette at once.
Remember: the Appearance panel always shows attributes for the currently selected object. Deselect, and it goes blank until you select something again.
