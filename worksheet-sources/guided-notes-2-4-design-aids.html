Name
Ctrl/Cmd  •  Outline  •  Pixel Preview  •  Presentation Mode  •  ruler origin
  1. Rulers ( + R) run along the top and left of the document window. Drag from the corner where they meet to move the (the 0,0 point): on the drawing below, the Header Logo's top-left corner.
  2. Guides are non-printing lines you drag out of a ruler. Smart Guides (⌘U) pop up temporary alignment hints as you drag objects near each other.
  3. The Grid (View › Show Grid) tiles the window with evenly spaced lines; its spacing is set in Preferences › Guides & Grid. Snap to Grid makes objects jump to the nearest line.
  4. View modes change how artwork is displayed, not how it's built: shows only wireframe paths (find a hidden object); previews the vector art on a screen's pixel grid; is a fullscreen, no-interface slideshow of your artboards for a client.
