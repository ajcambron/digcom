Do Part 1 every time before a GMetrix Illustrator exam. GMetrix's exam panel only loads when Illustrator is running in Rosetta. (Don't use Finder's Get Info → "Open using Rosetta." It doesn't work on our Macs.)
Part 1 · Open Illustrator in Rosetta
  1. Open the Creative Cloud app (Applications folder, or the Creative Cloud icon in the menu bar → Open Creative Cloud).
  2. Click the Apps tab, then Installed apps.
  3. Find Illustrator and click ••• (More actions) next to its Open button.
  4. Choose Open in Rosetta. Wait until Illustrator's Home screen appears, then leave Illustrator open.
Part 2 · Log in to GMetrix SMS
  1. Open GMetrix SMS (Applications folder → GmetrixSMSe).
  2. Log in with your GMetrix username and password. No account yet? Create one at gmetrix.net with your school email, then enter the class access code from Schoology.
Part 3 · Launch the Illustrator practice exam
  1. In your list of tests, find the Adobe Certified Professional: Illustrator practice exam (2025 exam version) and select it.
  2. Choose Testing Mode (unless your teacher says otherwise), then start the exam.
  3. Wait for the GMetrix panel to appear in Illustrator with your first task. Don't click anything while it loads.
  4. Work the whole exam independently, then submit it and screenshot your score. Save it to your Screenshots folder.
Troubleshooting
ProblemTry this
No Open in Rosetta in the ••• menu Quit and reopen the Creative Cloud app. Still missing? Tell your teacher.
Exam won't start, or the GMetrix panel never shows up in Illustrator Quit Illustrator completely (⌘Q), redo Part 1, then start the exam again.
GMetrix SMS says it can't find Illustrator Make sure Illustrator is open from Part 1, then tell your teacher.
Can't log in Check caps lock and spelling. Use your GMetrix login, not your school Google login.
Exam looks frozen at the start Wait a full minute before clicking anything; files load in the background.
