Part 1 · Log in to GMetrix SMS
  1. Open GMetrix SMS (Applications folder → GmetrixSMSe).
  2. Click the Login with Google button and sign in with your school Google account. Don't create a new account.
Part 2 · Enter our class code (first time only)
  1. Find the code on Schoology: open our class's Schoology homepage and scroll to How to Contact Me. The code is on the Gmetrix: line (it starts with Cambron-).
  2. In GMetrix SMS, click Redeem Code in the left sidebar, type the code exactly (including the dash), and click Redeem Code.
Part 3 · Launch the Illustrator practice exam
  1. In your list of tests, find the Adobe Certified Professional: Illustrator practice exam (2025 exam version) and select it.
  2. Choose Testing Mode and start the exam. GMetrix opens Illustrator for you.
  3. Wait for the GMetrix panel to appear in Illustrator with your first task. Don't click anything while it loads. If it doesn't open properly, use the Troubleshooting steps below.
  4. Work the whole exam independently, then submit it. On the results screen, take a screenshot: press ⌘ Command + Shift + 3 to capture the whole screen. The screenshot saves to your Desktop; move it to your Screenshots folder.
Troubleshooting: the exam doesn't open properly
If the GMetrix panel never shows up or the exam won't start, open Illustrator in Rosetta yourself, then try again:
  1. Quit Illustrator completely (⌘ Command + Q).
  2. Open the Creative Cloud app (Applications folder, or the Creative Cloud icon in the menu bar → Open Creative Cloud).
  3. Click the Apps tab, then Installed apps.
  4. Find Illustrator, click ••• (More actions) next to its Open button, and choose Open in Rosetta.
  5. Wait until Illustrator's Home screen appears and leave it open. Then go back to GMetrix SMS and start the exam again (step 6).
