  1. Open Schoology and find today's GMetrix/BrainBuffet classroom join code.
  2. Create an account on the GMetrix/BrainBuffet site using your school email address.
  3. Enter the classroom code to join the class. Codes are case-sensitive.
  4. Find the ACP Pre-Test (sometimes labeled "Pre-Assessment") and start it.
  5. Work quietly and independently. Guessing is expected; this isn't graded on correctness.
  6. Screenshot your results screen once you submit, and save it to your Screenshots folder.
A low score today is the whole point of a pre-test. Module 1 exists to teach you everything this test just asked about.
