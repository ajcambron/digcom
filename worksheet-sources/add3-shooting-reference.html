Before every take, check: tripod stable · eye-level · white balance · focus.
  • 📽
    Eye-level Camera height matches the subject's eyes, not above or below.
  • 🎥
    Medium close-up Subject fills most of the frame.
  • 📈
    Headroom Just enough space above the head. Not too much, not too little.
  • ⚖️
    White balance Set before rolling, not left on auto by default.
